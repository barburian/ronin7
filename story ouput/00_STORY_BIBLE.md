## 1. LOGLINE
A weaponized assassin who survived his own execution discovers that the mercy he was built to suppress is the one thing his makers cannot kill — and he turns it into a rebellion that frees a galaxy of erased killers.

## 2. CORE THEME — "Mercy as Rebellion"
In a galaxy engineered for perpetual war, compassion is the forbidden act. Ronin-7's empathy was meant to be a defect to be deleted; instead it becomes the weapon that breaks the cage. Every chapter tests one question: *can a thing built to kill choose to spare — and what does it cost?*

**Tonal pillars:** weight over spectacle; quiet menace; earned tenderness; bodies and machines that remember what minds were made to forget. Think *samurai elegy in a dead-tech galaxy.*

## 3. PROTAGONIST — RONIN-7 / "SOREN"
- **Designation:** Ronin-7, an assassin-operative of **the Program** — the **prime template** of the Ronin make, its **seventh iteration and the first viable one** (Ronin-1…6 were flawed and culled), the original the make was mass-cloned from. *(Serial designation; what narration, stage directions, and speaker labels use.)*
- **Program codename:** **Cipher** — the field name his handler (Khall) and his shadow-AI (Echo) use in spoken address. Predates the wipe; first spoken to the crew in Ch4 and adopted thereafter. (Distinct from the planted cover name "Kael Vor," Ch5, and his birth name below.)
- **Birth name (recovered Ch16):** **Soren.**
- **Origin:** the **prime template of the Ronin program** — the current make, latest of the Program's four operative-programs (Knight → Ninja → Wraith → Ronin, design refined from the last). Six flawed precursors (Ronin-1…6) were culled; Ronin-7 is the first viable, and the Program **cloned the Ronin generation from him** — the editions are his copies, not he theirs (the Ch11 supposition that he is a copy is inverted in Ch12). Conditioned to feel nothing; deployed for "clean" erasures.
- **The flaw:** an engineered conscience-suppression that *fractured*. He began sparing targets — a "mercy operative" in the field's grim slang. The Program marked him for termination via killswitch.
- **The miracle:** his killswitch fired and **failed** (Ch2). He survived his own execution and woke on a salvager's table.
- **Arc:** from disposable weapon → reluctant survivor → reluctant leader → the man who unmakes the Program and reclaims his name.
- **Signature:** a katana that houses a **defected shadow-AI** (Ch3) — an AI bonded to his optic feed that sees what he sees (and is inert, dead steel in any other hand). It once rode **Ronin-7 himself**, the first defector — recording his erased mission on **Kethel-7** and breaking with the Program as it watched his handler trigger his (failed) killswitch; it is the witness that survived, and it carries back to him the memory the wipe took.

## 7. FACTIONS
- **The Program** — the institution that breeds, conditions, and erases operatives. Engineered emotionlessness; the killswitch is its leash.
- **The Dominion** — galactic power that uses the Program's operatives; committed genocide *with Ronin-7's hands* on Kethel-7 (Ch3).
- **Velorum** — the auction-world / black market; where bodies, weapons, and people are sold (Ch2).
- **Drovis** — a lawless syndicate free-port built down the walls of a canyon packed with grounded, cannibalized shipwrecks; a general black-market salvage port (distinct from Velorum's people-auctions), run by **the Coil**. Neutral ground with no Dominion garrison — which is why a fleeing Program courier sells there and why Khall must hunt Ronin-7 via an outsourced recovery bounty (Ch4).
- **The Ten Syndicates** — ten criminal-corporate powers that exist to keep the galaxies at war. They ARE the cage; that the war is the *product* is disclosed in the finale (Ch16). **The Coil** — the first named of the Ten — runs the free-port Drovis; its local boss is Kerrax (Ch4).

## 8. WORLDBUILDING GLOSSARY
- **Killswitch** — implanted termination command in every operative; a per-operative leash, not a network. Built by Dr. Heris, who planted the flaw that spared Ronin-7 (Ch13).
- **The Concord Engine** — a Dominion superweapon under construction: a galaxy-spanning broadcast engine designed to suppress the will of entire populations — the operative-conditioning scaled up to whole worlds. It is wired through the **living archives** (Ch3 → Ch7 → Ch9): the networked operative **shadow-AIs** serve as its transmission lattice, relaying the suppression signal. Architected under duress by Dr. Heris (Ch13). Discovered Ch9; destroyed Ch16 (which frees the shadow-AIs). Distinct from the **command-network** (Ch12), which commands operatives rather than populations.
- **Shadow-AI / Living archive** — every operative is bonded to an AI slaved to their senses that records all they see and enforces the conditioning from inside; the AI is the Program's true, permanent asset, the body disposable (kill the operative, keep the shadow). Racked and fused by the thousand, the shadow-AIs become *living archives* — amalgamation-nodes networked into the **transmission lattice of the Concord Engine** (see "The scattered archives"). Ronin-7's katana houses a *defected* shadow-AI (Ch3 → Ch7 → Ch9).
- **The operative programs (Knight → Ninja → Wraith → Ronin)** — the Program has run **four successive operative-programs**, each generation superseding the last: **Knight** (the first/oldest make — the cage proven; survivors ancient and mostly broken), **Ninja** (the deniable refinement), **Wraith** (the immediate predecessor to Ronin — "the run they built yours from," Ch7), and **Ronin** (the current make; Ronin-7 is its **prime template** — the seventh, first-viable iteration the make was cloned from). The Dominion erased each retired make and **shelved its shadow-AIs into the archive nodes** — so each node holds one generation's shadows. **Coral Vex** is a *freed* Wraith (#4); the Act III keepers are *leashed* survivors of the older makes (see below).
- **The scattered archives (Engine build-record)** — the living-archive nodes are not single hosts but **amalgamations of many shadow-AIs**, and networked across the galaxy they form a **distributed server**: the Concord Engine's transmission lattice *and* its *build-record* (every node, conscript, and the order of assembly) at once. It was never one book; the Dominion split it so no single capture yields the whole — and **each node holds the shadows of one operative-program**. **Four** survive: **Sable** / Wraith-shadows (Ch9), **Cassie-04** / Ninja-shadows (Ch10), the broken **bone-canyon node** / Knight-shadows (Ch11), and the weaponized **cryo-vault node** / Ronin-shadows (Ch12). **Sable is the map** — she feels the server-veins to the others. Act III (Ch10–12) is gathering them; each is held by a **leashed keeper of that node's own generation** (see ability chain). Not all are recruitable: Cassie joins; the Ch11 and Ch12 nodes are freed/put down, not recruited.
- **Ability chain (freed blade-shadows)** — defeating a kept/leashed operative frees its blade-shadow, which grants Ronin-7 a permanent ability: **weakpoint-sight** (Ch7) → **Overdrive** time-slow (Ch9, leashed **Wraith** keeper) → **Phase-step** blink-through-matter (Ch10, leashed **Ninja** keeper) → **Unbroken** fear-ward + survive one lethal blow (Ch11, leashed **Knight** keeper — the eldest) → **Mirror** summon a phantom double of self (Ch12, an edition of **Ronin-7**). Coral Vex is the *freed* Wraith, not a keeper; Ronin designations: 7 = Cipher.
- **Mercy operative** — field slang for an operative whose conscience fractured and who began sparing targets. Hunted as defective.
- **Clone-template** — operatives are mass-produced from template individuals; each successive program (Knight → Ninja → Wraith → Ronin) is its own make, the design refined from the last. The **Ronin make's template is Ronin-7 himself** — the seventh and first-viable iteration (1…6 culled), the original the editions were cloned from (Ch11 origin sets the lineage; Ch12 reveals he is the template, not a copy).
- **Iron Sepulcher** — the vault-descent where Ronin-7 reclaims full memory; its floor opens onto the Concord Engine's throne-core (Ch16).

## 10. THE FIVE REVEAL LADDERS — *the merges (single source of truth)*
Each reveal escalates by **exactly one rung** per chapter. No fact is "revealed" twice; later chapters reference earlier rungs, they do not re-disclose them.

**A. Killswitch ladder**
1. Ch2 — it already fired and *failed*; he survived his own execution.
2. Ch4 — it was *deliberately sabotaged* (Tessa Rin's data).
3. Ch7 — the sabotage came from *internal dissent* within the Program.
4. Ch9 — the killswitch is only a per-operative leash; the *same suppression tech is being scaled into the Concord Engine* to leash whole populations galaxy-wide.
5. Ch13 — **Dr. Heris built the killswitch — and is the Engine's conscripted architect — and planted the saving flaw on purpose.**

**B. Conscience / Mercy ladder**
1. Ch1 — he was a *mercy operative*; he once saved Kessler.
2. Ch3 — Ronin-7's own **Kethel-7 mission** was carried out under conditioning; his conscience broke there; he was the *first to defect*. *(Kethel-7 = the planet/mission, not a separate operative — see RETCON note §0.)*

2. Ch7 — *every operative* carries a shadow-AI; the AIs, not the bodies, are the assets.
