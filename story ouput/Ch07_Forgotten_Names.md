# CHAPTER 7 — FORGOTTEN NAMES
**Act II · Beneath the Erasure · Episodes EP13–EP14**
**Logline:** A salvager-archivist called Coral Vex turns out to be an operative of the **Wraith line** — the variant that came before his own, who cut her own leash and survived erasure — and through her, Ronin-7 learns the erased aren't simply gone: every operative's shadow-AI is kept alive inside its blade and racked, conscious in the dark like the katana writ large, and his switch was sabotaged by dissent inside the Program itself.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — Morrigan's bench / command room)*
- With Morrigan newly aboard and her analysis bench coming online in the Hub's engineering deck, the crew runs the dojo's intercepts and Ronin-7's sabotaged switch through her eyes. A name keeps surfacing in the operative-record black market: a salvager-archivist called **Coral Vex**, who hoards the paper of dead operatives like relics.
- Coral might know who keeps the records of the erased — and, Morrigan suspects, why Ronin-7's leash was cut. The crew sets a heading for her derelict reliquary.
- **Interaction:** Morrigan, blunt and technical, doesn't soften her reads, which lands sideways with a crew still taking her measure; Mera flags that an archivist who knows operatives that well is either an ally or a hunter; Resh knows the reliquary's underworld. Echo, for Cipher alone, is uneasy about anyone who collects shadows.
- **Beat:** they set course for the reliquary, chasing records and the hand inside the Program that broke his leash. *(Hands into reaching the archivist.)*

### Scene 2 — The Archivist *(derelict data-reliquary)*
- Chasing "the others," the crew finds **Coral Vex**, who hoards operative records like relics. She knows too much, recognizes Ronin-7 too fast.
- **Beat:** she's not a collector of the dead. She's one of them, hiding in plain sight.

### Scene 3 — Coral Vex Is What Came Before Him *(reliquary core)* — REVEAL + ALLY #4
- Coral reveals she is of the **Wraith line** — the predecessor variant the Ronin line was refined from, presumed scrapped, who survived by hollowing out a false identity and cutting her own leash. **Ally #4.**
- **Beat:** he has a *forebear*. A design survived before his line ever existed — and she's been watching the Program from the shadows.

### Scene 4 — The Kept Shadows *(reliquary archive dive)* — REVEAL + MINI-BOSS
- Coral shows him the truth she's been guarding: erased operatives aren't deleted — their **shadow-AIs are seated in their blades and racked**, every witness shelved and still awake in its steel, the katana writ large and made literal. **(Ladder E, rung 2.)**
- **Beat:** the sword was a sample. The Program keeps the *blade* of every operative it ever "killed," and the shadow still living in it.
- **Mini-boss / ability:** the **oldest blade** in the hall has gone berserk from decades awake with nothing to witness. Coral can't kill it clean from the outside; the only thing that reaches a starving shadow is a compatible host-feed, and only another operative can survive offering one. Ronin grips it, lets it bond, and is thrown into a **mindspace duel against the blade's dead previous owner** (his combat imprint still defending the steel). On victory the blade calms; its pacified shadow-AI imparts its combat-read to Echo, granting Ronin permanent **weakpoint-sight**. The freed blade is then set to rest; Ronin keeps only his own katana.

### Scene 5 — Sabotage Was Dissent *(intercepted internal traffic)* — REVEAL
- Coral cut her own leash and knows the work from the inside; running Ronin-7's switch against that expertise — with Morrigan's analysis — she reads what she never could on her own: *another hand* on his leash. They confirm the next rung: Ronin-7's killswitch sabotage came from **internal dissent** — a faction *inside* the Program deliberately breaking leashes. **(Ladder A, rung 3.)**
- **Beat:** the Program is not monolithic. Someone inside is fighting it. (Identity of the saboteur lands Ch13: Dr. Heris.)
- **Hook out:** the crew now hunts not just allies but the dissenters; Coral cannot point to where they scatter, but she names an older legend, the **Silent Garden**, a dead-signal world that is said to show one seeker one true thing, and sends Ronin-7 there to see the hand that cut his leash for himself.

I reached in and tore it out myself, and I kept my shadow instead of letting them shelve it. *(she studies his hands)* Yours was cut clean, and cut from a distance. That wasn't you. Someone on the inside opened your door for you. *One of them.*

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub briefing — run the leads through Morrigan; set course for Coral Vex's reliquary.* (1) Locate Coral Vex's reliquary. (2) Earn her trust (she tests whether you kill or spare cornered foes). (3) Reveal: Coral = Wraith line (the predecessor variant) → recruit. (4) Archive-dive → operatives-as-shadows-in-blades + internal-dissent confirmation; pacify the berserk blade → unlock weakpoint-sight.
- **Environment:** derelict data-reliquary (vaulted archive, racks of mounted blades with lit hilts — eerie, reverent).
- **Encounters:** archive-defense automata; rival scavengers; **mini-boss:** the berserk blade's dead **previous owner**, fought in a mindspace duel after Ronin connects to the oldest kept blade (a kept witness gone feral).
- **Ally unlock:** **Coral Vex / Wraith line (#4)** — adds Program-insider knowledge and access to the dissenter network.
- **Ability unlock:** **weakpoint-sight** — the pacified blade's shadow-AI imparts its combat-read to Echo, who overlays enemy weak points / armor gaps on Ronin's optic feed (permanent focus mode, carried for the rest of the game).
- **Gameplay hooks:** blade-"rescue" side-objectives (free kept shadows from coherent blades, no duel required — seeds Ch16 payoff); dissenter-faction contacts open the route to Ch8.
