# CHAPTER 11 — GHOSTS AND ORIGINS `[CAVE]`
**Act III · Predators Become Protectors · Episodes EP21–EP22**
**Logline:** In a canyon carved from the bones of a dead leviathan, the second scattered archive lies broken — the oldest node of all, packed with the shadows of the **Knight** generation, the Program's first make. Its leaking shadows flood the deep with a narcosis dreamscape that walks Ronin-7 through his own ghosts. Putting down its leashed keeper — the eldest operative, the first of the first program — frees the mind-ward that lets Ronin-7 walk the dream lucidly to the dreamer at its heart; only putting *her* down clean ends the broadcast and brings the comm back. The node's record reveals the Program's true origin: a lineage of operative-makes, **Knight → Ninja → Wraith → Ronin**, each cloned and scattered, each refined from the last.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — war-room holo-table)*
- Sable's map and Cassie-04's cross-index agree on the next scattered archive: a node wrecked aboard an ancient **arkship** in a **leviathan bone-canyon**, older than the Dominion, that the records tie to the Program's very origin.
- But this node reads *wrong* on Sable's map. She names it before they go: a sister so long racked and so far gone that her kept shadows have begun leaking outward — she may not be one they can bring up alive. Cassie warns the canyon's "atmosphere" isn't atmosphere at all; it is the node's broadcast, and it shows you your dead. And like every node, it has a keeper.
- **Interaction:** Cassie-04's cartographer precision meets Vess's raw new-recruit grief — her people were taken by Cipher's old line, and she's aboard anyway; Sable sits apart, dreading what she's about to see herself become. Echo, for Cipher alone, is wary of a place that predates everything.
- **Beat:** they deploy into the bone-canyon toward the broken node and the Program's beginning. *(Hands into the canyon traverse.)*

### Scene 2 — The Bone-Canyon *(cave: leviathan skeleton)*
- Sable's map leads to a **leviathan bone-canyon** — a ravine threaded through the colossal skeleton of a long-dead beast, where an ancient **arkship** lies wrecked and half-fossilized, the broken node still strung into its hull. The shadows racked into this node are the **Knight** generation — the Program's first make, the oldest of the four, which is why it is the most overloaded and the most broken.
- **Beat:** this is older than the Dominion, older than the syndicates. The Program came from *here* — and this is where the first operatives, the Knights, were made.

### Scene 3 — The Narcosis Dreamscape *(cave depths, the node's broadcast)*
- The "atmosphere" is the broken node itself: a living archive so overloaded its kept shadows leak outward as a waking **dreamscape**. Ronin-7 walks through his ghosts: Kira (Ch5), the dead of Kethel-7 (Ch3), the faces from the ledger, and a recurring figure he can't place — *himself,* younger, before the Program. *(The younger-self thread runs to Soren, Ch16.)*
- **Guardian boss — Aldric / Knight-1:** the eldest operative there is — the very first of the very first program, the make every later make was refined from — posted here so long he is half-lost to the node's broadcast, fights Ronin-7 *through* the dream — a duel where the dead keep interrupting. He cannot be reached or freed; he has to be put down. (Not Ronin-7's mirror but his most distant ancestor; his technique is the oldest, heaviest, most archaic school the player faces.)
- **Beat:** killing Aldric frees his **blade-shadow**, granting Ronin-7 **Unbroken** — the mind-ward that lets him resist the narcosis and shrug off one otherwise-lethal blow (fourth permanent unlock). But killing the keeper does not end the dream, because the dreaming is the archive's own; the broadcast drifts on and the comm stays cut. The dream that kept its guardian cannot keep *him* — now narcosis-immune, he walks the still-active dream alone, Echo the only voice, toward the dreamer at its heart.

### Scene 4 — The Arkship Record *(arkship core)* — REVEAL
- With the keeper down and Ronin-7 now able to stand in the dream without drowning, the node's fragment of the build-record can finally be read — read by **Echo alone** off the optic feed, comm still cut, the crew not yet able to reach the heart. The oldest section of all: the origin. The Program began generations ago by taking a small set of **template operatives** and **scattering** their cloned descendants across the galaxy. And it did not stop at one make: it ran the line forward in generations — **Knight** first, then **Ninja**, then **Wraith**, then **Ronin** — each program refined from the one before, each cloned and scattered, each retired and shelved into a node when the next superseded it. The keepers Cipher has put down — Vane, Sever, Aldric — were the last leashed survivors of the three makes that came before his own. **(Sets up Ladder C, rung 2 — the clone truth, which lands as a personal gut-punch in Ch12, when the last node turns out to hold the Ronin make and wears his face — and inverts the assumption he carries out of here: not the newest copy, but the template they copied.)**
- **Beat:** the operatives aren't an army of individuals. They're echoes of a handful of people, multiplied across time and refined make by make — and the Ronin make is *his.* He takes it, here, as proof he is just the latest copy of a scattered template; whose original face he wears is the question he carries out (the answer waits, and inverts, in Ch12).

### Scene 5 — The Second Kill *(arkship, the broadcast ends, the climb home)*
- The reveal read, Ronin-7 turns to the dreamer who kept it. The sister inside the node is too far gone to bring up; he gives her the same door he gave the keeper and puts her down **clean.** On her death the broadcast ends, the dream collapses to cold real air, the node goes dark, and the **comm floods back** — the crew finally able to reach the heart.
- A brief grief: Sable feels her go; **Coral recognizes Wraith as her own make** off the recovered fragment (her relocated reveal-anchor). The origin reframes everyone Ronin-7 has fought and freed as family he never knew he had.
- **Cassie closes:** it is over with this one, come home — folding in the **hook out:** two nodes down, one to go — the last scattered archive is wired into a Dominion cryo-command vault, and a rival force is already racing for it (Ch12). And if the template was scattered, *whose* face is his? (Answered, brutally, in Ch12.)

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub briefing — Sable's map sets course for the broken bone-canyon node.* (1) Traverse the bone-canyon (`[CAVE]`, environmental platforming). (2) Survive the narcosis dreamscape — the node's broadcast — and defeat its keeper **Aldric / Knight-1** (boss) to **gain Unbroken** (the mind-ward that lets you walk the still-active dream). (3) Walk the still-active dream lucidly to the arkship core; read the node's fragment (origin record), delivered by Echo with comm down. (4) **Put down the dreaming archive herself** to end the broadcast and restore comm, then heed Cassie's homecoming order and climb out.
- **Environment:** `[CAVE]` — leviathan skeleton ravine + interior arkship; the **dreamscape** is a surreal, rules-bending sub-level.
- **Encounters:** ghost-manifestations (memory-enemies wearing faces of the fallen); **boss: Aldric / Knight-1**, the broken node's leashed keeper — the eldest make, the oldest/heaviest/most archaic fighting school the player faces — fought *through* the dreamscape (frees **Unbroken**).
- **Ally unlock:** none — the node is too broken to recruit; this is the **origins/lore** chapter and deepens the existing roster's bonds.
- **Gameplay hooks:** **Unbroken** unlock (status/fear resistance + survive one otherwise-lethal hit) from Aldric's freed blade-shadow; the dreamscape still seeds the personal "buried memory" thread (drives toward Soren, Ch16); two of three nodes now gathered; the scatter-map seeds the clone reveal and the Commander Vale plot (Ch12).
