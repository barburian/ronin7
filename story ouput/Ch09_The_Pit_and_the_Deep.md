# CHAPTER 9 — THE PIT AND THE DEEP `[CAVE]`
**Act III · Predators Become Protectors · Episodes EP17–EP18**
**Logline:** Descending through the Rustfang pirate-hold and the Tide depths, Ronin-7 recruits two more of the lost — and uncovers a buried Dominion worksite: the **Concord Engine**, a galaxy-wide weapon being built to silence the will of entire worlds, wired through the kept shadow-AIs of the erased.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — war-room holo-table)*
- Act III opens around the Hub's reignited **war-room holo-table** — the bridge is awake now, the roster large and loud. Following the dissenter trail and the Program's buried infrastructure, Morrigan pins a relay signal sunk deep underground beneath the **Rustfang**, a pirate free-hold dug into a dead world's caves and held by the old warrior **Gryph.**
- Whatever the Program is building, the trail goes down, not out. The crew plans a descent through the hold to reach the buried relay.
- **Interaction:** the home base is full for the first time — Morrigan itching to read the relay, Coral warning that the deep is where the Program hides the kept, Mera and Resh weighing Gryph and his band, Kessler running a ship that finally feels lived-in. Echo, for Cipher alone, notes how far down this one goes.
- **Beat:** they deploy into the Rustfang descent, hunting infrastructure and more of the lost. *(Hands into the hold.)*

### Scene 2 — The Rustfang Hold *(cave: pirate-hold descent)*
- The dissenter trail goes underground into the **Rustfang**, a pirate free-hold held by the old warrior **Gryph.** The crew has fortified the hold down onto a Program relay buried in the rock without ever knowing it was there.
- **Beat:** the deeper they go, the older the machinery — this is infrastructure, not a base.

### Scene 3 — Gryph's Bargain *(hold overlook)* — ALLY #5
- **Gryph (Ally #5)**, an old warrior who guards his deep, won't give the map to a stranger. When a Coil raid hits the hold, Ronin-7 and Gryph fight it off **side by side** instead of Ronin seizing the map off a corpse — predator becomes protector.
- **Beat:** Gryph, who has only ever watched outsiders come to *take* the hold, gives the map and his loyalty warrior to warrior. He names his second, **Rook**, to keep the cave, and throws in with the crew. (Act III's title made literal.)

### Scene 4 — The Tide Depths, Sable & Vane *(cave: flooded deep)* — ALLY #6 / BOSS
- Below the hold lie the **Tide depths**, a flooded archive-trench. There they find **Sable**, an operative who was made into a **living archive** — her body racked with kept shadow-AIs, a walking node of the lattice. The shadows fused into her are an older make than Ronin-7: the **Wraith** generation, the program right before his own. **Ally #6.** *(The racks of Ch7 walk and breathe down here.)*
- But Sable is not unguarded. The Dominion knows what she is worth — a live node of the Concord Engine's lattice — and has posted an operative to keep her. **Vane / Wraith-6**, a survivor of the **Wraith program** — the make right before Ronin, the run they refined into Ronin-7's line, the same make **Coral Vex** cut herself free of; but where Coral tore her leash out, Vane's still holds. He is the Dominion's hand, fully conditioned, deployed to *protect their asset.* Not Ronin-7's twin but his forebear — an older make of the same cage, still locked. *(The literal dark-mirror — a leashed copy of Ronin-7 himself — is held for the Ronin clone in Ch12.)*
- He cannot be reasoned with or subdued — the conditioning answers for him to the end. To free Sable, Ronin-7 has to put him down.
- **Beat — Overdrive:** Vane dies, and the leash dies with him. His **blade-shadow**, freed at last, floods into Echo, who spikes it through Ronin-7's optic feed — and the world stops. Ronin-7 moves through frozen water and frozen men, faster than the kept can fire. **(Second permanent ability unlock; killing him was the only mercy the Dominion left him.)**

### Scene 5 — The Concord Engine *(construction core, deepest point)* — REVEAL
- Reaching the buried relay, Morrigan and Sable map what it actually feeds: this is no killswitch hub. The killswitch is just a per-operative leash — but the same suppression science is being **scaled up.** The Dominion is building the **Concord Engine**: a galaxy-spanning broadcast weapon meant to do to whole populations what conditioning did to operatives — to switch off the will to refuse, world by world. **(Ladder A, rung 4.)**
- **Worse:** the Engine runs on the kept. The living archives — operatives like Sable, racked with shadow-AIs — are being conscripted as its **transmission lattice**, every kept shadow a relay forced to carry the silence outward. **(Ladder E, rung 3.)**
- **Beat:** the leash on his skull was only the prototype. They're forging one big enough for the galaxy — and stringing it through the kept shadows of everyone they ever erased.
- **Hook out:** the Engine's build-record was never one book — the Dominion split it across living archives like Sable, scattered across the galaxy. Sable is the map to them; the first is filed in the Ninefold mines (Ch10).

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub briefing at the war-room table — trace the buried relay; descend the Rustfang hold.* (1) Descend the Rustfang hold. (2) Fight the Coil raid side by side with Gryph → earn the map and his loyalty (protector choice). (3) Dive the Tide depths; kill **Vane / Wraith-6** to free **Sable** (recruit Ally #6) and gain **Overdrive**. (4) Reach the construction core → reveal the Concord Engine.
- **Environment:** `[CAVE]` — a vertical pirate-hold (dust, salvage-rigs, drop-shafts) fortified down onto sealed Program ruins, transitioning to flooded **Tide depths** (water traversal, pressure, bioluminescence).
- **Encounters:** Coil raiders (a co-op hold-defense fought alongside Gryph and his band, allied NPCs including his second **Rook**); **boss: Vane / Wraith-6** (a still-leashed, fully-conditioned survivor of the Wraith program — the make right before Ronin, Coral's make — posted to guard Sable; he can't be talked down or spared — the fight ends in his death, which frees his blade-shadow).
- **Ally unlock:** **Gryph (#5)** and **Sable (#6)**.
- **Ability unlock:** **Overdrive** — when Vane/Wraith-6 dies, his freed blade-shadow passes to Echo, who can now spike Ronin-7 into a hyper-reflex burst where the world appears frozen in time (second permanent ability after Ch7's weakpoint-sight).
- **Gameplay hooks:** underwater traversal kit; the **Concord Engine schematic** becomes a meta-objective (locate the Engine's scattered construction nodes); Sable's living-archive ability lets the player read kept shadow-AIs in the field; **Overdrive** as a chargeable combat state — enemies and projectiles crawl while Ronin-7 acts at full speed.
