# CHAPTER 5 — THE DEBT OF ASHES
**Act II · Beneath the Erasure**
**Logline:** A grieving survivor named Vera Dusk forces Ronin-7 to face the dead he made — including her sister — and the reckoning peels back the first layer of his stolen identity: the name "Kael Vor" was never his.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — command room)*
- Act II opens aboard the Hub. Cross-referencing the Velorum termination files with Mera's Dominion operation logs, the crew surfaces a hard lead: a settlement on an **ash-world** that Ronin-7's old operative line scoured. The trail to *who he was* runs straight through *what he did.*
- Mera, newest aboard and still half a stranger, reads the old kill-records fluently — and warns them what they'll find. Iris flags a name in the operation roster she can't yet resolve: the operative-of-record, "Kael Vor."
- **Interaction:** the first mission the crew chooses with open eyes that it will *cost* them. Mera's professional flatness rubs against Kessler's protective unease; Resh asks the question nobody wants to (do they really go dig up his graves?); Mira is kept close. Echo, for Cipher alone, says the quiet part: this one isn't a rescue, it's a reckoning.
- **Beat:** they set course for the ash-world knowing the only thing waiting there is the bill. *(Hands into the accusation at the mass grave.)*

### Scene 2 — The Accusation *(ash-world settlement, mass grave)*
- The crew reaches a settlement Ronin-7's old line scoured. **Vera Dusk** confronts him with a ledger of the dead — among them her sister, **Kira.**
- Ronin-7 cannot deny it; the katana's stored memories confirm he was there.
- **Beat:** mercy can't undo the dead. This is the chapter where the cost is named, not paid down.

### Scene 3 — Walking the Dead *(memory-dive into the massacre)*
- To give Vera the truth, Ronin-7 dives the katana's memory of the operation. He witnesses his own hands at work under conditioning — and the moment, late, where he hesitated.
- **Beat:** the hesitation came too late to save Kira. The mercy that defines him now was *born* in this failure.

### Scene 4 — "Kael Vor" Exposed *(operation records cross-check)* — REVEAL
- Working the records from aboard The Cairn while the landing party holds the grave, Iris and Mera cross-reference the massacre's command records against his own file and relay the contradiction down over comm: the operative-of-record was logged under the name **"Kael Vor"** — a planted identity. It was never his real name; the Program issues false names to bury accountability. **(Ladder C, rung 1.)**
- **Beat:** he doesn't just not remember his name — the name he half-believed was his is a *fabrication.* (True name lands Ch16.)

### Scene 5 — The Reckoning *(graveside)*
- Vera demands his life. He offers it. She doesn't take it — because killing him won't raise Kira, and because she sees the thing the Program feared: a weapon that grieves.
- Vera doesn't join the crew, but she releases him to a harder sentence — *live, and make it mean something.*
- **Hook out:** if his name was faked, his whole history is a forgery to be excavated.

## KEY SCENES (screenplay)

**INT. THE CAIRN — COMMAND ROOM**

> MERA lays an old Dominion operation-log beside IRIS's decrypt. KESSLER watches CIPHER, who is very still.
>
> **MERA**
> I've read a hundred of these. Pacification logs. This one carries your old line's signature. A settlement, scoured. I confirmed kills off records like this for nine years. I know what we'll find.
>
> **IRIS**
> The operative-of-record is named Kael Vor. I can't make the name resolve to anything. It's a wall.
>
> **RESH**
> So we're flying to a graveyard to read your name off the kill-record that put them there. You sure you want this one?
>
> **CIPHER**
> No. *(beat)* Set the course anyway.
>
> **ECHO (V.O.)** *(to Cipher alone)*
> This isn't a place you save, Cipher. It's a place you answer for. I'll be with you in it.

**EXT. ASH-WORLD — MASS GRAVE**

> VERA DUSK holds a worn ID-chit: KIRA.
>
> **VERA**
> You logged this as "pacification." She was fourteen.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> I won't tell you I don't remember. The sword remembers for me.
>
> **VERA**
> Then say her name.
>
> **RONIN-7** *(without hesitation)*
> Kira Dusk. *(beat)* I was there. I was late. Late is the same as never to her.
>
> *VERA's hand shakes on a drawn weapon. She doesn't fire.*
>
> **VERA**
> Dying's the easy ledger to clear. *(lowers it)* You owe the harder one now. Stay alive and pay it.

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub briefing — cross-reference the records, set the ash-world reckoning.* (1) Reach the ash-world; confront Vera. (2) Memory-dive the massacre (emotional set-piece; limited combat). (3) Cross-check records → expose "Kael Vor." (4) Resolve the reckoning (Vera lives; player cannot "win" by force).
- **Environment:** ash-world settlement & mass grave; memory-dive of the massacre.
- **Encounters:** mostly traversal/horror-tone; optional scavenger threats; **no roster boss** — the antagonist is grief.
- **Ally unlock:** none (Vera is a one-chapter foil who survives; she may recur as a contact).
- **Gameplay hooks:** introduces the **ledger-of-the-dead** motif (full mechanic at Ch10); the false-name reveal opens an "identity" investigation thread tracked in the Hub.
