# CHAPTER 1 — THE SALVAGER'S DEBT
**Act I · Mercy as Rebellion · Episode EP01**
**Logline:** Three weeks after pulling a sealed casket out of open space, a salvager finally wakes the dead man inside — just as a Dominion patrol boards the ship and recognizes a face the records swear was already executed.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — The Wake *(the Hub — Kessler's salvage rig)*
- EP01 opens on a body on the revival table. For three weeks Kessler has nursed it back from a flatline; now, for the first time, the vitals climb toward something like consciousness.
- Ronin-7 boots up *lucid* — no name, fragmentary memory, lethal reflexes. Before he knows where he is, he has Kessler by the throat.
- Kessler talks him down. **Beat:** this is the wake the whole revival has been building toward — the first time the body is actually *present* instead of thrashing and going under again.
- Tutorial/Hub establishment: movement, the katana, the Hub as home base.

### Scene 2 — Three Weeks Adrift *(the Hub)*
- With Ronin-7 calmed, Kessler fills the gap. Three weeks ago he snagged a sealed casket drifting alone in the wreck-field — **Dominion seals, fresh, no wreck anywhere near it.** Someone threw away a sealed pod. Whatever was inside, the Dominion meant to be rid of it. *(Discovery-only: the drop is inferred from the casket, never shown.)*
- Inside: a man flatlined, throat-scarred, expensive military augments under cheap skin. Kessler's instinct said *burn it.* His debt said *open it.* He opened it.
- **Beat:** he's stirred before, in those three weeks — woke thrashing, stared straight through Kessler, said nothing a man could use. No name, no memory, nothing. Kessler couldn't get a single answer out of him. This morning is the first he's been *home* behind the eyes.
- Kessler reveals he *knows* this face. **Six years ago** a Program operative spared his life when the order said clean sweep. **(Ladder B, rung 1 — mercy operative; he saved Kessler.)**
- **Beat:** Ronin-7 doesn't remember doing it. The kindness is a stranger's, and it's his own.

### Scene 3 — The Boarding *(the Hub — corridors & medical bay)*
- A Dominion patrol intercepts the barge for a routine sweep and **comes aboard.** Kessler stalls them at the hatch, but there's no talking the search down — they push deeper into the ship, toward the medical bay where Ronin-7 is.
- They find him. A trooper's visor-scan snags on his face and *escalates* — an alert that routes straight to a handler named **Khall.** (Khall unnamed to the player here; Ronin-7 only sees the boarding party react to a flag they can't believe.)
- The squad moves to take or kill him. **Fight:** the first true combat encounter — Ronin-7 cuts the boarding party down in the cramped medical bay and corridors, body remembering what the mind forgot.
- **Beat:** these soldiers mobilized for a man the records say is already executed. *He is supposed to be dead.* The casket in open space reads differently now — not dumped cargo, but a body the Dominion was certain it had already killed.

### Scene 4 — The Ultimatum *(the Hub — corridor walk → command room)*
- Aftermath of the boarding. Kessler tells Ronin the squad didn't die quiet: their visors streamed everything — Ronin's living face went straight up the chain before the last of them fell. The Dominion has its proof now. *(Callback to the boarding scan.)*
- As they talk, they move through the ship toward the command room. Kessler explains the debt that made him crack the casket at all — years ago this man spared his life, and a salvager pays what he owes.
- In the command room, a battered ship-drone chirps: **"Message incoming."** A hologram resolves — a Dominion handler. *(Author note: this IS Khall — kept UNNAMED on screen to preserve Ladder D, named Ch4.)*
- **The handler addresses Kessler by name.** The Dominion hasn't forgotten his prior servitude. He has **3 days** to deliver **"the lost item"** — Ronin-7 — or they execute his daughter.
- **Beat — Iris revealed:** the threat names what Kessler's been carrying. His daughter, **Iris**, taken and held in Velorum's markets as collateral; he's been scraping wrecks for the leverage to buy her back. Now there's a clock on her life.
- Ronin-7 offers himself — his life for hers, a clean trade. *He doesn't remember being a man worth keeping; this, at least, he can do.*
- **Beat — the bluff:** Kessler refuses. He knows the Dominion — hand over the "item" and they bury Iris and Ronin both and call it tidy. The handler is bluffing that obedience buys mercy; there is no mercy in them, that's the whole point. So they don't trade. They take her back.
- **Hook out:** a 3-day run to Velorum's auction, where bodies and pasts are bought — and where Iris is held. The debt is no longer one man's. *Who was I that I spared a man I can't remember? — Ask Velorum.*

## KEY SCENES (screenplay)

**INT. THE HUB — REVIVAL BAY**

> RONIN-7 has KESSLER by the throat against the bulkhead. Sparks. Silence.
>
> **KESSLER** *(strained, calm)*
> You already did the hard part. You didn't kill me — last time either.
>
> *Ronin-7's grip falters. He studies his own hand like it belongs to someone else.*
>
> **RONIN-7**
> I don't remember you.
>
> **KESSLER**
> I figured. They take that part out. *(beat)* Three weeks I've had you on that table. You've woken before — never like this. You'd thrash, look right through me, give me nothing. No name. No nothing.
>
> *Ronin-7 releases him. Looks at the scar at his own throat.*
>
> **RONIN-7**
> Where did you find me.
>
> **KESSLER**
> Floating. Sealed casket, Dominion locks, drifting in the open with no wreck for a hundred klicks. *(beat)* Somebody put you in a box and threw the box away. But six years back you let me live, when the order said clean sweep. Whatever they built you to be — you broke that day. I'm betting you can break again.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> Then they'll come to finish it.

- **Environment:** the **Hub** — Kessler's salvaged capital-class hull, of which only a few compartments are powered this chapter (a cramped lit core inside a cold, vast leviathan; full layout in `17_THE_HUB_Kesslers_Ship.md`). Persistent home base / upgrade & save node introduced here. The casket-in-space find is backstory — optionally a brief playable/cinematic flashback (Kessler's POV, the salvage-hook snag) layered under Scene 2's dialogue.
- **Encounters:** wake-up reflex fight (vs. Kessler, non-lethal/tutorial); **Dominion boarding fight** — first true combat encounter, fought in the cramped medical bay and corridors (teaches the katana in real stakes).
- **Ally unlock:** none yet — **Kessler** becomes the Hub anchor NPC.
- **Gameplay hooks:** Hub upgrade bench; the katana introduced as signature weapon (its shadow-AI nature is *not* yet revealed); "flagged face" wanted-level system seeded.
