SETTING / ENVIRONMENT — WHERE THIS CHAPTER TAKES PLACE
Reference for art, lighting, level, and audio direction. Chapter 6 took him to
the place that made the hand, and a dying architect told him the hollowness was
cut in on purpose. Chapter 7 takes him to the place that keeps the proof. The
chapter unfolds in a derelict DATA-RELIQUARY, a dead archive-hulk adrift in a
debris field off the shipping lanes: a salvager's hoard built inside the gutted
hull of a Program records-barge, where one woman has spent a lifetime collecting
the paper of dead operatives like relics. It should not feel like a dungeon. It
should feel like a tomb that someone tends. Vaulted halls of mounted blades run
off into the dark in every direction, rack on rack of sheathed and bracketed
swords, each one a kept operative's shadow-AI seated in its steel the way the
katana carries Echo. A small steady light burns in every hilt, each light a kept
witness. The air is reverent and wrong, a cathedral hush over
a warehouse of the erased. Where Chapter 6 dressed horror up as a kindness, this
one dresses it as devotion: the place is lovingly kept, and what it keeps is
unforgivable.

The reliquary is cold, low-lit, and quiet to the point of pressure. Frost rimes
the dead conduits; the salvager's own patched power runs warm only at the core,
where the racks are densest and the hilt-lights brightest. Sound design carries a
constant low layer under everything: a faint chorus of shelved shadow-AIs, awake
in the dark inside their blades, too quiet to make out, never fully silent. Treat that undertone as
the chapter's pulse. The horror here is not that the dead are gone. It is that
they were never allowed to be.

The chapter moves through five guided segments with a combat-and-traversal core,
descending from the breach in the hull, through the defended outer stacks, into
the tended core, and down into the deep archive.

  SEGMENT 0 — THE CAIRN (the briefing, before the breach)
  - Not the reliquary: Kessler's ship, the command room, Morrigan's new analysis
    bench freshly bolted into the dead bridge and already running hot. The crew
    feeds the dojo intercepts and Cipher's sabotaged switch through the engineer's
    eyes for the first time. A name surfaces out of the operative-record black
    market: Coral Vex, a salvager-archivist who hoards the records of dead
    operatives. They set a heading for her reliquary, chasing the records and the
    hand that cut his leash. Coral does NOT appear here.

  SEGMENT 1 — THE OUTER STACKS (the breach → the defended archive) — COMBAT/TRAVERSAL
  - The Cairn cannot dock; Cipher breaches the hull alone and works inward through
    the outer stacks. Archive-defense automata still run their dead patrol routes,
    and rival scavengers are prying blades off the racks for resale. A fight-and-climb
    segment through vaulted shelving and broken gravity, Echo reading the room off
    the optic feed and uneasy, for Cipher alone, about a place that collects
    shadows. Ends at the tended core, where the salvager is waiting and already
    knows him.

  SEGMENT 2 — THE ARCHIVIST (the tended core) — MEETING + REVEAL
  - Coral Vex, who knows too much and recognizes Cipher too fast. The trust-test:
    she has watched whether he kills or spares the cornered. The turn: she is not
    a collector of the dead. She is one of them, hiding in plain sight.

  SEGMENT 3 — WHAT CAME BEFORE HIM (the core, deeper) — REVEAL + ALLY #4
  - Coral reveals she is of the Wraith line, the predecessor variant the Ronin
    line was refined from, presumed scrapped, who cut her own leash herself and
    kept her own shadow. She becomes Ally #4. Framing is forebear, not sibling.

  SEGMENT 4 — THE KEPT SHADOWS (the deep archive) — REVEAL + MINI-BOSS
  - Coral shows him the truth she guards: erased operatives are not deleted, their
    shadow-AIs are seated in blades, racked and kept, every witness shelved and
    still awake, the katana writ large and made literal. The oldest blade in the
    hall, a witness left awake too long with nothing to witness, has gone berserk.
    To quiet it, Cipher must grip it and let it take his feed, which throws him
    into a mindspace duel against the blade's dead owner, the chapter's mini-boss.
    On victory the blade calms and its pacified shadow-AI gifts Cipher a permanent
    weakpoint-sight ability through Echo. Blade-rescue side-objectives thread the
    dive: the player may free other kept shadows.

  SEGMENT 5 — SABOTAGE WAS DISSENT (the read bench) — REVEAL + SEEDS
  - With her insider's eye and Morrigan's analysis, Coral reads what Cipher never
    could alone: another hand on his switch. They confirm the sabotage came from
    internal dissent, a faction inside the Program breaking leashes. The saboteur
    is NOT named. The hook out: the dissenter trail opens the quiet path to the
    Silent Garden.

INTRUDING / RECURRING ELEMENTS
- THE RACKS (the kept): vaulted halls of mounted blades running off into
  the dark, each sword's shadow-AI a small steady light in its hilt, each a
  shelved witness still awake in its steel. The chapter's atmosphere and its
  accusation both. The deeper Cipher goes, the denser and brighter the blade-fields
  get, until the core is lit like a reliquary of saints. Treat the devotion as
  the horror.
- THE UNDERTONE (the chorus): a constant low layer of shelved shadow-AIs, too
  quiet to make out, never silent. It rises when Cipher nears a dense rack and
  goes briefly, terribly clear during the mini-boss and the rescues. The sound of
  the dead not being allowed to be dead.
- ECHO (the katana's shadow-AI): heard by Ronin-7 alone, wired to his optic feed,
  sees what he sees. Calls him Cipher. Uneasy here in a way Echo has not been
  before, because this is the one place that could have shelved Echo too. The wry
  warmth thins fast; in the kept-shadows reveal Echo recognizes the racks as its
  own near-fate and goes to bare witness. The one voice that knows what these
  blades carry from the inside, because it is one.
- CORAL VEX (the forebear): the Wraith-line predecessor, hidden as a salvager for
  a lifetime. Reverent toward the shadows she keeps, fast to clock Cipher, weary
  and steel-spined from tearing out her own leash and outliving her own erasure.
  She watches the Program from underneath it. Her shadow recognizes his.

TONE OF PLACE
Chapter 6 named the flaw: they built him hollow on purpose and the seam opened
anyway. Chapter 7 asks what happened to all the others the seam never opened in,
and answers that they were not allowed to end. The horror is not violence; it is
preservation. A woman who should be a dead file is alive and keeping the dead
files company, and what she keeps is awake. The arc descends from a breached hull
through a looted outer archive into a tended core lit like a shrine, and lands on
two truths stacked on each other: he is not the first of his kind, and his escape
was not an accident. Someone inside the machine reached in and opened his door.
The chapter ends with a forebear at his shoulder, a hall of kept shadows behind
him, and the first proof that the Program is fighting itself.
