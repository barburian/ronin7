# CHAPTER 2 — THE AUCTION
**Act I · Mercy as Rebellion**
**Logline:** On the auction-world Velorum, Ronin-7 buys a smuggler's freedom, frees Kessler's stolen daughter, gains a stowaway — and learns the killswitch in his skull already fired and failed.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — command room, en route to Velorum)*
- **Aboard the rig after Ch1**, Kessler and Ronin-7 sit with the only lead they have: the Handler's three-day ultimatum and the question of who Ronin-7 was. Kessler lays it out at the battered command console — if a dead operative's records survive anywhere, they survive on **Velorum**, the auction-world that buys and sells the dead and their paperwork.
- Kessler hates the destination and says so; he swore he'd never set foot on Velorum again. He goes anyway, because the man he pulled out of a coffin has nowhere else to start.
- **Interaction:** the two of them, alone, feeling out the shape of a partnership neither asked for — the salvager who saved a weapon, the weapon learning how a person talks. Ronin-7 answers in blunt assessment; Kessler answers in gallows dry. The crew is two, and the ship is mostly dark around them.
- **Beat:** the hunt begins as an errand for paperwork and a clock counting down. They set course for Velorum and the long descent into the markets.

### Scene 2 — Velorum Descent *(auction-world undermarket)*
- The crew arrives at Velorum: flesh-markets, weapon-blocks, and the great Auction where anything with a price changes hands.
- Ronin-7 chases his only lead — a broker who trafficks in operative records.
- **Beat:** the broker is dead-end paranoid; the one man who can route past him is **Resh** — a smuggler who has spent years running stolen children out of the markets along hidden back-channels.

### Scene 3 — Sparing Resh *(the Auction floor)* — ALLY #1
- Ronin-7 sees Resh. He could take Resh by force (loud) or buy him (clever). Either way, the choice that *binds* Resh is mercy: a man with this kind of power choosing to spare rather than spend — exactly the thing Resh has never once seen from anyone standing above the gutter.
- **Resh (Ally #1)** joins — not to repay any debt, but because he wants to **stop the Dominion**. The Dominion's machine is what feeds the markets and takes the children he can only rescue one at a time; a crew led by a turned Dominion weapon is the first real chance he has seen to strike at the source instead of bailing water forever. He throws in with Kessler and Ronin-7 for that. He knows every back-channel on Velorum because he has *used* them — the same routes he runs children along to get them out of the markets. he also mentions that he got a lead this morning.
- **Plant:** as Resh has joined, a small figure in the auction crowd watches him closely — a child who knows that face. (Payoff in Scene 5: it is **Mira**.)

### Scene 4 — Iris Freed *(holding cells)*
- Resh leads them into the cells along one of his smuggling routes. Following the broker's trail there, Ronin-7 frees a captive tech held as collateral — and the name on her collar-tag is one Kessler has been chasing across the wreck-fields: **Iris**, Kessler's daughter. The broker's lead and Kessler's lost girl were the same trail all along.
- **Beat:** Kessler and Iris reunite over the comm as Ronin-7 cuts her loose — the man built to erase families just put one back together. Iris stays, joining the crew as its conscience and systems hand, and leads them on toward the broker's vault.

### Scene 5 — The Failed Execution *(broker's records-vault → diagnostic table)* — THE REVEAL
- Resh's routes reach the broker's records-vault. What the broker trades in is the paper that follows dead operatives: when the Program executes an asset it jettisons the body *and* its termination manifest as disposal, and on Velorum that disposal is salvage — bought, catalogued, and sold. (It is the same trade through which Kessler pulled Ronin-7's casket out of open space in the first place.) Ronin-7's own manifest is filed here, among the dead.
- Iris pries it loose and reads the diagnostic: his **killswitch fired** on the date of his "execution" — and *did not kill him.* **(Ladder A, rung 1.)**
- He survived his own execution; the manifest filed him as a corpse. The Program's perfect leash slipped, and no one in the records knows why.
- **Beat:** relief curdling into the hunt — if his leash failed once, others can break too, and the men who built them don't yet know it can slip at all. Ronin-7 has them copy every termination file in the vault.

### Scene 6 — Mira the Stowaway *(cargo bay, on the way out)* — CODA
- Pulling back from the vault along Resh's route, the crew falls back through a dockside cargo bay to catch its breath — and finds **Mira**, a child who slipped in after them to escape the markets. She is no stranger to Resh: she knows his face from the routes — he is the smuggler the market-children whisper about, the one who gets them out. When she spotted him on the auction floor walking free *with* Ronin-7's crew, she trusted them enough to follow them down. Kessler — softened, a father again, on the comm from the barge — hesitates over taking in a market child; Ronin-7 refuses to put her out.
- **Beat:** the crew is becoming a family of the unwanted. They take Mira and board the ship — the chapter closes on a full hold lifting off Velorum, the leash that failed copied in Ronin-7's hand.

## KEY SCENES (screenplay)

**INT. THE CAIRN — COMMAND ROOM — EN ROUTE TO VELORUM**

> KESSLER works a cracked nav-console. RONIN-7 stands at his shoulder, the wrapped katana slung low. A single lit room in a dark ship.
>
> **KESSLER**
> If your records survived anywhere, they're on Velorum. Auction-world. Sells weapons up top and people down the bottom, and files the receipts on both.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> You don't want to go.
>
> **KESSLER**
> I swore I never would. *(sets the course anyway)* But that voice gave you three days, and you've got nothing else to chase. So we go shopping for a dead man's paperwork.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> And if it's mine.
>
> **KESSLER**
> Then we find out who they buried. Strap in.

**INT. BROKER'S RECORDS-VAULT — DISPOSAL TERMINAL**

> Among the disposal manifests of executed operatives — the salvage-trade that runs through Velorum — IRIS finds one filed under Ronin-7's designation. She scrolls the med-log. Her face changes.
>
> **IRIS**
> This is your termination order. Executed. Signed off. *(beat)* The killswitch discharged. Full sequence.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> Then why am I reading it?
>
> **IRIS**
> Because it didn't take. It fired into your skull and you just... kept breathing. *(she looks up)* Do you understand what that means? Every one of you has this. Yours is the only one on record that *failed.*
>
> *Ronin-7 touches the scar at his throat — the one Kessler noticed.*
>
> **RONIN-7**
> No. Mine's the only one they've *found* that failed.

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Aboard the rig, set the Velorum lead and course (Hub briefing).* (1) Reach the Auction; locate the broker. (2) Win/buy Resh's freedom (combat OR economy path). (3) Free Iris — reunite her with Kessler. (4) Crack the broker's records-vault → discover the failed execution. (5) Fall back through the cargo bay; take in the stowaway Mira, then board ship.
- **Environment:** Velorum undermarket, Auction floor, holding cells, cargo bay (vertical, crowded, faction-dense).
- **Encounters:** Auction-floor brawl OR bidding mini-system; syndicate enforcers in the cells; mini-boss: the broker's bodyguard.
- **Ally unlock:** **Resh (#1).** Crew additions: **Iris** (Kessler's daughter), **Mira** (non-combat, story-critical).
- **Gameplay hooks:** Resh unlocks black-market vendors, info-buys, and the hidden smuggling back-channels he runs children along — the routes the crew uses to reach the cells and the records-vault here; Iris enables data-decryption gameplay (used for ladder reveals throughout) and deepens the Hub through her bond with Kessler; Mira introduces protect-the-NPC stakes — the child the crew shields across the saga.
