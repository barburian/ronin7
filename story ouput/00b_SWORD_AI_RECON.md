# RONIN-7 — RECON: THE SWORD IS A SHADOW-AI
*Single source of truth for the "sword = AI wired to Ronin-7's eyes" reframe.*
*Supersedes the "memory-vessel / stored dead" concept everywhere it appears. Read this before editing any chapter file.*

---

## 1. WHAT CHANGED (one paragraph)
The katana is no longer a *memory-vessel storing the consciousness of the dead.* It now **houses an artificial intelligence** — a defected **shadow-AI** — that is **bonded to Ronin-7's optic feed and sees what he sees.** Nobody's soul is trapped in the steel. The AI is a machine: it watched, it recorded, it modelled, and it survived the man it used to ride. The poetry shifts from *"the dead are kept"* to *"the witness remembers what the killer was made to forget."*

---

## 2. THE SHADOW-AI (core concept)
- **What a shadow-AI is:** the Program bonds every operative to an AI slaved to their senses — it rides the optic nerve, records everything the operative sees and does, models their cognition, and enforces the conditioning from the inside. The **AI is the Program's true, permanent asset; the body is the disposable part.** Kill the operative, keep the shadow.
- **This particular AI** was slaved to **Ronin-7**, the first defector. It rode his eyes through his last erasure on **Kethel-7**, through his refusal, and through Khall triggering his killswitch over it. Witnessing that — a handler condemning a man he agreed was right — **broke the AI's allegiance too.** The switch fired and *failed* (Ch2); the AI went dormant in the katana sealed in the casket with Ronin-7's body. It holds the full recorded record of the **Kethel-7 mission** — Ronin-7's own erased past.
- **Now**, aboard the Cairn after Velorum, It sees through his eyes.

### Why this satisfies the three asks
- **Sees what Ronin sees:** literal. It's slaved to his optic nerve.
- **Explains the alone-rule:** it is neurally bonded to *his* nervous system. In any other hand it has no feed and no host — inert, dead steel. (Same canon outcome as before, now with a mechanical reason.)
- **Narrative witness:** it carries **Ronin-7's own recorded footage** — the eyes it rode before the wipe — *and* its live read of everything Ronin sees, so it recognises faces, places, and ghosts of his past that his wiped memory cannot. Feeds the amnesia / "who was I" thread.

### Host-transfer rule — RESOLVED (added 2026-06-22, Ch7)
A shadow-AI bonds to **exactly one operative's nervous system at a time, and the original owner's death is a hard precondition for any transfer.**
- **While the bonded operative lives, the bond is exclusive and locked.** No one else can host the shadow — not a civilian (no compatible interface, inert dead steel) and **not even another operative** (a compatible interface, but the bond is blocked while its owner draws breath). This is why **Echo answers only to Ronin-7**: its owner — Ronin-7 himself — is alive, so the bond cannot move.
- **Only once the bonded operative is dead** does the shadow become host-free, and only then can a *new operative* (compatible feed) take it. A civilian still cannot, ever.
- Even with a dead owner, transfer is **not clean**: the shadow stays locked to the dead owner's combat imprint and, if left awake too long, goes feral. A new operative who grips it must **dominate that imprint** before the blade accepts him. → **Ch7 berserk-blade duel**: the owner is long dead (transfer possible), the shadow is feral (the duel is the cost), and Ronin — an operative — is a valid new host.

### Speaker label & name — RESOLVED
- **Ronin names it `Echo` in Ch3** — *after* the playback, in the quiet aboard the Cairn. He asks its name; it has none (the Program never names its shadows; "Shadow" is only ever the field word); he names it Echo, because it echoes back what he sees and hands him the self he forgot. In-fiction and in the scripts the speaker label is **`Shadow`** through the bonding and playback, **`Echo`** from the naming beat onward (same AI). It is *not* a stored soul and does not pretend to be one — the man in its footage is **Ronin-7 himself before the wipe**, the self he no longer remembers, not a separate person.
- **Voice — RESOLVED:** warm / personable from the first line — dry, present, emotionally fluent, buddy-companion energy, with real grief under it. Not a cold machine waking up.

## 3. WHAT IS PRESERVED (so the saga doesn't break)
Almost everything. The reframe is a swap of *substance* (dead minds → AI witnesses), not of structure.
- **The Kethel-7 mission** still happened, **Ronin-7** was still the first defector, still refused over the caretaker and the children, and **Khall** still triggered his killswitch after agreeing he was right (**"I know"**) — believing it killed him. → Ladder B rung 2, Ch8 guilt, Ch16 forged-order twist all still seed.
- **Killswitch (single event):** the switch Khall triggers on the Kethel-7 mission *is* Ronin-7's — it fired and *failed* (Ch2). The AI shows Ronin the footage of his own near-execution: the death his switch refused to complete.
- **The dive** survives as a **playback**: Ronin doesn't enter a soul, he lets the AI replay the footage it captured through **his own eyes before the wipe**. Same desaturated dreamspace; now justified as **redacted archived sensor-footage** the conditioning is trying to wall off (the "intrusion" enemies = the Program's redaction fighting the playback).
- **"Find the others"** charge intact — now: *there are other operatives still wearing live shadows; find them before the Program collects the AIs.* Still drives the ally hunt.
- **Concord Engine** intact — its transmission lattice is now the networked **shadow-AIs** instead of "archived dead." Freeing them in Ch16 still pays off.
- **Theme** intact — *"bodies and machines that remember what minds were made to forget"* is, if anything, more literal now.
