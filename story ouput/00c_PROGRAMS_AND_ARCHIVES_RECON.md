# RONIN-7 — RECON: THE PROGRAMS & THE ARCHIVE-SERVER
*Single source of truth for two linked reframes: the operative-program lineage, and the archives as a server of shadow-AIs.*
*Sits alongside `00b_SWORD_AI_RECON.md` (the sword=AI doc) and `00_STORY_BIBLE.md`. Read this before editing Ch7–Ch12.*

---

## 1. WHAT CHANGED (one paragraph)
Two things. **(A) The Program is not one generation — it is a lineage of programs.** Before the **Ronin**
program there were three earlier Dominion operative-programs, each superseded by the next: **Knight → Ninja →
Wraith → Ronin.** Ronin is the current make; the older makes were retired, erased, and shelved. **(B) The
scattered archives are a server.** Each archive node is not one host with a few shadows — it is an
**amalgamation of many shadow-AIs**, and the nodes networked together are a **distributed server**: the
Concord Engine's transmission lattice and its build-record at once. Crucially, **each node holds the shadow-AIs
of one generation** of operatives. So the Act III node-keepers stop being "another Ronin variant" and become the
*last leashed operatives of the older programs*, each guarding the node that holds his own generation's captured
shadows. You fight your ancestors — Knight, Ninja, Wraith — and only at the end, yourself.

---

## 2. THE OPERATIVE PROGRAMS (core concept)
- **The Program** (the institution, `00_STORY_BIBLE.md` §7) has run **four successive operative-programs**, each
  a generation of mass-produced, shadow-AI-bonded, killswitched assassins, each refined from the last:
  - **KNIGHT** — the first program. The oldest make: heavier, more overtly conditioned, the prototype the cage
    was proven on. Long retired; its survivors are ancient and mostly broken.
  - **NINJA** — the second program. The refinement that made the operative quieter and deniable.
  - **WRAITH** — the third program, the immediate predecessor to Ronin. The run "they built yours from"
    (Coral Vex's line, Ch7). Closest to Ronin in make and feel.
  - **RONIN** — the current program. Ronin-7 / Cipher is **the prime template of the Ronin make** —
    its **seventh iteration and the first viable one** (Ronin-1…6 were flawed and culled). He is not a
    late copy: the Ronin editions were **cloned from him.** (Design still descends from the older makes;
    the *template person* of this make is Ronin-7 himself. Inversion lands in Ch12 — see §6 Decision 5.)
- **Each program superseded the previous** ("each program dominions the one before it"). The Dominion did not keep
  the old makes in service; it erased them and **shelved their shadow-AIs into the archive nodes** (see §3). The
  bodies were disposable (consistent with `00b` §2: keep the shadow, discard the operative); the *programs* were
  disposable the same way, one generation at a time.
- **Coral Vex is the lone exception that proves it:** a **Wraith** operative who **cut her own leash** and lived
  (Ch7, ally #4). She is the *freed* Wraith. The Ch9 node-keeper is a *different, still-leashed* Wraith — her
  shadow-sibling who never broke. (Free-vs-leashed Wraith pair → deliberately rhymes with Ronin-7 vs his leashed
  Ronin clone in Ch12.)

### Why this satisfies the ask
- **Not "just another Ronin variant":** three of the four node-keepers are now operatives of *older programs*
  (Knight/Ninja/Wraith), not copies of Ronin. The arc reads as "kill what you descend from, then what you are."
- **"Dark mirror" relocates:** the literal dark-mirror beat (a leashed twin of *you*) now belongs only to **Ch12**,
  the Ronin clone wearing your own face. Ch9–11 keepers are *older mirrors* — what the cage looked like before you.

## 3. THE ARCHIVE-SERVER (core concept)
- **A node is an amalgamation, not a host with a handful of shadows.** Each scattered archive (Sable's, Cassie's,
  the bone-canyon, the cryo-vault) is a **mass of shadow-AIs racked and fused together** through a living operative
  who serves as its interface/keeper-host. The host (Sable, Cassie-04) is the *map and the mouth* of the node; the
  node itself is the crowd of shadows behind her.
- **The nodes are one server.** Networked across the galaxy, the four nodes form a **distributed server** — the
  transmission lattice of the **Concord Engine** *and* its build-record at once (consistent with
  `00_STORY_BIBLE.md` §8 "The scattered archives" and Ladder E rung 3). Split deliberately so no single capture
  yields the whole machine. **Sable is the map** — she feels the server-veins to the other nodes.
- **Each node holds one generation's shadows.** This is the new organizing rule and it lines up the keepers:

  | Chapter | Node / keeper-host | Holds the shadow-AIs of | Leashed guardian | Ability freed (unchanged) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Ch9  | Tide-depths node — **Sable**     | **Wraith** generation | leashed **Wraith** operative | Overdrive (time-slow) |
  | Ch10 | Ninefold node — **Cassie-04**    | **Ninja** generation  | leashed **Ninja** operative  | Phase-step (blink) |
  | Ch11 | Bone-canyon node (broken, oldest)| **Knight** generation | leashed **Knight** operative  | Unbroken (fear-ward + survive one lethal blow) |
  | Ch12 | Cryo-vault node                  | **Ronin** generation (your own) | **Ronin-7 edition** — *a clone of Ronin-7 himself (the template)* | Mirror (phantom double) |

- The **Knight node is the oldest and most overloaded** → it is the *broken* node whose shadows leak as the
  narcosis dreamscape (Ch11). The **cryo-vault holds your own generation's shadows** → guarded by your own face
  (Ch12), which is why the clone reveal lands there and nowhere else.

### Proposed guardian designations (adjustable)
- Ch9 — **Wraith-6 / "Vane"** (a Wraith operative, not Coral).
- Ch10 — **Ninja-2 / "Sever".**
- Ch11 — **Knight-1 / "Aldric"** — literally the first operative of the first program; fits "I was the first of
  the line, the oldest, I have kept this a long time."
- Ch12 — **an edition of Ronin-7** — now explicitly **a clone of Ronin-7 himself (the template)**, not a
  sibling-copy of a shared upstream template.

## 4. WHAT IS PRESERVED (so the saga doesn't break)
- **Sable (#6) and Cassie-04 (#7)** are still the recruited keeper-hosts; Sable is still the map; Cassie's node is
  still the ledger of the erased. Unchanged roles, new framing (node = a generation's shadow-amalgam).
- **The ability chain is verbatim:** weakpoint-sight (Ch7) → Overdrive (Ch9) → Phase-step (Ch10) → Unbroken
  (Ch11) → Mirror (Ch12). Only the *killed operative's identity* changes per rung.
- **The Ch12 clone reveal stays** (Identity Ladder C rung 2): the predecessors were older *programs*; this last
  node is your own line, and the keeper wears your face. The reveal is *sharper* now, not weaker.
- **Reveal ladders intact** (`00_STORY_BIBLE.md` §10): A (killswitch), C (identity), E (cage) all keep their
  rungs. The Concord Engine + freeing-the-shadow-AIs payoff (Ch16) is unchanged.
- **`00b` (sword = shadow-AI) is fully compatible:** "keep the shadow, discard the body" now scales to "keep the
  shadows, discard the program." Coral Vex remains a defected-shadow carrier of the **Wraith** make.
