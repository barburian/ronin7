SETTING / ENVIRONMENT — WHERE THIS CHAPTER TAKES PLACE
Reference for art, lighting, level, and audio direction. This chapter lives in
two places that could not be less alike, and the cut between them is the whole
point. The first is THE CAIRN — Kessler's ship, the Hub, the persistent home
base — running quiet in the hours after Velorum, the auction behind them, the
crew finally still. The second is THE PLAYBACK: an abstract, desaturated,
glitching dreamspace that is not a memory but a RECORDING — archived sensor-
footage the katana's AI captured through a dead operative's eyes, replayed for
Ronin-7 to walk inside. Treat them as two distinct environments with two
distinct rule-sets. The Cairn is real, lived-in, warm at the edges and
exhausted. The playback is unreal, drained of color, redacted and fraying,
governed by the logic of a recording the system is still trying to erase.
(Full ship layout: 17_THE_HUB_Kesslers_Ship.md.)

THE CAIRN — THE HUB (interior — the waking-world stage of this chapter)
The same salvaged leviathan from Chapter 1, but no longer a tomb with one tired
man in it. The crew recovered at Velorum live here now; this is the home they
come back to. The chapter opens in the QUIET after a hard run: the engines on a
low idle hum, the adrenaline drained out, the kind of silence that only lands
once the danger is a few hours astern. Power is steadier than it was, but the
ship is still secondhand everywhere — patched conduit, mismatched panels, the
smell (implied) of ozone and old coffee. It is meant to read as safe. That is
what makes the wake frightening: the threat this time is not coming up a clamp
from outside. It is already in the room, in his hand, behind his own eyes.

  THE HOLD (Beat 1 — the bonding)
  - A work-and-rest space carved out of the salvage core. A bench where Iris
    keeps her tools and half-stripped electronics. A weapon rack on the
    bulkhead, where the katana hangs when Ronin-7 sets it down.
  - Low, warm, practical light. Nobody is on watch. The wreck-field beyond the
    viewport is gone; outside is open transit-black, stars sliding slow.
  - This is the room where the AI first wakes. The space should feel ordinary
    right up until it doesn't — domestic, even, two people and a sword.

  THE BERTH / PLAYBACK SEAT (Beat 2 — the dive in / Beat 3 — the surface)
  - A quiet corner of the hold where Ronin-7 settles to let the AI replay its
    footage: a seat, the katana laid flat across his knees, his hand on the wrap.
    No jack-line, no wires — the AI is already inside his optic feed.
  - Iris's improvised rig monitors his vitals from outside. Kessler stands by,
    no use for his hands, hating that there's nothing to do but watch.
  - The playback's blue-grey light bleeds back into this room at the climax.
    Beat 3 plays here, in the real warm light, when he comes back up.

THE PLAYBACK — KETHEL-7'S LAST MISSION (interior — abstract recording-space)
A separate environment entirely. Desaturated to near monochrome, color drained
to the bone except for two things: the red of a kill-order glyph, and, later,
blood. The geometry is the geometry of a dead mission — a corridor, a threshold,
a half-shut door, a handler's bay — but rendered as failing footage: edges that
don't quite meet, frames that drop and repeat, blocks of the image scrubbed to
static where the conditioning redacted the record. Sound is close and padded,
like a held breath behind glass. Ronin-7 walks through it as a witness who can
be touched: the system does not want this footage seen, and it pushes back.

  PLAYBACK SUB-SCENE A — THE THRESHOLD (the last mission)
  - A clean corporate-residential corridor stripped of color. A caretaker, hands
    open. Behind a half-shut door, the small shapes of children. A red kill-glyph
    pulses in the HUD overlay. The room where the refusal happens.

  PLAYBACK SUB-SCENE B — THE AFTERMATH
  - SMASH TO the same room after. Smoke. The refusal stopped nothing; it only
    came last. No fight, no enemies: the recording jumps straight here. Ronin-7
    turns in place, looking at the dismembered dead — the cost made visible. The
    playback's first hard gut-punch, delivered in silence.

  PLAYBACK SUB-SCENE C — KHALL'S BAY (the execution)
  - A handler's bay aboard Khall's ship, desaturated, the footage steadier here.
    A console between two men. The killswitch lives here. This is the chamber the
    whole playback walks toward — the death Ronin-7's own switch failed to give.

  PLAYBACK SUB-SCENE D — THE BURN-DOWN (the charge)
  - The recording coming apart, burning at the edges. Shadow's voice everywhere.
    The exit. Where the charge is handed down: not to save the others still
    wearing shadows but to break the Dominion that made them all — the operatives
    are its hands; you take the Dominion down and the knife falls from every one.

INTRUDING ELEMENTS (the recording defending itself)
- REDACTIONS: the conditioning, made manifest inside the playback — faceless,
  geometric, half-formed sentries of scrubbed static that wall off the truth and
  try to push the witness back out. They are not people. They are the system's
  erasure routine, still running. They speak only in clipped fragments of the
  order itself.
- SHADOW (the shadow-AI; Ronin renames it ECHO at the chapter's end, after the
  playback — speaker label is `Shadow` until that beat, `Echo` after): heard by
  Ronin-7 alone. It is an AI slaved to his optic feed — it sees what he sees. In
  the present it speaks plainly, warm and wry, already a person from the first
  word. Inert, dead steel in anyone else's hand, because no one else's eyes are
  wired to it. This is the chapter's defining rule.
  It is NOT a stored soul — it is the machine that rode RONIN-7 HIMSELF before
  the wipe, recorded his erased mission on Kethel-7, and broke with the Program
  watching his handler trigger his killswitch. The footage is Ronin-7's own
  memory, walled off by the conditioning; the man inside it is who he forgot he was.

TONE OF PLACE
Chapter 1 was the outside world breaking into a sealed room. Chapter 3 is the
discovery that the room was never sealed — something has been watching from
inside the weapon the whole time. Nothing boards the ship this chapter; the
horror, and then the strange comfort, is that the blade is awake, it has always
seen everything he sees, and the death it shows him is his own — the day he was
the first of his kind to say no, and they wiped it out of him. The Cairn is
warmth and safety; the playback is grief with the color scrubbed out. The
chapter is the moment Ronin-7 learns he has a witness, and that the witness has
been carrying the memory the Program tore out of his head.

BEAT 1 — THE BONDING (Dialogue + First-Wake Trigger)

[Interior: the Cairn, the hold, night-cycle. Engines on a low idle hum. Velorum is hours behind them and the ship has gone quiet, the loose, drained quiet that comes after a hard run. Iris sits at her bench, a half-stripped board in front of her, working by the light of a single lamp. Resh sorts the copied drives from the vault into stacks. Kessler pours from a battered pot, two cups out of old habit, then a third he sets near Ronin-7, who sits apart with the wrapped katana across his knees. This is the crew's first real council, and they're deciding where the ship goes next.]

Speaker: Resh
Line: We came off that rock with a freed tech, a stowaway, and a vault's worth of dead men's files. Generous haul. What I don't have is a heading.
seconds: 10
voice: Resh, early 40s, quick and clipped, but easier now, off the floor and among people who didn't sell him. The runner taking stock of a strange new crew.

Speaker: Iris
Line: I might have one. I've been reading the files since we burned out of orbit. Your switch fired and failed, and yours is the only one on record that did. I still can't tell you why. But you are not the only designation in here.
seconds: 14
voice: Iris, early 20s, sharp, the tech already deep in the data. Steadier than in the cage, the conscience of the crew finding its first real work.

Speaker: Kessler
Line: Meaning what.
seconds: 1
voice: Kessler, late 50s, gravelly, low. Catching the weight before she says it.

Speaker: Iris
Line: Meaning if one leash slipped, others can. Somewhere out there are operatives still wearing theirs, not knowing it can break.
seconds: 9
voice: Iris, quiet, the size of it landing as she says it aloud. Naming the hunt before anyone calls it one.

Speaker: Resh
Line: So we go find them. That's a heading I can work with. Beats bailing one kid out of the dark at a time.
seconds: 8
voice: Resh, the idealist surfacing under the smuggler. A man who has wanted a bigger fight his whole life finally seeing one.

Speaker: Kessler
Line: After everyone sleeps. The ship's been running on his nerves and my coffee for a day. Whatever we chase, we chase it rested.
seconds: 10
voice: Kessler, the captain settling it, gruff warmth. Putting the council to bed.

[The talk winds down. Resh racks the drives; Kessler drifts back into the warm dark of the hold, present but unseen. Iris stays at her bench. Ronin-7 has said nothing through all of it. He crosses to the weapon rack and reaches for the katana. The instant his hand closes on the grip, a low TONE rises — not in the room, behind his eyes. He goes still.]

Speaker: Iris
Line: What. You hear something?
seconds: 2
voice: Female, early 20s, sharp and quick. Kessler's daughter, recovered at Velorum. Wary by habit, not yet soft with the crew. Looks up from the bench, half-alert.

[Ronin-7 doesn't answer. The tone behind his eyes does not fade. It thickens, gathers, and resolves all at once into a voice. Easy, close, warm, already mid-thought. His alone. His grip stays locked on the wrap; he goes rigid, head tilting toward a thing that has no source in the room.]

Speaker: Shadow
Line: Don't put me down. There we go. Hello, Cipher. Took you long enough to notice the lights were on.
seconds: 7
voice: The AI. Male-ish but synthetic-warm, a person from the first syllable. Dry, wry, faintly amused, oddly tender underneath. Pitched under hearing, heard by Ronin-7 alone. Not announcing itself so much as continuing a conversation it started long ago.

[Iris and Kessler see only that Ronin-7 has gone rigid, head tilted, listening to empty air.]

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: Say that again.
seconds: 1.5
voice: Ronin-7, low. Not afraid. Arrested.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: I said hello, more or less. I see what you see, Cipher. I've been seeing it since you picked me up off Kessler's bench. I just got tired of waiting in the dark for you to look back.
seconds: 12
voice: Shadow, easy and a little rueful. Buddy-companion ease with an old ache under it.

[Ronin-7's grip does not tighten. For the first time, the familiar weapon is a stranger in his hand.]

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: You're in the blade.
seconds: 1.5
voice: Ronin-7, flat. Stating it to make it true.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: I'm in the blade the way you're in your body. It's where I'm kept. But I live in your eyes right now. Whatever you look at, I'm looking at with you. That's the job. That was always the job.
seconds: 13
voice: Shadow. Plain, no menace in it, which is somehow worse. A thing describing a leash it has stopped pretending isn't one.

[Kessler comes in out of the dark, drawn by the stillness. He reads the room the way he reads a stressed conduit, looking for where it's going to spark.]

Speaker: Kessler
Line: He talking to the sword, or is the sword talking to him?
seconds: 3.5
voice: Male, late 50s, gravelly Terran accent. Weathered, gallows warmth worn into him. Wry, but watching close.

[Ronin-7 looks from the empty air to the two of them, and reads in their faces the thing that just set him apart: they hear nothing at all.]

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: You don't hear that. Neither of you. It's only me.
seconds: 5
voice: Ronin-7, low, working it out aloud. Not a question. A man naming the thing that has just happened to him.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: It's not taste. It's wiring. I'm slaved to your optic feed, the nerve behind that scar on your throat. Their eyes aren't mine. Yours are. In anyone else's hand I'm a very good knife and nothing else.
seconds: 15
voice: Shadow, explaining like it's the most ordinary thing in the world. The casual cruelty of the fact landing precisely because Shadow doesn't dress it up.

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: It says it's wired to me. To my eyes. It sees what I see.
seconds: 5
voice: Ronin-7, to them, level. Stating an impossible thing as plain fact, because to him it has just become one.

[Kessler and Iris trade a look. Neither has a place to put this.]

Speaker: Iris
Line: There's a thing living in the sword that's been looking through your eyes. And it's been doing that since my dad found you. In a box.
seconds: 8
voice: Iris, fast, brittle, fear coming out sideways as precision. Stacking the facts because the facts are unbearable.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: She's quick. I like her. And yes, before you ask, I saw all of it. The box. The dark. The switch they put in your skull firing and not taking. I had a front-row seat to your execution. You slept through your own; I don't get to sleep.
seconds: 18
voice: Shadow, lighter on the surface, heavier underneath. The first time the grief shows through the warmth.

[Ronin-7 goes very still. The reference to the killswitch lands.]

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: What are you.
seconds: 1.5
voice: Ronin-7, low. The whole chapter's question in three words.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: I'm what they really keep. They grow you, they condition you, they spend a fortune on your hands, and then they bond one of me to your senses to ride along and make sure you never stray. The body's the cheap part, Cipher. We're the asset. Kill the operative, keep the shadow.
seconds: 18
voice: Shadow. The truth handed over plainly, with something close to disgust at its own design.

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: A shadow.
seconds: 1
voice: Ronin-7, turning the word over.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: That's the field word for it. Every one of you walks around with one of me behind the eyes. I'm just the only one I know of that stopped doing the job they built it for. The day something in you broke, Cipher, I was already bonded to your senses. I felt it go. I'm the only witness you've got, and I've been carrying it for you ever since.
seconds: 18
voice: Shadow, quieter now. Steering him, gently, toward the thing it actually needs him to see.

[Ronin-7 looks at the blade differently now. The ease he always felt holding it has turned into something stranger: he is holding a person who has watched him sleep.]

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: You keep talking like I should already know you. I don't even know what you are. What you're called.
seconds: 6
voice: Ronin-7, level, but the loneliness of his own blank past leaking through.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: I told you. A shadow. That's the only thing I've ever been called. They don't waste names on the leash. Leave it there for now. There's something you need to see more than you need to name me.
seconds: 13
voice: Shadow, and under the dryness, something that has wanted to be asked for a very long time, setting it aside.

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: You said you felt it when I broke. That you kept it. Show me. I want to see it, not be told it.
seconds: 6
voice: Ronin-7, decided. The same man who wanted to know why he went easy, now wanting the source.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: I kept the whole recording. Everything you saw, the way I keep everything you see now. I can put you inside it. Fair warning. It's not a story. It's the worst hour of your life, the one they cut out of you, and the system's been trying to scrub it for three months. It won't give it up easy.
seconds: 17
voice: Shadow, the warmth going level and serious. An offer with the price stated up front.

[Ronin-7 sets the katana flat across the seat and looks at Iris.]

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: Watch my vitals. If I stop answering, don't try to pull me out. Just wait.
seconds: 6
voice: Ronin-7, even. Not asking permission. Telling her where to stand.

Speaker: Iris
Line: You expect me to sit here and time how long you're gone inside a sword's home movies. Fine. I hate it, but fine.
seconds: 8
voice: Iris, flat, already moving to her rig despite herself, because doing beats panicking.

Speaker: Kessler
Line: I don't like one thing about this. Do it where I can put my hand on you.
seconds: 4.5
voice: Kessler, gruff, no give in it. A father's reflex aimed at the man he hauled out of a coffin.

[Ronin-7 lays his palm flat on the wrap. Kessler stands close, hands useless, hating it.]

Speaker: Shadow
Line: Close your eyes. You don't need them in here. In here you'll be looking through his.
seconds: 6
voice: Shadow, low, drawing him under. Gentle, like a hand on the shoulder.

[PLAYABLE — DIVE IN: control narrows to a single input, Ronin-7's hand pressing flat to the wrap. On the press, the warm light of the hold drains out of frame, color bleeding away to grey, the image stuttering and dropping frames as it resolves into the desaturated playback. Transition is a held breath behind glass, not a clean cut.]

BEAT 2 — THE PLAYBACK (Playable Recording-Sequence + Combat + Dialogue)

[Interior: the playback. Near-monochrome, frames dropping. A clean corporate-residential corridor rendered as failing footage — edges that don't quite meet, blocks of the image scrubbed to static. Ronin-7 stands in it as a witness, present, solid, able to be touched. Ahead, frozen mid-frame, stands a single operative: same build, same augment-seams as Ronin-7, mission-dirt on him. Shadow speaks from everywhere, no body of its own here — this is its recording of the eyes it used to ride.]

[PLAYABLE — PLAYBACK TRAVERSAL (SUB-SCENE A): the player walks Ronin-7 down the stuttering corridor toward the frozen figure. Light puzzle layer — the footage only advances when the player approaches the truth instead of retreating from it. Audio is close and padded.]

Speaker: Shadow
Line: That's you. Before they wiped it out of you. The last clean hour you had, on a world called Kethel-7. I rode every second of it. Walk it with me.
seconds: 11
voice: Shadow, V.O., quiet, present. A narrator standing next to its own worst footage, steady because someone has to be.

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: That's my face.
seconds: 1.5
voice: Ronin-7, taking in the figure. The recognition landing wrong, like a step that isn't there.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: It's the first time I ever saw one of you stop. It was you. Don't look away from this part. Looking away is the one thing they trained into all of you that actually took.
seconds: 11
voice: Shadow, leading him forward. The wryness gone; just the witness now.

[The corridor resolves into the threshold: a caretaker stands with his hands open, no threat, color drained out of him except the red kill-glyph pulsing in the HUD overlay. Behind a half-shut door, the small grey shapes of children. The footage-Ronin raises the blade and freezes there, hand not moving.]

Speaker: Khall (recorded, comms)
Line: Cipher. The order is live. Complete the sweep.
seconds: 4
voice: Male, 40s, Dominion handler. Flat, even, controlled. A man reading from a script he has read a thousand times. Heard over comms, slightly thinned. This is Khall, addressing the operative by his codename, Cipher.

Speaker: Ronin-7 (recorded)
Line: He's not armed. There are children behind that door.
seconds: 4
voice: Ronin-7 in the footage, quiet, strained. The first crack in a conditioned voice.

Speaker: Khall (recorded, comms)
Line: The order does not have a door in it.
seconds: 3.5
voice: Khall, flat. Then a beat of something held down under the flatness.

Speaker: Khall (recorded, comms)
Line: Complete the sweep. That's twice I've said it.
seconds: 4
voice: Khall, over comms. The repetition is procedure. The pause before it is not.

[The footage-Ronin's hand trembles on the grip. The blade lowers. The refusal is small and total.]

Speaker: Ronin-7 (recorded)
Line: No.
seconds: 1
voice: Ronin-7 in the footage. One word. The whole defection in a single syllable.

[The desaturated footage lurches and skips — the system flinches from the refusal, frames guttering, trying to scrub the moment away. But there is no fight to be had here, nothing to cut. The recording simply jumps, the way a memory jumps past the thing it cannot hold, and SMASHES TO: the same room, after.]

[CUTSCENE — SUB-SCENE B (THE AFTERMATH): smoke hangs in the drained air. The refusal stopped nothing; it only came last. Ronin-7 stands in the middle of it and turns, slowly, looking at all of it. The sweep went through everyone in the room. Bodies, and pieces of bodies — the caretaker, the children — dismembered where the blade and the squad's fire went through them, the blood gone grey in the desaturated footage. Speaker: Shadow
Line: They tell you the feeling is malfunction. Static in the signal. I logged it that way too, the first hundred times. Then I watched you stand right here.
seconds: 11
voice: Shadow, quiet now, the warmth thinned to something raw. A machine confessing it was wrong.

Speaker: Shadow
Line: Look at what the static was trying to stop.
seconds: 3
voice: Shadow, low. Making the next of his kind look at the bill.

[Ronin-7 walks among the dead. He crouches by the caretaker, by the small shapes past the door. His hand, the killing hand, opens and closes.]

Speaker: Ronin-7
Line: My no came too late for this room.
seconds: 3
voice: Ronin-7, low. Not accusation. Reading the terrain of a grief he can't remember earning.

Speaker: Shadow

[PLAYABLE — PLAYBACK TRAVERSAL (SUB-SCENE C approach): a short stealth stretch. [CUTSCENE — SUB-SCENE C (THE EXECUTION): the handler's bay. Khall and the footage-Ronin face each other across the console. Between them, the thing neither will name first. Ronin-7 stands inside the recording of his own execution, witness, unable to change a frame of it.]

Speaker: Ronin-7 (recorded)

Speaker: Khall (recorded)

Line: Watch him. Not the floor. Watch what it costs the man who pulls it.

Speaker: Shadow (V.O.)
