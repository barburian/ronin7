# CHAPTER 14 — THE CAGE `[CUT]`

**This chapter was cut from the saga.**

The original Ch14 ("The Cage") ran a harvest-festival infiltration: a planet-wide festival as cover
for a child-harvest in the marrow chambers beneath it, a reliving of the Kethel-7 genocide, and the
reveal that the ten syndicates exist to keep the galaxies at war (the cage's full shape).

It was dropped because the chapter did not work. Its only load-bearing reveal — **Ladder E rung 4,
"the war is the *product*"** — is now **folded into the finale** (`Ch16`, Scene 8 / the forged-order
reveal), where the Synod's perpetual-war motive is disclosed as part of unmasking Maelgorn. Ladder E
accordingly collapses from five rungs to four (close at the finale).

Two threads the original chapter carried were **intentionally not salvaged**:
- **Mira's payoff** — her arc now rests on her Ch2 rescue and her place as family aboard The Cairn;
  there is no dedicated child-harvest payoff chapter.
- **The Kethel-7 "genocide with his own hands" set-piece** — treated as already reckoned across
  Ch3 (the sword's playback), Ch6 (Kaelen's confession), and Ch8 (the Silent Garden).

Story order is now: **Ch13 (the maker) → Ch16 (the finale: the Iron Sepulcher descent, reclaiming
Soren, and the Throne of Ashes).** See `Ch15` (merged into `Ch16`) and `Ch16` for the ending.
