SETTING / ENVIRONMENT — WHERE THIS CHAPTER TAKES PLACE
Reference for art, lighting, level, and audio direction. Chapter 2 was a market
that sold people; Chapter 4 is a city that sells everything else, and tonight it
is selling him. The whole chapter unfolds in DROVIS, a lawless syndicate free-
port built down the walls of a canyon packed with grounded, cannibalized ship-
wrecks: dead capital hulls welded into tenements, salvage stacked into a city.
It is run by THE COIL, the first named of the Ten Syndicates. Drovis is neutral
ground, no Dominion garrison, which is why a Program courier on the run comes
here to sell and vanish, and why Khall cannot send soldiers and must instead put
a recovery bounty on the table the Coil is glad to collect. Treat the MANHUNT as
the fifth character: not a chase down a corridor but the entire city turned into
a net, scan-drones drifting, enforcers thumbing slates, every face read against
his. The pressure is ambient, patient, everywhere. The crew is never not hunted.
(Full ship layout: 17_THE_HUB_Kesslers_Ship.md.)

The chapter moves through five guided segments, descending from the open docks
at the canyon rim down to a single beaten man at the bottom of the world.

  SEGMENT 0 — THE CAIRN (the briefing, before the drop)
  - Not Drovis: Kessler's ship, the command room, more of it lit than a chapter
    ago. A salvaged holo-plate throws the canyon-city up in projection while the
    crew sets the job. The crew is four and a child now, still new to each other.
    The only quiet before the city becomes the net: where the courier lead is laid
    out, Mira is ordered to stay sealed aboard, and the shuttle is sent down.

  SEGMENT 1 — THE THROAT (descent / dock canyon)
  - The free-port's mouth: a vast wreck-canyon where boarding shuttles ground on
    gantries bolted into dead hulls. Smoke, sodium glare, hanging cargo, the roar
    of a city that runs all night. Scan-drones drift the crowd throwing pale grids
    across passing faces; Coil enforcers thumb slates at the chokepoints.
  - The first read of the manhunt: impersonal, total, the city itself the net.
  - At a rail across the dock, a figure watches one beat too long, then is gone.
    The first, unnamed glimpse of Mera Voss. Seeded here, paid off in Segment 5.

  SEGMENT 2 — THE SINK (black-market bazaar)
  - A churning bazaar sunk into the canyon's mid-tier: stacked stalls, fences,
    chop-shops, salvage graded and resold. Lower and tighter than the Throat,
    louder, harder to see across. The place a courier comes to dump hot goods and
    disappear into the churn.
  - Where the crew reaches Tessa Rin, fends off a Coil snatch-team, and Iris
    cracks the truth out of the Program data: the killswitch was sabotaged.

  SEGMENT 3 — THE MAST (hijacked relay spire)
  - A relay spire crowning the wreck-stacks, an old Overseer comms mast the Coil
    has tapped to run the bounty. High, exposed, wind-loud, strung with cable.
    The chamber at the top hums with a signal that is not the Coil's.
  - The room where the handler answers in person: a flat, omnidirectional voice
    filling the dark. Where the name lands, both names, his and Khall's.

  SEGMENT 4 — THE DEEPWORKS (flooded cave network beneath the city)
  - The mine-and-cave network the free-port was built over: collapsing ledges,
    rusted spars, drowned shafts, drops over black water that gives nothing back.
    No clean route in. A movement-and-tension beat, not a battle: wall-runs,
    ledge-jumps, failing footing, the dark thickening the deeper it goes.
  - Echo reads the cave and calls the routes. The city's net thins to nothing
    down here; by the bottom it is just the man, the dark, and the one he came for.

  SEGMENT 5 — KERRAX'S HOLD (the Coil's keep, deep in the caves)
  - A syndicate bolt-hole sunk at the foot of the Deepworks: a salvaged keep of
    plundered plate, trophy-cargo, and the cold light of stolen Dominion gear. The
    air is close and still: a slow drip of water in the dark, the low hum of that
    stolen gear. The chamber where Kerrax sells him to Khall, and the chamber where
    the choice that closes Act I is made.
  - The mercy happens here, watched from cover. Where Mera Voss steps out of the
    dark and stops being a hunter.

INTRUDING / RECURRING ELEMENTS
- THE MANHUNT (the heat): scan-drones, Coil enforcer patrols, the recovery notice
  live on every slate. It rises with exposure and bleeds off in the crowd. It is
  the chapter's recurring pressure and carries the chapter's title. Treat it as
  weather, not an enemy you can kill.
- THE COIL: the syndicate that owns Drovis. Enforcers, snatch-teams, lookouts,
  and Kerrax at the bottom of it. Owners collecting a bounty, not soldiers on a
  cause. Bored, professional, in it for the cut.
- KHALL (the Overseer, V.O. this chapter): the handler hunting him, heard only as
  a voice through the Mast relay. Clinical, controlled, precise, with audible
  buried guilt under the procedure. He wants Cipher brought in alive. He is the
  same handler from the Ch3 playback, named here for the first time.
- ECHO (the katana's shadow-AI): heard by Ronin-7 alone, wired to his optic feed,
  sees what he sees. Already named (Ch3). Calls him Cipher from his first line.
  Dry, warm, the only voice in his ear all chapter, and the only one that can read
  the dark of the Deepworks for him.

TONE OF PLACE
Chapter 1 was the outside breaking into a sealed room; Chapter 3 was the room
turning out to have a watcher in it. Chapter 4 is the moment the whole world
becomes the room. The hunt has no single hunter to face until the very end; it is
the city, the crowd, the drone, the slate, until at last it narrows to one voice
that knows his name and one beaten man who sold it. The arc of the chapter is a
descent out of noise into silence: from a thousand faces scanning his, down to
the dark where there is no one left to perform for, where he spares a man because
he chooses to, and someone who came to confirm a kill watches a machine choose
twice.
