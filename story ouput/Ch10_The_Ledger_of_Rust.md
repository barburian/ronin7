# CHAPTER 10 — THE LEDGER OF RUST `[CAVE]`
**Act III · Predators Become Protectors · Episodes EP19–EP20**
**Logline:** Sable's map leads the crew to the first of her scattered kin — Cassie-04, a living archive buried in the Ninefold mine shafts whose lattice holds the names of every erased operative — kept under guard by a still-leashed Ninja-program operative; and a vengeance-driven raider, Vess, must choose between killing the man who took her people and joining the cause that could free them.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — war-room holo-table)*
- The Concord Engine reveal (Ch9) gives the crew a target and a gap: the Engine's **build-record** was never one book. The Dominion split it across its **living archives** — operatives like Sable, each carrying one section of the build in their racked shadows — so no single capture yields the whole. Sable is the map: she can feel the nearest node still strung into the lattice, filed deep in the **Ninefold mine shafts** — nine descending tiers where the Dominion keeps its accounts where no signal reaches.
- **Interaction:** Sable, racked with kept shadows, unsettles and moves the crew at once — and quietly tells Cipher this is the first of three she can feel, and the only one she might bring up alive. Gryph, the new ally, an old warrior who knows the buried deep firsthand. The node down there isn't theirs yet, and it isn't unguarded. The roster argues the descent over the holo-table. Echo, for Cipher alone, wonders whose names they're about to read.
- **Beat:** they deploy into the Ninefold descent for the first scattered archive — the node that could name every erased operative. *(Hands into the shafts.)*

### Scene 2 — The Ninefold Shafts *(cave: nine-tiered mine complex)*
- Following the Engine's build-trail to its record-store, the crew enters the **Ninefold mine shafts** — nine descending tiers, each older and stranger.
- **Beat:** the Dominion keeps the Engine's accounts physically, deep where no signal reaches — paranoia made architecture.

### Scene 3 — Cassie-04 and Her Keeper *(shaft seven: the archive)* — ALLY #7 / BOSS
- **Cassie-04 (Ally #7)** is a **living archive** like Sable — an operative racked with kept shadow-AIs (the **Ninja** generation, two makes older than Ronin's line), her lattice carrying the **ledger of the erased**: every operative ever erased and their shadow filed away, with true names and disposal sites, and the **cross-index** to where the other two scattered nodes are held.
- But the Dominion knows what a live node is worth, and has posted a keeper. **Sever / Ninja-2**, a still-leashed survivor of the **Ninja program** — the Vane of this descent, fully conditioned, deployed to guard the asset. He cannot be reasoned with or subdued; to free Cassie, Ronin-7 has to put him down. (Unlike the empty Vane, Sever is *almost* present — a man straining against his own leash; an older make of the same cage, not a copy of Ronin-7.)
- **Beat:** killing Sever frees his **blade-shadow**, which grants Ronin-7 **Phase-step** — a short blink through matter and bodies (third permanent unlock, after Ch7's weakpoint-sight and Ch9's Overdrive). Sable, watching a sister come loose from the racks instead of dying on them, is the one who convinces Cassie she can be more than infrastructure *(callback to Sable's own Ch9 line)*.
- **Beat:** the dead now have *names.* Ronin-7 can finally read who was taken — possibly including clues to himself (his true name is *not* here yet; that's Ch16).

### Scene 4 — Vess's Vengeance *(the ship entrance: the mercy-mirror)* — ALLY #8
- **Vess**, a raider whose people were destroyed by Ronin-7's old line, has tracked him to the mine's only way out and lies in wait at the salvage rig. As the crew climbs out of the Ninefold, she takes him at the ship entrance and gets her chance to kill him.
- The names Cassie carries change everything: they prove the killings fed the same machine that files the shadows into the Engine, the operatives themselves victims — and that the freed node is the key to dismantling it.
- **Beat:** Vess chooses **alliance over vengeance.** Killing Ronin-7 buries the names with him; sparing him weaponizes them against the real enemy. **Ally #8.**

### Scene 5 — Reading the Names *(shaft seven, after)*
- The crew brings Cassie up out of the shaft. Her cross-index, laid over the Concord Engine schematic (Ch9), lights the **two remaining scattered archives**: one wrecked in a leviathan bone-canyon, far older than the rest, and one wired deep into a Dominion cryo-command vault.
- **Beat:** the rebellion now has a target list — two more nodes to gather before the build-record is whole. Predators have become protectors with a *plan.*
- **Hook out:** the nearer node lies in a leviathan bone-canyon, older than the Dominion and tied in the records to the Program's very origin — and it reads *wrong* on Sable's map, a sister she may not be able to save (Ch11).

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub briefing — Sable's map points to the first scattered archive in the Ninefold shafts; descend.* (1) Descend the nine shafts (escalating environmental hazards). (2) Reach Cassie-04; defeat her keeper **Sever / Ninja-2** (boss) to free the node and **gain Phase-step**; recruit Cassie (Ally #7). (3) Survive/resolve Vess (the mercy-mirror: *she* spares *you*). (4) Cross-index Cassie's lattice + Concord Engine schematic → light the two remaining nodes (Ch11, Ch12).
- **Environment:** `[CAVE]` — nine-tier vertical mine (collapsing shafts, lift-puzzles, deeper = older tech).
- **Encounters:** mine automata & syndicate guards; **boss: Sever / Ninja-2** (Cassie's leashed keeper — lethal, frees **Phase-step**); **boss/duel: Vess** (a fight that converts to alliance — non-lethal resolution is canon).
- **Ally unlock:** **Cassie-04 (#7)** and **Vess (#8)**.
- **Gameplay hooks:** the **Ledger** (Cassie's lattice) becomes a hub system — a searchable list of erased operatives that unlocks rescue missions, side-stories, and name-recovery (foreshadows Soren, Ch16); Cassie-04 adds galaxy-map navigation. **Phase-step** as a new traversal/combat unlock (blink through matter and enemies); Sable's map now tracks the two remaining living-archive nodes as meta-objectives.
