# CHAPTER 4 — THE OVERSEER'S HUNT
**Act I · Mercy as Rebellion · Episodes EP06–EP07–EP08**
**Logline:** The handler hunting Ronin-7 finally has a name — Khall — and he speaks the operative's own codename, Cipher, like a leash he still believes will hold; the proof that his killswitch was deliberately sabotaged comes wrapped in the one act no malfunction could fake: mercy, freely given.

---

## SETTING — DROVIS, THE FREE-PORT

A lawless syndicate free-port built down the walls of a canyon packed with grounded, cannibalized shipwrecks — dead capital hulls welded into tenements, salvage stacked into a whole city. Run by **the Coil**, one of the Ten Syndicates. Drovis is *neutral ground*: the Dominion keeps no garrison here, which is exactly why a Program courier on the run comes to sell and vanish — and why Khall must outsource his hunt to a recovery bounty the Coil is glad to collect. The hunt is no longer a chase through transit lanes; it is the whole city, scanning every face.

**The chapter moves through five guided segments:**
1. **The Throat** *(descent/dock canyon)* — landing & setup.
2. **The Sink** *(black-market bazaar)* — Tessa Rin / the sabotage.
3. **The Mast** *(hijacked relay spire)* — Khall / the naming.
4. **The Deepworks** *(flooded cave network beneath the city)* — parkour traversal to Kerrax's bolt-hole.
5. **Kerrax's Hold** *(the Coil's keep, deep in the caves)* — boss, mercy, Mera defects; Act I closes.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — command room)*
- Acting on Echo's charge to *find the others* and the still-open question of *why* his killswitch slipped, the crew tracks a lead to **Drovis**, the Coil's lawless free-port: a Program courier, **Tessa Rin**, is there trying to sell Program internals before she vanishes. If any records can name the hand that broke his leash, it is the data she is carrying.
- Resh knows the kind of port Drovis is — neutral ground, no Dominion garrison, syndicate-run — which cuts both ways: the Dominion can't send troopers, but the Coil sells anything, including a wanted face.
- **Interaction:** the crew is four and a child now, still learning each other. Resh reads the job like a smuggler; Iris weighs the data; Kessler counts the risk to Mira and orders her to stay sealed aboard; Echo, in Ronin-7's ear alone, flags that walking a Program weapon into a syndicate market is its own kind of bait.
- **Beat:** they go in for a courier and a question, not yet knowing the city already has Ronin-7's face on every slate. The shuttle drops toward the wreck-canyon. *(Hands into the grounding at The Throat.)*

### Scene 2 — Grounding at Drovis *(The Throat — docks)*
- The crew grounds the boarding shuttle in the wreck-canyon. Establish Drovis, the Coil, and the **ambient manhunt**: Cipher's recovery notice is live, scan-drones drift the docks, Coil enforcers check faces. Echo flags the heat.
- A tracker clocks Ronin-7 from the crowd and slips away — the first, unnamed glimpse of **Mera Voss** (seeded here, paid off Scene 5).
- **Beat:** the hunt is everywhere and impersonal; the city itself is the net now.

### Scene 3 — Tessa Rin *(The Sink — black-market bazaar)*
- The crew reaches courier **Tessa Rin** in the Sink, where she's trying to sell Program internals and disappear — but a Coil snatch-team is closing on her too. Reach and extract her first. **Iris decrypts:** Ronin-7's killswitch didn't randomly fail — it was **deliberately sabotaged.** Someone inside the Program reached in and broke his leash on purpose. **(Ladder A, rung 2.)**
- Tessa hands off the data and goes to ground (a one-chapter character, not an ally).
- **Beat:** he wasn't lucky. He was *chosen* — by an unknown hand. (Who? lands Ch13.)

### Scene 4 — The Name *(The Mast — hijacked relay spire)* — REVEAL
- The bounty is coordinated through a Coil-tapped **Overseer relay** crowning the Mast. Ronin-7 reaches it and the signal answers: the handler's identity — **Khall** — who addresses him by his Program operative codename, **Cipher** — the name the crew now hears for the first time and adopts thereafter. **(Ladder D, rung 1.)**
- Khall speaks directly — clinical, almost grieving. He wants Cipher brought in, not destroyed. The reason is hidden, but the *guilt* in his voice is audible (seed for Ch8).

### Scene 5 — The Deepworks *(flooded cave network beneath the canyon)* — TRAVERSAL
- The relay trace pins Kerrax to a bolt-hole sunk deep in the **Deepworks**, the mine-and-cave network the free-port was built over. There is no clean route in: the crew descends and Cipher crosses it on foot — wall-runs, ledge-jumps, collapsing footing, drops over dark water — working toward Kerrax's Hold.
- Light ambient threat (Coil lookouts, environmental hazards) rather than a full battle; a movement and tension beat that earns the boss. Echo reads the cave and calls the routes.
- **Beat:** the city's net thinned the deeper he went; down here it's just him, the dark, and the man he came for.

### Scene 6 — The Kerrax Mercy *(Kerrax's Hold — deep in the caves)* — REVEAL + ALLY #2
- The traverse ends at **Kerrax**, the Coil boss holding the leverage and selling Cipher to Khall. He is beaten and at Ronin-7's mercy. The Program-conditioned answer is to kill. Ronin-7 *spares* him — a deliberate, sober choice, not a glitch.
- Watching from cover is **Mera Voss**, the tracker from Scene 1 — an ex-Dominion hunter sent to confirm the kill. The mercy shatters her belief that operatives are unfeeling machines. She defects. **Ally #2.**
- **Beat:** the mercy proves the conscience-fracture is **genuinely his own** — chosen, repeatable, real. **(Ladder B, rung 3.)** **Closes ACT I.**

## KEY SCENES (screenplay)

**INT. THE CAIRN — COMMAND ROOM**

> RESH spreads a port-map of DROVIS across a salvaged holo-plate. IRIS reads a courier intercept. KESSLER stands with a hand on MIRA's shoulder. RONIN-7 listens at the edge of the light, the wrapped katana at his side.
>
> **RESH**
> Drovis. Coil free-port, built down a canyon of dead ships. No Dominion badges, which is the only reason a Program courier would run there to sell and vanish.
>
> **IRIS**
> Tessa Rin. She's carrying Program internals. If anything names who cut your leash, it's in what she's selling.
>
> **KESSLER**
> Neutral ground cuts both ways. They can't send soldiers after you. Doesn't mean they won't sell you.
>
> **ECHO (V.O.)** *(to Ronin-7 alone)*
> Walking you into a market that buys faces. What could go wrong. I'll watch the exits, Cipher. Somebody has to.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> Then we reach her first. Take us down.

**EXT. THE THROAT — DROVIS DOCKS**

> The shuttle's ramp drops into noise and smoke. A SCAN-DRONE drifts over the crowd, throwing a pale grid across passing faces. A Coil enforcer thumbs a slate showing RONIN-7's recovery notice.
>
> **ECHO (V.O.)**
> That's your face on his screen. And the one beside him. And the drone. Welcome to Drovis.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> *(low, into the crowd)* Then we keep moving and we don't look up.
>
> *A figure at the rail across the dock watches him a beat too long, then turns and is gone. RONIN-7 marks it. Says nothing.*

**INT. THE MAST — RELAY CHAMBER**

> A flat, omnidirectional voice fills the chamber. KHALL.
>
> **KHALL (V.O.)**
> Cipher. You are listed terminated. You are making that a difficult fiction to maintain.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> That's not my name.
>
> **KHALL (V.O.)**
> It's the one I gave you. *(a pause too long for a machine)* I am your Overseer. My name is Khall. I am not trying to kill you, Cipher. I am trying to bring you home before someone less... patient finds you first.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> Home doesn't put a switch in your skull.
>
> **KHALL (V.O.)** *(quietly)*
> No. It doesn't.

**INT. THE DEEPWORKS — A LEDGE OVER BLACK WATER**

> RONIN-7 runs a collapsing ledge, drops, catches a rusted spar, swings to the far wall. Below, the water gives nothing back.
>
> **ECHO (V.O.)**
> Gap's too wide on the right. Take the pipe, then the wall. Trust me, I can see the whole throat of it.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> *(already moving)* Then talk me down.
>
> **ECHO (V.O.)**
> Down is the easy part. He's at the bottom.

**INT. KERRAX'S HOLD — AFTERMATH**

> RONIN-7 stands over KERRAX, blade lowered. MERA VOSS watches from cover, weapon half-raised.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> Tell them what you saw.
>
> **KERRAX**
> They'll say you malfunctioned.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> *(sheathing the blade)* Then I'll keep malfunctioning.
>
> *MERA steps out, lowers her gun.*
>
> **MERA VOSS**
> A machine doesn't choose twice. *(beat)* I've been hunting the wrong thing.

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub briefing — track the courier lead to Drovis; deploy.* (1) Ground at Drovis and move through the manhunt. (2) Reach Tessa Rin in the Sink → decrypt the sabotage. (3) Reach the Mast relay → learn the handler's name (Khall) and hear his own codename, Cipher. (4) Descend and traverse the Deepworks caves to reach Kerrax's bolt-hole. (5) Defeat Kerrax → choose mercy → recruit Mera.
- **Environment:** five guided segments of one free-port — The Throat (wreck-canyon docks), The Sink (black-market stacks), The Mast (relay spire), the Deepworks (flooded cave network), Kerrax's Hold (syndicate keep).
- **Ambient-manhunt mechanic:** a **heat/wanted meter** rising with scan-drone and patrol exposure; blend with crowds or break line-of-sight to bleed it down; maxed heat triggers Coil enforcer ambush waves. This replaces the cut chase as the chapter's recurring pressure across the city segments and carries the chapter's title.
- **Parkour-traversal segment (Deepworks):** a movement and platforming beat — wall-runs, ledge-jumps, collapsing footing, drops over water — pacing the lull between the relay reveal and the boss. Echo calls routes; light ambient threat, no full combat. Showcases Cipher's operative agility.
- **Encounters:** Coil enforcer patrols + Dominion scan-drones (ambient); Sink snatch-team skirmish; Deepworks lookouts and environmental hazards (light); **boss: Kerrax** (mercy/kill choice is mechanically meaningful — sparing him recruits Mera and shifts later faction reactions).
- **Ally unlock:** **Mera Voss (#2)** — adds tracking/scouting kit and Dominion intel. On return she berths aboard the Cairn; every recruited ally lives on the Hub.
- **Gameplay hooks:** the **mercy-vs-kill** system is formalized here (it began with Resh); the **heat/manhunt** system debuts; **Khall (the Overseer)** becomes the hunting antagonist driving Act I's finale and reappears via Ch8 dead-channel logs.
