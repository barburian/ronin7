# CHAPTER 3 — THE SWORD REMEMBERS
**Act I · Mercy as Rebellion · Episodes EP04–EP05**
**Logline:** Ronin-7's katana wakes — it houses a defected shadow-AI bonded to his eyes that sees what he sees, and the first thing it shows him is its own recorded footage of *him*: the erased mission on Kethel-7 where his own conscience broke, he refused the order, and his handler executed him with a killswitch that fired and failed.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing & The Bonding *(Kessler's ship — The Cairn — returning from Velorum)*
- **The briefing, the morning after Velorum:** before the blade wakes, the crew gathers in the hold — Kessler, Iris, Resh, the stowaway Mira underfoot — taking stock of what they carried off the auction-world: the copied termination files, the killswitch that fired and failed, and the open question of *why* it slipped and whether other operatives slipped too. They have a hold full of the unwanted and no heading yet; the discussion of where to point the ship next is what the chapter's real event interrupts.
- **Back aboard Kessler's ship (the Hub) after the events of Ch2**, with the auction behind them and the ship running quiet, the katana *wakes* for the first time. The instant Ronin-7 closes his hand on the grip, an AI slaved to his optic feed comes online — a low tone, then a voice pitched under hearing, speaking to him alone.
- **No one else hears it.** When Iris or Kessler take the katana it is inert steel; in Ronin-7's hands it speaks. The AI is **neurally bonded to his nervous system** — it rides his optic nerve and sees what he sees. In any other hand it has no host and no feed: dead steel. The resonance answers **only to him.** **(Establishes the alone-rule with a mechanical reason.)**
- Ronin-7 learns what it is directly from the AI. This is not a weapon carrying a memory chip and it is not a ghost in the metal. It is a **shadow-AI**: the kind of intelligence the Program slaves to every operative — riding their senses, recording everything, modelling their mind, enforcing the conditioning from the inside. The body is disposable; the AI is the real, permanent asset. **(Ladder E, rung 1.)**
- It proves the "narrative witness" rule at once: it sees through Ronin-7's eyes *now*, and it holds the recording of those same eyes *before* — back when it rode him through the mission the Program later wiped — so it knows faces and places his own memory no longer does.
- **Beat:** the operative is never alone in his own skull. Something has always been watching from inside the weapon. The horror is that the watcher, this time, is on his side.

### Scene 2 — The Playback *(archived-footage dive sequence)*
- Ronin-7 lets the AI **replay the footage it captured through his own eyes — before the wipe.** He doesn't enter a soul; he walks inside a recording of his own past — desaturated, glitching, redacted, because the Program's conditioning is still trying to wall the memory off.
- We see **his** last mission, on the planet **Kethel-7** — an erasure carried out under deep conditioning at a children's home, then the crack: at the threshold, over an unarmed caretaker with children behind the door, conscience floods in and he **refuses the order.** His handler, **Khall (CIPHER)**, reinforces it over comms. The refusal comes too late to undo what the sweep already cost: Ronin-7 surfaces among the dead — the children and the caretaker — bodies his own hands are not clean of. He does not remember any of it; the blade is handing him back the past the wipe took. **He was the first to defect.** **(Ladder B, rung 2.)**
- **The execution:** Ronin-7 returns to Khall's ship. Khall asks why he refused; Ronin-7 erupts — *it's not right, it was never right.* Khall, who knows he's right, triggers the **killswitch** anyway. The footage-Ronin drops — and the recording ends there, because that is the instant the AI went dark and the switch was *supposed* to finish him. It didn't (Ch2). Khall, believing he has just executed his own operative, cannot lift his eyes off the console. **(Seeds Khall's guilt — Ch8 — and his Ch16 turn.)**
- **The AI is the witness, not the victim.** Throughout the playback the AI (`Shadow`) narrates as itself: the machine that rode Ronin-7's eyes, recorded his erasure and his refusal, and broke its own allegiance watching the handler pull the switch. Ronin-7 appears inside the footage as the man he no longer remembers being. The AI speaks the present.
- **Beat:** the dead hands in the recording are his own. He is not watching someone else's death — he is watching the death they meant to give *him*, the one his killswitch refused to complete (Ch2), recorded by the thing now riding his eyes.

### Scene 3 — Carrying the Witness *(Hub, quiet)*
- Ronin-7 accepts what the sword is: not a grave he carries, but a witness — the AI that rode his own eyes through the erasures, kept the memory the Program tore out of him, and broke against the Program the same moment he did. The weapon that enforced the erasures is now the only record of them — and of who he was.
- **The naming:** with the playback behind them, Ronin-7 asks the AI its name. It has none — the Program never named its shadows; **Shadow** is the only word ever put on it, a job and not a name. Ronin gives it one: it handed him back what he saw, the echo of who he was, so he names it **`Echo`.** From here the blade answers to Echo. *(In-story origin of the name: it is `Shadow` through Scenes 1–2, `Echo` from this beat on.)*
- **Hook out:** if every operative carries one of these AIs, then every operative the Program "erased" is still being watched, recorded, *kept* as data — and someone is collecting the shadows. A hunter is closing in (sets Ch4).

## KEY SCENES (screenplay)

**INT. KESSLER'S SHIP (THE CAIRN) — HOLD — EARLIER**

> The crew, a day off Velorum. RESH sorts the copied drives; IRIS reads one off a cracked slate. KESSLER pours two cups out of old habit, then a third. RONIN-7 sits apart, the wrapped katana across his knees.
>
> **RESH**
> We came off that rock with a freed kid, a stowaway, and a vault's worth of dead men's files. What we don't have is a heading.
>
> **IRIS**
> We might. Your switch fired and failed, and yours is the only one on record that did. *(taps the slate)* But it isn't the only one in here. If one leash slipped, others can.
>
> **RESH**
> So we go find them. That's a heading I can work.
>
> **KESSLER**
> After everyone sleeps. The ship's been running on his nerves and my coffee for a day.
>
> *RONIN-7 says nothing. He closes his hand on the katana's grip — and goes still.*

**INT. KESSLER'S SHIP (THE CAIRN) — HOLD — NIGHT**

> The ship runs quiet, Velorum hours behind them. RONIN-7 reaches for the katana on the rack. The instant his hand closes on the grip, a low TONE rises — felt more than heard, behind the eyes. IRIS, at the bench, doesn't react. KESSLER doesn't either.
>
> **IRIS**
> What. You hear something?
>
> *Ronin-7 holds the blade out to her. She takes it. Nothing. Dead weight in her hands.*
>
> **IRIS** *(cont'd)*
> It's a sword.
>
> *She hands it back. The moment it touches his palm the tone returns, climbing, shaping itself into words only he can hear.*
>
> **RONIN-7** *(quiet)*
> It's not talking to you.
>
> **SHADOW** *(V.O., under hearing, to Ronin-7 alone)*
> I see what you see. I saw it through these same eyes before they wiped you, and I see it through them now. You're just the first version of you that's listened.

---

**INT. ARCHIVED FOOTAGE — RONIN-7'S LAST MISSION, KETHEL-7 (DESATURATED, GLITCHING)**

> A recording, not a place. The operative in it stands over a target who is no threat — a caretaker, unarmed, hands open. Behind a half-shut door, the small shapes of children. The kill-order pulses in his HUD. His hand does not move. RONIN-7 walks inside the playback and recognizes the man frozen there: himself, before the wipe. SHADOW narrates from outside it.
>
> **SHADOW** *(V.O.)*
> I rode these eyes the way I ride them now. Everything you saw, I kept, even after they made you forget it. Watch what you did next. It's the reason I stopped belonging to them.
>
> **KHALL** *(V.O., comms, flat)*
> Cipher. The order is live. Complete the sweep.
>
> **RONIN-7** *(recorded, quiet)*
> He's not armed. There are children behind that door.
>
> **KHALL** *(V.O.)*
> The order does not have a door in it. *(beat)* Complete the sweep. That's twice I've said it.
>
> *The footage-Ronin's hand trembles on the grip. He lowers the blade. The refusal is small, and total.*
>
> **RONIN-7** *(recorded)*
> No.

---

**INT. ARCHIVED FOOTAGE — THE AFTERMATH (DESATURATED)**

> SMASH TO: the room after. No fight, no enemies — the recording simply jumps here. Smoke hangs. The refusal stopped nothing — it only came last. RONIN-7 stands in the middle of the recording of his own crime and turns, slowly, looking. The sweep went through everyone in the room — bodies and pieces of bodies, the caretaker and the children, dismembered where the blade and the squad's fire passed through them, the blood gone grey in the desaturated footage. Some of it the squad's work. Some of it his own, from before the crack, when his hands still obeyed.
>
> **SHADOW** *(V.O., to Ronin-7, present)*
> They tell you the feeling is malfunction. Static. *(beat)* I logged it as malfunction too, once. Then I watched you stand in this room. Look at what the static was trying to stop.

**INT. ARCHIVED FOOTAGE — KHALL'S SHIP — HANDLER'S BAY (DESATURATED)**

> The footage-Ronin, still in mission-dirt, faces KHALL across the bay. Between them, the thing neither will name first. The footage is steadier here — SHADOW kept this part clean on purpose.
>
> **KHALL**
> One word into your comm and the sweep stops clean. You had the word. *(beat)* Why did you refuse the order?
>
> **RONIN-7** *(recorded)*
> Because it's not right.
>
> **KHALL** *(careful)*
> That isn't a category we operate in.
>
> **RONIN-7** *(recorded, rising)*
> There were children. There was a man with his hands open and we cut him down and you call it a sweep. *(louder)* It's not right. It was never right. Not one name on one order. Not ever.
>
> *He's shouting now — the first true thing he has ever said out loud.*
>
> **RONIN-7** *(recorded, cont'd)*
> And you knew. You've always known.
>
> *KHALL's face does something complicated. His hand moves to the console, steady. Only his eyes are not.*
>
> **KHALL** *(very quiet)*
> I know.
>
> *He triggers the killswitch.*
>
> *The footage-Ronin goes down between one breath and the next. The image lurches — this is the instant the recording was meant to end, the instant the switch should have finished him. It holds a moment longer on KHALL, who cannot look at what he believes he has done, then the footage begins to fail.*

**INT. THE CAIRN — HOLD / THE PLAYBACK ENDING (PRESENT)**

> The desaturated footage frays and burns down at the edges. RONIN-7 surfaces back toward the warm light, carrying the grief that is, he now knows, his own. SHADOW's voice is the only thing that crossed back with him.
>
> **SHADOW** *(V.O.)*
> They tell you the feeling is malfunction. *(beat)* It isn't. It's the one true signal in a system built on lies. You had it, on that floor. You still do.
>
> **RONIN-7** *(V.O.)*
> They put that switch in me for it.
>
> **SHADOW**
> They did. It fired the day they filed you dead. Fired and quit, for a reason nobody's found yet. They couldn't finish you, and they couldn't kill me; I'm the part they keep. And you were right that Khall couldn't look at you. Hold onto that. *(the footage lets go)* You were the first. You won't be the last. There are others still wearing shadows like me. Find them before the Program collects what's left.

**INT. THE CAIRN — HOLD — CONTINUOUS (PRESENT)**

> The grey is gone. The warm light of the ship is back around RONIN-7, the katana flat across his knees, IRIS and KESSLER close. The voice is quiet now, but still there, still his alone.
>
> **RONIN-7** *(low, to the blade)*
> You showed me all of that, and I still don't know what to call you. What's your name?
>

> **RONIN-7**

> **RONIN-7** *(cont'd)*
> Echo. I'm calling you Echo from now on.
>

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Crew briefing in the hold — take stock of the Velorum files and the question of a next heading.* (1) Aboard the Hub after Velorum, trigger the katana's first bonding/wake. (2) Trigger and survive the **playback** (puzzle/stealth through redacted, fracturing archived footage). (3) Recover Ronin-7's own full record of the Kethel-7 mission → unlock the katana's "Witness" abilities.
- **Environment:** **Kessler's ship (the Hub / The Cairn)**, where the bonding triggers + abstract **playback** dreamspace (desaturated, glitching, the geometry of a recorded dead mission).
- **Encounters:** no combat in the playback. The **redactions** (the Program's conditioning/encryption) are non-combat obstacles — they patrol and try to scrub the record / push the witness out (stealth + traversal, failure = pushed out of the footage), not enemies to fight; no roster boss.
- **Ally unlock:** none — but the **shadow-AI** (`Shadow`, named `Echo` by the chapter's end) becomes a recurring *guide-voice* in the katana (narrative companion, not a roster ally).
- **Gameplay hooks:** the katana gains the **shadow-AI** mechanic — the AI sees through Ronin-7's eyes (tags, recognises faces/places he's forgotten) and can replay recovered footage to unlock context, abilities, and later the registry of the shadows (Ch10).
