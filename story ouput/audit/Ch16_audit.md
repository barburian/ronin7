## 4. Summary — Biggest Recurring Problems

1. **The antithesis reflex is the chapter's structural tic.** "Not X, it's Y" / "It was never A. It was B" appears roughly 28 times and in nearly every speaker. A handful are canon-protected climax lines and earn their symmetry; the rest pile up until the device stops reading as emphasis and starts reading as a writing pattern. The same three-part lattice frame ("not dead, not stored, they're awake/wired") is recycled for the kept shadows in Beats 0, 5, and 9.

2. **Aphorism-per-line / shared elevated register.** Almost every major line resolves into a quotable maxim, and Coral, Vess, Soren, Khall, Cassie, Sable, and Echo all do it in the same balanced cadence. Differentiation is carried by metaphor domain, not by rhythm or diction, so a finale that should feel like ten distinct people often sounds like one very eloquent narrator distributed across ten mouths. The script's own best lines prove the alternative works: Samurai-4's fractured post-break speech and Iris's flat "I've got nothing" read as people because they refuse the polish.

3. **The theme is spoken aloud.** The Story Bible's logline — "Mercy was the rebellion. Choice is the victory." — is recited verbatim by Echo in the final beat, and Soren repeatedly announces the thesis ("That's the victory," "I'd like to spend the rest of it choosing"). The chapter has *already* dramatized all of this through the empty throne and the released lattice; stating it on the nose double-charges the audience for an image that was landing on its own.

**Credit where due:** the em-dash ban is honored without exception, false-starts and interruptions are present in the right places, contractions are natural for the crew, and the deliberately stiff, contraction-free register for Maelgorn and (pre-break) Samurai-4 is effective characterization. Canon discipline is strong — the shadow-AI/memory-core distinction, codenames, Kethel-7, the template reveal, and all three closing ladders are handled correctly. The one substantive consistency item to resolve is **Samurai-4's "newer make"** framing against Ronin being the canon current make.
