# Ch07 — "Forgotten Names" — Dialogue Audit

**Chapter:** Ronin-7, Chapter 7: Forgotten Names (Dialogue + Voice Direction Script)
**Scope:** Audit only. No script edits made.
**Overall naturalness grade: C**

The prose is genuinely strong, characterful, and the voices are well-differentiated (Morrigan dry/technical, Mera cold-economical, Coral reverent-weary, Echo warm-but-uneasy, Ronin terse). But by the strict naturalness rubric it leans hard on one construction: the **"not X, it's Y" antithesis**, which fires roughly fourteen times across five different speakers. Combined with aphorism-stacking (nearly every Coral line lands on a quotable maxim) and a repeated "records / paper / relics" triad used as a verbal motif, the chapter reads polished in the way AI dialogue reads polished. Consistency, by contrast, is excellent: it tracks the recon docs closely.

## Summary — biggest recurring problems

1. **The "not X, it's Y" antithesis is a house tic, not a voice.** It appears ~14 times across Morrigan, Iris, Mera, Echo, and Coral. Because five distinct characters all reach for the same rhetorical shape, the cast stops sounding like separate people and starts sounding like one author's reflex. This is the chapter's single largest naturalness liability and the easiest to fix by varying half the instances.

2. **Aphorism-stacking, especially on Coral.** Nearly every Coral line closes on a polished, quotable maxim ("waste is the one thing it never throws away," "the truth keeps thieves away better than any lock," "a debt I'll die owing," "the draft before the draft"). Individually each is good; in sequence they make her speak in epigrams rather than in a tired woman's voice. Thin them so the big lines land harder.

3. **The "records / paper / relics / files" triad is overused.** The same three-item dismissal recurs in Beats 0, 2, and 4, turning a deliberate motif into a verbal tic. Vary the wording on at least one repetition.

Minor: a few on-the-nose lines narrate emotion or state the scene's meaning aloud (Echo's "I have never been anywhere that felt like this"; Echo's "you're a thing somebody chose to free"). Trust the subtext.

**Consistency is a strength** — the chapter is one of the cleaner alignments to the recon docs, with the Cipher-codename handling and the kept-shadows / Wraith-forebear / internal-dissent reveals all on-canon.
