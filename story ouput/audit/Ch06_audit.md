## 4. Summary — biggest recurring problems

1. **Aphorism saturation.** Nearly every line is engineered to be quotable, and the dominant device is the antithesis "It's not X, it's Y" / "not a fault, it was the seam" (~13 instances). One or two land; thirteen make the dialogue feel like a wall of fortune cookies. The reveal (Beat 4) stacks four maxim-shaped lines in a row, which dilutes the single line that should hit ("Hollow is the product"). Cut the surrounding maxims so the load-bearing one stands alone.

2. **Uniform register.** A six-year-old, a smuggler, a parade-ground brute, a burnt-out engineer, an AI, and a stoic operative all speak with the same balanced cadence, the same tricolons, the same tidy reversals. The chapter has no vocal fingerprints. This is the single highest-leverage fix: rough up Caradoc, clip Morrigan, simplify Mira, and let Echo's "wry warmth" actually sound different from Ronin's flatness.

3. **The "hand on the strings" bleed** at the close: three different speakers reach for the identical metaphor in 40 lines, exposing the author's hand. Vary it.

Consistency is strong — the chapter sits cleanly on canon (Cipher usage, the Kaelen reveal, the template, Morrigan as Ally #3, the briefing structure). The only real watch-item is scoping Morrigan's "one architect / all of it" Heris-seed to the Ronin line so it doesn't read as "one mind built every make," which the current retcon forbids.
