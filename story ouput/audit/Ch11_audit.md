## 1. Header

- **Chapter:** Ronin-7 — Chapter 11: Ghosts and Origins
- **Overall naturalness grade: C+**
  Genuinely skilled, well-characterized writing with strong, distinct voices (Aldric's broken-ancient cadence, Cassie's gallows-clerk, Vess's flint). But it leans on one AI-tell so heavily it becomes the chapter's signature rhythm: the **"not X, it's Y" antithesis** appears ~18 times, in nearly every speaker's mouth, which flattens the character distinctiveness the voice notes promise. Add dense aphorism-stacking in the monologues and two reveal-dump speeches, and the polish reads machine-even rather than human-uneven.

## 4. Summary — biggest recurring problems

The chapter's writing is strong on character and atmosphere, but three habits drag it toward the AI-even register:

1. **The "not X, it's Y" antithesis is the chapter's reflex.** It fires ~18 times across almost every speaker (Cassie "not a chapter, the first page"; Sable "not air, it's her"; Echo "not cliffs, they're ribs," "not raiding a vault, standing at the root," "not strangers, your ancestors"; Coral "not an exception, an edition"; Cassie again "not a dig, a race"). Used once it's a punch; used by everyone it becomes a tic that erases the vocal distinctiveness the voice-notes carefully describe. This is the single highest-leverage fix — de-symmetrize roughly two-thirds of them.

2. **Aphorism-stacking in the monologues.** The keeper-kill speeches (Ronin at 372, Echo at 384/419) and the briefing (Cassie 218) tend to make *every* sentence a polished maxim ("the first one gets to be the last one to put it down," "give instead of keep," "they're family coming home"). Real grief stumbles; let one or two lines be plain so the quotable ones land.

3. **Reveal delivered as lecture.** Echo's lineage block (413) and Coral's origin-of-the-Program speech (266) tell what the production notes already plan to *show* (the lineage-tree, the architecture). Both are canonically correct but over-explained; trim and lean on the diegetic visuals.

Mechanically clean elsewhere: zero em-dashes in dialogue, contractions used naturally (Aldric's missing contractions are an intentional ancient cadence, correctly), and **zero canon-consistency violations** — codenames, lineage, ability chain, sword-as-AI, Kethel-7, Coral's Wraith make, and the deferred Ch12 "copy" inversion are all handled correctly.
