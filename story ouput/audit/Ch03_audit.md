## 4. Summary — the chapter's biggest recurring problems

The script is strong and clearly written by someone with an ear: contractions throughout, real fragments and false-starts ("Easy. Easy."), Kessler's gallows-warm voice is pitch-perfect, and the hard global rule (no em-dashes in speech) is fully respected. The consistency hygiene is excellent — every load-bearing canon fact (shadow-AI, Kethel-7-as-mission, failed killswitch, the playback being Ronin's *own* memory, the Shadow→Echo naming) lands exactly as the recons specify, with no hard violations.

The one persistent craft weakness is **antithesis addiction**: the "It's not X. It's Y." / "not X, it's Y" construction appears eight times, and it's reinforced by **aphorism-stacking** (characters speaking in portable, quotable maxims) and **motif over-repetition** ("it was the job," "keep the shadow / the watcher is what they keep," "they tell you the feeling is malfunction" each recur across beats). The cost isn't any single bad line — it's that the *cumulative* rhythm makes Echo and Ronin-7 converge into one mythic, maxim-trading register, undercutting the deliberate Echo-warm / Ronin-laconic contrast the SETTING and `00b` notes ask for. Two on-the-nose theme statements (most notably "I'm looking at the death I was supposed to have") say aloud what the imagery is already delivering. The fix is light and surgical: de-symmetrize roughly half the antitheses, cut one instance of each repeated motif, and pull the maxims out of Ronin's mouth in particular.
