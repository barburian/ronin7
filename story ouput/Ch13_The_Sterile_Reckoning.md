# CHAPTER 13 — THE STERILE RECKONING
**Act IV · Reckoning and Revelation**
**Logline:** Ronin-7 confronts Dr. Heris — the engineer who built both him and his killswitch, and the conscripted architect of the Concord Engine — and learns she planted the flaw that saved him on purpose; beside her waits Sallow, the body designed to absolve, the tenth and final ally.

---

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — The Sensing *(The Cairn — war-room holo-table)*
- Act IV opens the moment Cipher steps back aboard. He's barely off the cryo-vault (Ch12) with the last fragment when **Cassie** and **Sable** — the two recruited archive keeper-hosts, the map and the mouth of the scattered-archive server — both go still at once. They *feel* it: somewhere out past the lattice they spent the war tracing, there is **one archive larger than all four of them put together** — the four scattered nodes were never the machine, only its veins. This is the trunk.
- They name what it is: not a build-record, not a graveyard of shed shadows — a **transmitter.** The **Concord Engine** itself, racked and waiting, scaled to do to whole worlds what a killswitch does to one operative — the Dominion's ultimate instrument of **large-scale mind control.** Sable can feel its weight from here and can't feel a seam anywhere on it.
- **Interaction:** the room reckons with the size of it — Cassie shaken that everything they've freed so far was a rounding error against this; Coral and Sable, made by the Program's cruelty, sick that the cage has a galaxy-sized version; Kessler asking the practical question — you can't cut something you can't even find a weak point on. **Morrigan** gives the answer: you don't break a machine this big by force — you find the one person who could. Whoever planted the flaw in Cipher's killswitch did it from *inside* the suppression science. That same insider hand — the dissent thread she's tracked since the dojo (Ch7) — is the only mind that could know where a leash this size is weak. **Find the one who sabotaged the switch, and you find the Engine's seam.**
- **Beat:** the sensing converges with the trails the crew already had — the command-network (Ch12) and the internal-dissent traffic (Ch7) both terminate at the sterile lab of **Dr. Heris**, the Program's own architect. The hunt for the killswitch-planter and the hunt for the Engine's weakness are the same hunt. *(Hands into breaching the sterile vault.)*

### Scene 2 — The Sterile Vault *(Heris's white laboratory-sanctum)*
- The command-network (Ch12) and the dissenter trail (Ch7) converge on one location: the lab of **Dr. Heris**, the Program's own architect.
- **Beat:** after caves and bone and rust, a place of unbearable cleanliness — where people were *designed.*

### Scene 3 — The Maker *(lab core)* — REVEAL (closes Ladder A)
- Heris admits everything: she **built Ronin-7** — and she **built his killswitch.** And she **planted the flaw** in his suppression that let his conscience fracture and let the switch fail. The sabotage (Ch4), the internal dissent (Ch7) — it was *her.* **(Ladder A, rung 5 — the saboteur named.)**
- And she names the larger sin: the Dominion took her suppression science and conscripted her to scale it into the **Concord Engine** (Ch9) — the galaxy-silencing weapon. She built that too, under threat — and seeded it with the same kind of deniable flaw she put in him. She knows where its seam is.
- **Beat:** the hand that made the leash also made the gap in it — and hid a gap in the galaxy's leash as well. His freedom was *engineered* by a guilty maker who has been waiting to spend it.

### Scene 4 — Heris Defects *(lab, confrontation → alliance)* — ALLY #9
- Heris doesn't ask forgiveness; she asks to *finish it.* She designed the rebellion's keystone into him and has waited for him to come back and let her help tear the Concord Engine apart through the flaw she hid in it. **Ally #9.**
- **Beat:** the architect of the cage becomes the engineer of its destruction.

### Scene 5 — Sallow and the Absolution Body *(lab annex)* — REVEAL + ALLY #10 (TEN COMPLETE)
- Heris reveals **Sallow**: a construct-body built to **"absolve"** — designed to host the shadows, to be the body that bears every kept witness so the living don't have to. Ronin-7's own body was made on the same principle: *a thing designed to carry the shadows of the dead.*
- Sallow joins. **Ally #10 — the ten are complete.**
- **Beat:** the body that was made to erase was also, secretly, made to *remember and atone.*
- **Hook out:** with the maker, the ledger, and the Engine's weakness in hand, the crew turns to the finale — the descent into himself and the strike at the Engine (Ch16).

>
> **SABLE**

> So there's a version of the cage big enough for everyone.
>
> **KESSLER**

You find the one person who built it and left a flaw. *(beat)* Someone planted the gap in Cipher's killswitch from *inside* the suppression science. The insider I've tracked since the dojo. That's the only hand that could tell us where a leash this big is soft. *(turns to the holo)* Find who sabotaged the switch, and we find the seam in that thing.

> Bait or not, she has the answer. We go.

**INT. HERIS'S LAB — CORE**

> DR. HERIS faces RONIN-7 across a sterile table. No fear in her.
>
> **HERIS**
> Ask the question you came with.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> Who sabotaged my switch.
>
> **HERIS**
> I did. *(beat)* I built it. I built *you.* And when I understood what we were making, I put a flaw in the suppression — small, deniable. A seam. *(quietly)* I prayed it would open in one of you before I died. It opened in you.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> You made the leash *and* the gap.
>
> **HERIS**
> Then they made me build a bigger leash. *(beat)* The Concord Engine. One voice to quiet every world. I had no flaw small enough to hide in *you* and live — but I hid one in *that.* A seam, waiting. *(quietly)* I've held its location for years, hoping the man I broke open would come back for it.
>
> **RONIN-7**
> You made the cage. Both sizes.
>
> **HERIS**
> I made the cage. *(stands)* Let me help you unmake it. I've earned no forgiveness. I'm asking for *work.*

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub — Cassie & Sable sense the master-archive (the Concord Engine, scaled mind-control); Morrigan resolves to hunt the killswitch-planter for its weakness; the trails converge on Heris's lab; breach it.* (1) Reach and breach Heris's sterile vault. (2) Confront Heris → the killswitch-origin **and Concord Engine** reveal. (3) Recruit Heris (the maker). (4) Meet Sallow → recruit → **complete the ten.**
- **Environment:** stark white laboratory-sanctum (clinical horror; contrast with Acts II–III).
- **Encounters:** lab security & loyalist constructs; **mini-boss:** a Program enforcer sent to silence Heris (proves she's now a target too).
- **Ally unlock:** **Dr. Heris (#9)** and **Sallow (#10)** — the **final two**, completing the roster of ten.
- **Gameplay hooks:** Heris unlocks **Engine-sabotage tech** (the means to reach and break the Concord Engine seeded from Ch9 — keyed to the hidden seam she built into it); Sallow's "absolution" mechanic lets the player *carry/free* kept shadow-AIs at scale — the literal tool for the Ch16 liberation.
