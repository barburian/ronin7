# CHAPTER 6 — THE IRON DOJO
**Act II · Beneath the Erasure · Episodes EP11–EP12**
**Logline:** Resh names the place that built the thing Ronin-7 became — the Iron Dojo, a mountain citadel where the Dominion harvests children into the next generation of operatives — and inside its walls an informant named Morrigan hands Ronin-7 a kill-list of three masters, while one of them, dying, confirms the unspeakable: the hollowness was engineered on purpose, and his mercy is the flaw they always feared.

---

## SETTING — THE IRON DOJO, A MOUNTAIN CITADEL

A vast fortress-school carved across **three granite peaks** of a mountain spur, on a world with a **climate like old Earth**: breathable air, a real sun, weather that turns, pine forest cloaking the lower slopes, snow on the high ridges. The Dominion built it beautiful on purpose. Terraced training yards step down the mountainsides; aqueducts thread the rock; bells ring the hours over a central muster ground, **the Iron Yard.** The children raised here are meant to believe this is a home and a school, not the front end of a machine that erases them. It is the most monstrous thing in the saga precisely because it looks like the kindest.

Each peak is crowned by a **tower**, and each tower is a stage of the making. High bridges and a cable-tram span the gorges between them, over the central works where the citadel's reactors, archives, and the keeper-engineer's quarters are sunk into the mountain's spine.

**The chapter moves through guided segments, with a free-order core:**
1. **The Hub Briefing** *(The Cairn — command room)* — Resh's pitch; the informant is named.
2. **The Drop & the Climb** *(forest slope → mountain face)* — parkour traversal up to the informant.
3. **Morrigan's Room** *(the engineering spine)* — the kill-list; the citadel's true purpose; the three towers open.
4. **The Three Towers** *(player chooses the order)* — defenders and a master in each.
   - **The Cradle** *(Intake Tower)* — boss: **Matron Hespa.**
   - **The Proving** *(Trial Tower)* — boss: **Drillmaster Caradoc.**
   - **The Vesting** *(Graduation Tower)* — boss: **Master Kaelen** *(carries the reveal).*
5. **The Evacuation** *(return to Morrigan → the Iron Yard → exfil)* — the school emptied and destroyed; Ally #3 recruited.

## BEAT TREATMENT

### Scene 1 — Mission Briefing *(The Cairn — command room)*
- **Resh** brings the target himself: every child his railroad pulls out of the markets, he has learned, was bound for one address — the **Iron Dojo**, the mountain citadel where the Dominion grows and conditions the next generations of the Program. This is the source of the thing they have all been fighting downstream of.
- Resh has one card to play: a single contact *inside* the walls, a Program engineer who has been quietly feeding the railroad. A name and nothing else. **Morrigan.**
- **Interaction:** for once the smuggler is not reading a job, he is settling a debt he has carried for years; **Mera** warns that a Dominion training fortress is not a free-port, there is a real garrison and no neutral ground to hide in; **Iris** wants the citadel's tech, **Kessler** counts the risk and keeps **Mira** sealed aboard. **Echo,** for Cipher alone, is quiet in a way the others cannot hear: this is the building he was poured in.
- **Beat:** they set course not to learn what he was, but to break the place that keeps making him. *(Hands into the drop on the mountain.)*

### Scene 2 — The Drop & the Climb *(forest slope → mountain face)* — TRAVERSAL
- The boarding shuttle cannot near the towers; the citadel's air-defenses own the sky. It drops Cipher low and far, on the **forested lower slope** under a real, weathering sky, and pulls off. There is no gate to walk; he has to *go up* — and the only route to Morrigan that the garrison does not watch is the mountain itself.
- A movement-and-tension beat: wall-runs up cliff faces, ledge-jumps between aqueduct spans, climbs along the curtain wall, drops past patrol-lit terraces, weather and loose rock as hazard. **Echo** reads the rock and calls the routes, the same partnership that crossed the Drovis Deepworks (Ch4) — only now it is open air and a long fall instead of black water.
- **Beat:** the deeper Drovis went down, this goes *up;* by the time he reaches her window the garrison is below him and it is just the climber, the cold, and the woman waiting in the dark.

### Scene 3 — Morrigan's Room *(the engineering spine)* — THE KILL-LIST
- Cipher reaches **Morrigan** in her hidden quarters sunk into the central works. She is the keeper-engineer of this place and has been rotting inside it for years, building the leashes she hates and slipping children to Resh's railroad to keep her own conscience alive. She can read Program tech from the inside; she has been **gathering the citadel's intercepts** the whole time.
- She lays out the citadel the way only its engineer can: three towers, three stages, three masters. Cut off any one and the school staggers; cut off all three at once and it *falls,* with no one left to stop her emptying it. She gives him the names he has to kill: **Matron Hespa** of the Cradle, **Drillmaster Caradoc** of the Proving, **Master Kaelen** of the Vesting.
- **Beat:** he came to hit the Dominion; she hands him exactly where it bleeds. The three towers unlock and **the player chooses which to take first.**

### Scene 4 — The Three Towers *(player-chosen order)*
- **The Cradle (Intake Tower):** nurseries, conditioning cribs, the "unlearning" halls where the youngest are stripped of names and pasts. Defended by warden-nurses, conditioning-enforcers, and suppression drones. **Boss: Matron Hespa** — soft-voiced, certain she is kind. Ronin-7 kills her.
- **The Proving (Trial Tower):** the sparring pits, live-fire gauntlets, and climbing courses where adolescents are forged or discarded. Defended by the instructor-cadre and senior cadet packs. **Boss: Drillmaster Caradoc** — the brutal hand that does the breaking. Ronin-7 kills him.
- **The Vesting (Graduation Tower):** the final stage — shadow-AI bonding, killswitch implantation, the last hollowing before deployment. Defended by handler-cadre and *vested young operatives,* near-finished versions of what he is. **Boss: Master Kaelen** — the citadel's master, an architect who knows the design from the blueprint up.
- **The children are the rescue, never the target.** Cipher cuts through the adult cadre, the garrison, and the machines; he does not raise the blade to the trainees. *(Optional mercy beat available only against the vested cadets of the Vesting — the ones most like him.)*
- Ronin7 will do parkour trhough the castle fences and fight enemies so it can reach the entrance of each tower. 

### Scene 5 — Kaelen's Confession *(the Vesting — top of the tower)* — REVEAL
- Master **Kaelen** is the last master to fall, and dying he says the thing the whole Program is built to keep buried. They did not *fail* to give operatives feeling. They cut it out **on purpose** — hollow soldiers do not hesitate. But you cannot seal nothing in a vessel forever; somewhere the seam stays weak, and that weak seam is the **empathy** that surfaces in the rare few. Ronin-7's mercy is not damage done *to* him. It is the **flaw they designed in and prayed would never open.** **(Ladder B, rung 4.)**
- **Beat:** the man built to end lives is told, by the man who built men like him, that the one human thing in him is the thing his makers feared most. He is exactly what they made, and could not bear to admit they made.

### Scene 6 — The Evacuation *(return to Morrigan → the Iron Yard)* — ALLY #3 + first outside-hand hint
- All three masters dead, Cipher returns to **Morrigan.** With the towers leaderless she does what she has waited years to do: triggers a full **evacuation** and rigs the citadel to come down behind them. There is no carrying hundreds of children off a mountain by force, so the plan is the only one that respects them — the **eldest trainees lead the younger ones out,** down the forest routes, toward a normal life none of them were ever meant to have. Resh's name opens the doors at the bottom.
- Morrigan walks out with **Cipher,** not with the children: this war is upstream of one school, and she means to follow it to the end. **(Ally #3.)** Studying his sabotaged switch and the stack of intercepts she carries, she names two unease that will shape everything after: the Program's own actions do not fully explain events, *someone outside it is steering* **(first hint of outside manipulation — pays off Ch16: the Obsidian Synod)** — and the work she has been reverse-engineering all these years traces back to one **insider,** the Program's own architect, whom she will track from here **(seed — pays off Ch13: Dr. Heris).**
- **Hook out:** behind them the Iron Dojo burns and the children scatter free; ahead, a hand bigger than the Program, and a name she means to find.

**INT. THE CAIRN — COMMAND ROOM**

**INT. THE VESTING — TOP OF THE TOWER**

>
> **KAELEN**

> The bells are ringing wrong now, a long unbroken call. Lines of children move down the forest roads, the oldest at the front and the back, counting heads. Behind them the towers begin to come apart. MORRIGAN watches the youngest disappear into the treeline, then turns and walks toward the shuttle with RONIN-7.

> **RONIN-7**
> You're not going with them.
>
> **MORRIGAN**
> They get a normal life. I don't, not yet. *(she taps the stack of intercepts under her arm)* Because your leash didn't slip on its own, and the work in here doesn't trace to the Program. Someone outside it has a hand on the strings. *(beat)* And it all runs back to one insider. I'm going to find him.

## GAME NARRATIVE DESIGN
- **Objectives:** (0) *Hub briefing — Resh's target; deploy.* (1) Drop on the forest slope and **climb the mountain** to Morrigan. (2) Receive the kill-list; the three towers unlock. (3) **Clear the three towers in any order** — kill Hespa, Caradoc, and Kaelen, each defended by their cadre. (4) Hear Kaelen's dying reveal. (5) Return to Morrigan → evacuate and destroy the citadel → recruit Morrigan + receive the "outside hand" warning and the "insider" seed.
- **Environment:** an Earth-climate **mountain citadel** — forested lower slopes, terraced yards, curtain walls, aqueducts, a central Iron Yard, three peak-towers joined by bridges and a cable-tram, the engineering spine sunk in the mountain.
- **Parkour-traversal segment (the Climb):** an exterior vertical traversal — wall-runs, ledge-jumps between aqueduct spans, wall-crawls past patrols, weather/loose-rock hazards, long falls. Echo calls routes; light ambient threat, no full combat. The mirror-image of Ch4's *descent* through the Deepworks: this one goes up.
- **Free-order boss structure:** three self-contained towers the player tackles in any chosen order, each with themed defenders and a master:
  - **Cradle / Hespa** — warden-nurses, conditioning-enforcers, suppression drones.
  - **Proving / Caradoc** — instructor-cadre, senior cadet packs, gauntlet hazards.
  - **Vesting / Kaelen** — handler-cadre, vested young operatives; carries the reveal.
- **All three masters are killed** — no spare condition for the bosses. The chapter's **mercy is redirected to the children:** trainees are non-combatant rescue objectives, never targets. An *optional* spare beat exists only for the Vesting's vested cadets (the ones most like Ronin-7).
- **Ally unlock:** **Morrigan (#3)** — Program-tech reverse-engineering; unlocks killswitch and Concord Engine analysis used in Ch9 & Ch13. On return she berths aboard the Cairn; every recruited ally lives on the Hub.
- **Gameplay hooks:** vertical-parkour traversal showcase; choose-your-order multi-boss level; Morrigan's analysis bench seeds the Concord Engine investigation (Ch9), the Heris insider thread (Ch13), and the Obsidian Synod thread (Ch16).
