==================================================================
GRYPH
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — GRAVELKIN (cave-dwelling stoneblood). Non-Dominion; the pirate/hold-keeper species,
and Gryph is its definitive old-warrior.

ROLE & FACTION: Old warrior, pirate-captain of the Rustfang free-hold dug into the deep caves; held
it thirty years with a band of outcasts. Ally #5, earned by fighting a Coil raid at his shoulder
instead of robbing him. Names his second Rook to keep the hold and gives the crew the map to the
Concord Engine's construction node.

CHAPTERS: Ch9–Ch16 (joins after the hold-defense).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Old (gravelkin are long-lived; a lifetime of war on him); ~2.1 m. Massive,
craggy, boulder-built.

HEAD: Slate-to-basalt lithic hide, heavily mineralized with age — crystalline nodules crusting the
brow, jaw and skull; small deep-set glassy pale eyes under a heavy bony brow; tusk-like lower canines;
old hide-fractures (scars) re-mineralized across the face. Gravel-rough voice.

BODY: Immense, slow, immovably strong; "hands like cut stone"; nodules and crystal growths erupting
along knuckles, shoulders and spine; ancient chip-scars.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A heavy old war-sword (worn, enormous); salvaged hold-armor; a captain's deep-cave gear.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Scavenged heavy plate and iron over the hide, leather straps, a captain's worn
mantle/cloak; deep-hold practical, battered by thirty years.

COLOR PALETTE: Slate-grey/basalt hide with mineral glints; rust iron, dark leather, dead-cave tones.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A mountain of a craggy grey stone-skinned warrior with mineral
nodules and tusks, hauling a huge war-sword; the immovable old captain.

DEMEANOR: Gruff, direct, fiercely loyal to his people; earned only by deed. "You're the first who came
down with a blade and used it for my people instead of against them." (Ch9)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Big, old, scarred; hands like cut stone; heavy old war-sword; held the hold thirty years (Ch9).
