SALLOW   (the "absolution" construct body)
==================================================================
SPECIES: CONSTRUCT / NON-CORPOREAL — a synthetic vessel built by Dr. Heris. Program-derived tech, no
organic core; not human, not an alien species.

ROLE & FACTION: A failed "absolution" body / mercy-construct: the vessel built to HOST shadow-AIs —
to give a witness a body so the living don't have to carry the kept dead. Ronin-7's own body was made
on the same principle (a vessel meant to carry shadow-dead). Ally #10; completes the Ten; unlocks the
shadow-release mechanics for the Ch16 finale.

CHAPTERS: Ch13–Ch16 (introduced in Heris's lab).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: N/A (constructed); built to a human-operative template — ~1.8 m, humanoid, blank.

HEAD: A smooth, unfinished, blank-featured synthetic face — a vessel, not a person; pale, waxen,
sallow (its name) construct skin over a visible internal AI-host cradle; sockets/ports where a
shadow-AI seats; eyes dim and empty until a witness is poured in, then a soft host-glow behind them.

BODY: A clean humanoid host-shell built to contain witness — partially transparent or paneled at the
chest to show the empty AI-cradle/lattice meant to hold shadow-AIs; seams and ports along the spine
and skull (echoing the living archives, but sterile and unfilled). Silent, purposeful, slightly
unfinished.

WEAPONS / PROPS: None — it is the vessel itself; the open host-cradle, seating-ports, and (in Ch16)
streams of released shadow-light are its visual function.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Minimal — a sterile lab shroud / unfinished plating; the look of a thing still on
the workbench, made to be filled.

COLOR PALETTE: Sallow waxen white / pale grey construct skin; sterile lab-blue host-glow; clean steel
ports; no warmth until witness fills it.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A blank, waxen, port-seamed humanoid vessel with a visible empty
chest-cradle for shadow-AIs; a body that is a container — built to erase, secretly built to remember.

DEMEANOR: Silent, purposeful, uncanny; a machine that is also a mercy.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - "The vessel built to host a shadow-AI — to give a witness a body; completes the ten." (Bible §4 #10)
