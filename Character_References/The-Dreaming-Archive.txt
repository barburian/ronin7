THE DREAMING ARCHIVE   (broken Knight-generation living-archive node)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program operative weaponized as a living-archive node — human per rule, but
heavily wired/racked and far gone). Holds the KNIGHT generation's shadow-AIs — the Program's oldest,
most overloaded make.

ROLE & FACTION: The SECOND scattered living archive — a sister racked aboard the fossilized arkship in
the leviathan bone-canyon, strung into the hull as a node holding the origin (Knight-generation) section
of the Concord Engine's build-record. Racked so long, and so far gone, that her kept shadows LEAK
outward as the canyon's waking narcosis dreamscape — the "atmosphere" that shows Cipher his dead. She
cannot be brought up alive; after the record is read (by Echo alone, comm cut), Cipher gives her the
same door he gave the keeper and PUTS HER DOWN CLEAN — her death ends the broadcast, collapses the
dream, and floods the comm back. The unnamed, unsalvageable counterpart to Sable and Cassie.

CHAPTERS: Ch11 (the broken bone-canyon node; freed by death, not recruited).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Indeterminate under the hardware; reads ageless/worn; ~1.7 m. An operative frame
all but consumed by the racking grown into her — gaunter and further gone than Sable.

HEAD: A slack, dreaming face, eyes half-open and rolled to inward visions, never quite seeing the
living; data-light crawls thick under the skin of temples, throat and jaw. Hair shaved or lost at the
dense contact-points; the faces of her leaked shadows ghost faintly across her features.

BODY: Near-total RACKING — a heavy lattice of conditioning hardware, cabling and glowing conduits grown
deep into spine, ribs and limbs and fused into the arkship hull itself, so she is more node than person.
Barely moves; the dreamscape pulses out of her in slow waves, the canyon breathing with her.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Not a fighter (not a combat boss) — the leaking dreamscape is her only "function";
trailing hull-conduits and broadcast-tethers; archive-glyphs and ghost-faces surfacing on her skin and
in the air around her.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Almost none — the remains of an archive-shift dissolved into the racking and the hull;
salvage straps; the look of a person grown into a machine and never unplugged.

COLOR PALETTE: Ashen, bloodless grey skin; cold blue-white data-light veins guttering toward sickly dim;
black corroded hardware; pale dream-haze and faint amber archive-glyphs bleeding into the canyon air.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A human host half-fused into an ancient arkship hull, lattice
grown out of her spine, wreathed in a leaking dream-haze of ghost-faces; tragedy past saving made
visible — the sister too broken to bring up.

DEMEANOR: Lost to dreaming; no malice, barely a presence — a witness drowned in her own broadcast. The
mercy is the killing blow that ends her conscription; she is quieted, not defeated.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Sable, of her: "It's one of us. But it's wrong. She's been on the rack so long her shadows are
    leaking out of her. That's your canyon air, Cipher. It isn't air. It's her, showing you your
    dead." (Ch11)
