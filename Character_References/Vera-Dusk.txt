==================================================================
VERA DUSK
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — ASHKIN (frontier dust-world settler). Non-Dominion; the ash-world colonist species,
and Vera is its defining figure (sister of Kira Dusk).

ROLE & FACTION: Survivor of the ash-world settlement Ronin's line scoured; has kept vigil over her
14-year-old sister Kira's grave for ten years. Confronts Ronin with a ledger of the dead, demands his
life, then refuses to take it — sentencing him to live and pay it. Does not join; a possible later contact.

CHAPTERS: Ch5.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Late 20s; ~1.75 m. Lean, hardy ashkin frontier build; exhausted from the vigil.

HEAD: Ruddy ochre-to-terracotta cracked clay-like skin with raised dried-mud keratin plates across
the cheekbones; amber/ember eyes (faint inner glow) squinted against grit, double dust-lids; coarse
ash-grey hair literally caked with grey ash; grey ash settled in every skin-crease. Raw, grief-hardened face.

BODY: Standing-stone stillness over dangerous grief; weapon-hand steady even as it shakes; weathered,
dust-marked.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A worn settler's rifle/sidearm (kept steady at Ronin); the ledger of the dead
(Kira's name among them); grave-marker tools; a vigil-lantern.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Practical frontier survival wear — dust-cloak/wrap, layered rust-colored cloth,
sun-bleached leather, breath-scarf; mourning tokens; everything grey-dusted.

COLOR PALETTE: Ochre/terracotta skin, grey ash, rust cloth, bone, sun-bleached leather.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A cracked-clay-skinned, ash-caked frontier woman with ember
eyes, a steady weapon and a ledger; grief made flint.

DEMEANOR: Raw, exhausted, dangerously precise; refuses comfortable lies; mercy without forgiveness.
"Dying is the easy ledger to clear... You owe the harder one now. Stay alive." (Ch5)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Ash in her hair; exhausted from vigil; weapon steady though her hand shakes (Ch5).
