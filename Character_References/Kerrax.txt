==================================================================
KERRAX
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — VOLL (syndicate / black-market profiteer). Non-Dominion; the crime-boss species,
and Kerrax is its definitive figure.

ROLE & FACTION: The Coil's local boss on the free-port Drovis; holds leverage and sells Ronin to
Khall for the bounty. Ch4 boss — spared by Ronin (the deliberate mercy that turns Mera Voss).

CHAPTERS: Ch4 (Deepworks bolt-hole boss).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: 50s; ~1.85 m but vastly broad. Heavyset, soft-bulked over real mass, scarred.

HEAD: Sickly green-to-liver-brown oily-sheened voll skin; jowled, multi-folded face; throat dewlaps
that inflate when he gloats; small close-set hooded gold eyes; wide lipless many-toothed mouth (some
teeth gilded). Old scars seamed across the bulk; bored menace worn smooth.

BODY: Bloated and heavy, never been on the floor; stubby ringed clawed fingers; comfort-augments
implanted; moves with lazy entitled weight.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Holdout/ornate sidearm, syndicate enforcers and stun-poles around him, trophy-cargo
and stolen Dominion gear in his hold; a boss's throne-seat in the Deepworks.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Ostentatious syndicate boss finery — rich stolen fabrics, gaudy gold, an armored
ceremonial coat over the bulk, jeweled rings, neon Coil trim.

COLOR PALETTE: Sickly green/liver-brown oily flesh, gaudy gold, rich dark fabrics, neon syndicate accent.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A vast, jowled, gold-draped oily crime-boss with inflating
throat-dewlaps and gilded teeth; smooth bored menace.

DEMEANOR: Transactional, greedy, certain operatives always kill; genuinely confused to be left alive.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - 50s, heavyset, scarred; "confidence of a man who has never been on the floor; menace worn smooth
    by habit" (Ch4).
