==================================================================
THE WARDEN   (Silent Garden boss — a construct, not a person)
==================================================================
SPECIES: CONSTRUCT / NON-CORPOREAL — the Silent Garden's physical fist; a grave-construct/spirit,
not a person and not a species.

ROLE & FACTION: The Garden's final test and guardian — a towering construct assembled from grave-iron,
headstone, root, and packed fog, wearing the half-shape of the oldest thing buried in the Garden.
Pure mechanism; cannot be reasoned with. Ch8 sole boss; after defeat it is put back, sinking into the
barrow rather than destroyed.

CHAPTERS: Ch8.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Wears the age of the grave itself; monumental, ~3.5 m+; massive and slow.

HEAD: No conventional face — a looming head-mass of fused headstone, grave-iron and root wrapped in
packed grey fog, a half-formed effigy of the buried ancient; no mouth, it only TOLLS like a deep
grave-bell. A faint cold light may pulse in its hollows.

BODY: A towering humanoid-ish silhouette built of grave-iron bars, broken headstones, knotted roots
and compacted fog held together by the Garden's will; immense fists; moves with slow inevitability,
shedding grave-dirt and mist. Does not tire.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Its own grave-iron fists and mass; the barrow, headstones and roots of the Garden it
can wield/raise; the toll of the grave-bell.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: None separate — it IS its materials: iron, stone, root, fog, and grave-soil fused
into a half-shape.

COLOR PALETTE: Grave-grey stone, rusted black iron, dark root-brown, drifting white-grey fog; cold
blue-grey inner light.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A monumental, faceless, slow guardian of fused headstone, iron,
root and fog; geology and graveyard made to stand.

DEMEANOR: No voice, no personality — pure mechanism; the Garden's fist; tolls like a grave-bell.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - "Assembled from grave-iron, headstone, root, and packed fog; wears the half-shape of the oldest
    thing buried in the Garden"; tolls like a deep grave-bell; does not tire (Ch8).
