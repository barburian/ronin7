THE MOURNERS   (collective — the Silent Garden's keepers)
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — THE MOURNERS (their own ancient race). Non-Dominion; an eldritch soul-keeper species,
not a Program creation.

ROLE & FACTION: Ancient keepers of the Silent Garden, a burial world. The souls they keep are those
of a native people dead for millions of years (no Program tie). Implacable examiners who run the Trial
of Mind and grant one worthy seeker the sight of one hidden truth by opening what he already carries.
They appear as a ring of near-identical solemn figures; "we" speak as one.

CHAPTERS: Ch8.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Eldritch-ancient ("older than any power now living"); tall and narrow, ~2.1 m
each; they drift rather than stand.

HEAD: No face — a smooth hollow of drifting grey mist where a face should be, sometimes a faint cold
light deep inside. One cold voice braided from many throats, from no mouth and no direction.

BODY: Translucent grey vapor packed into long shroud-robes; only pale, elongated six-fingered
forearms/hands ever resolve out of the fog. They drift, robes never touching the ground, leaving no
print and a grave-cold in the air.

WEAPONS / PROPS: None — they are keepers and examiners; the Garden, the barrow, and the souls they
hold are their domain. (The Warden is the Garden's separate physical fist — see its own file.)

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Long, ancient shroud-robes — ash-grey, tattered at the hems, fog bleeding through
the weave; no ornament but age.

COLOR PALETTE: Ash-grey, shroud-white, cold grave-blue inner glow; no warm color anywhere.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A drifting ring of tall, faceless, fog-bodied shrouded ancients
with pale six-fingered hands; solemn, eternal, identical.

DEMEANOR: Implacable, just, neither cruel nor kind; "You were made to walk where you were sent. So
walk. But the dead remember every name." (Ch8)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Robed, grey, faceless figures ringing the barrow; a single cold voice braided from many throats;
    older than any power now living (Ch8).
