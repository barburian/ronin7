KHALL  —  "The Overseer"
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program handler — human per project rule).

ROLE & FACTION: Ronin-7's Program handler / Overseer. Triggered the killswitch that fired and
failed (Ch2); hunts him through Act I addressing him as "Cipher." Final twist (Ch16): the Kethel-7
order was forged by the Hollow Kings; Khall was a manipulated victim who faked his death, repents,
and allies.

CHAPTERS: Ch1 (unnamed hologram), Ch3 (memory playback), Ch4 (named, direct comms), Ch8 (grief
vision), Ch16 (repentance/alliance).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Mid-40s; ~1.8 m. Upright, controlled military bearing; lean, disciplined.

HEAD: Composed, even-featured command face; clipped greying hair; clinical calm laid over held-down
grief — the eyes are the only thing not steady. By Ch16, older and worn hollow by years of faked
death and guilt.

BODY: Still, precise, unhurried; the steady hand of procedure (only the eyes betray him).

WEAPONS / PROPS: Command sidearm; handler's console/holo-rig; Overseer's authority tokens. In Ch3
playback, he moves to a console to trigger the switch with a steady hand.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Dominion handler's uniform — high-collared dark command coat with restrained
Program insignia, sharp lines, gloves; austere and official. Post-Ch16: stripped, worn, insignia
removed.

COLOR PALETTE: Deep charcoal/ox-blood Dominion command colors, dull silver insignia, black gloves.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: High-collared officer's coat; rigid posture; the controlled
man with grief behind the eyes.

DEMEANOR: Clinically precise, procedure-bound, audibly guilty; "I am not trying to kill you, Cipher.
I am trying to bring you home..." (Ch4); "I'm sorry it has come to this." (Ch8 vision).

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - "I know." (Ch3, agreeing Ronin is right — then pulls the switch anyway).
