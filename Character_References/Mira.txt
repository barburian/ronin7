MIRA
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — WARREN-FOLK (market underclass). Non-Dominion; same species as Resh (she knows him
from his smuggling-route legend).

ROLE & FACTION: Market child / stowaway who followed the crew aboard after seeing Resh walk free with
them. Non-combatant; the crew's moral anchor and the living face of the market's victims. The child
the crew shields, and family aboard The Cairn for the rest of the saga.

CHAPTERS: Ch2–Ch16 (stows away Ch2; recurring crew/family thereafter).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: ~9 years old; tiny warren-folk child; small, light-boned.

HEAD: Grey-mauve child's face, slightly oversized large dark eyes (child proportions); small soft
throat-frill; flat nose-slits; no hair, fine down. Quiet, watchful, decided rather than frightened.

BODY: Small, nimble, still; reads adults' moods with uncanny accuracy. Market-worn.

WEAPONS / PROPS: None — a child. Maybe a single kept token/toy; later, ash-dusted in Ch5.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Oversized scavenged market hand-me-downs — a patched tunic too big for her, a
wrap/hood, worn shoes; the look of a kid who survives on castoffs.

COLOR PALETTE: Grey-mauve skin; faded patched cloth, dust; one small bit of kept color (a ribbon/toy).

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A tiny grey-mauve big-eyed frilled child in oversized rags;
fragile but composed.

DEMEANOR: Few words, each chosen; certain despite fear; trusts Resh's market legend. "Is the loud
part over? Up there. It's loud." (Ch2)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - ~9, small, market-worn, quiet and watchful; "decided," not frightened (Ch2).
