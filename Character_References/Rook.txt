ROOK
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — GRAVELKIN (cave-dwelling stoneblood). Non-Dominion; same species as Gryph, raised
under him in the hold.

ROLE & FACTION: Gryph's second, a fighter who came up under him over ten years. When Gryph leaves
with the crew, Rook is appointed keeper of the Rustfang pirate-hold — a loyal friendly faction in the
deep the saga can return to.

CHAPTERS: Ch9–Ch10 (fights the raid, appointed heir, stays behind; recurring faction-keeper).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Late 20s into 30s (young for a long-lived gravelkin); ~2.0 m. Big, hard, fast
— less crusted than the ancient Gryph.

HEAD: Younger gravelkin hide, smooth-grey with only light early mineral nodules at the brow and
knuckles (age-marker contrast to Gryph); deep-set glassy eyes, blunt jaw, no full tusks yet; fresh
raid-scarring (chipped hide, healing). Determined, capable face.

BODY: Strong and quick for the species — a rally-leader's vigor; bloodied and breathing hard in the
raid; quarried-lean rather than boulder-bulked.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A heavy blade/axe and salvaged hold-gear; a captain's keys/banner handed over when
made keeper.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Lighter scavenged plate and leather than Gryph — practical raider's harness, hold
colors; battle-worn.

COLOR PALETTE: Smooth slate-grey hide (fewer nodules), iron, dark leather; hold-band accent color.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A big, smoother-skinned younger stoneblood with only early
mineral growth; the loyal heir at Gryph's shoulder.

DEMEANOR: Loyal, capable, a natural rally-leader; takes the weight of the hold seriously when it
passes to him.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Late 20s–30s; hard and fast; came up under Gryph over ten years; appointed keeper (Ch9).
