==================================================================
VESS
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — VESH (raider nomad clan). Non-Dominion; the raider/hunter species, and Vess is its
definitive figure.

ROLE & FACTION: Vengeance-driven raider whose people were taken by Ronin's old line; tracked him
across the galaxy. Chooses alliance over killing the man who took her people — boards to strike the
cage instead. Ally #8.

CHAPTERS: Ch10–Ch16 (joins Ch10).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Adult, hard-used; ~1.85 m. Lean, tall, sinewy, predatory.

HEAD: Dark red-brown chitinous skull-cap plating over the brow and crown; narrow wide-set burning
orange/yellow eyes with vertical slit pupils; small paired mandible-tusks at the jaw corners;
clan scarification/branding carved into the face plates. Grief gone to flint.

BODY: Whipcord-sinewy, digitigrade-leaning fast stance; segmented carapace bands over forearms,
shins and chest; long four-fingered hooked-nailed hands; kill-notch tallies carved into the plating.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Raider's blades/hooked weapons, a hunting rifle or harpoon-gun, trophy cords; a
tracker's kit (she hunted Ronin across systems).

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Scavenged raider armor strapped over the carapace — harness, plate scraps, trophy
cords and clan tokens; nomadic, battered, fierce.

COLOR PALETTE: Charcoal-to-oxblood carapace, bone-white scar-brands, scavenged armor straps, trophy bone.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A lean, plated, mandible-tusked predator-raider with carved
kill-tallies; coiled lethality.

DEMEANOR: Unforgiving, hard before and after joining (only her target changes); spares Ronin as a
sentence, not forgiveness; lethal competence.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Hard, lethal bearing; grief gone to flint; chooses alliance over killing the man who took her
    people (Ch10).
