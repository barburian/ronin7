IRIS
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (exempt civilian — explicitly human per project rule). Kessler's daughter.

ROLE & FACTION: Systems/decryption tech and conscience of the crew. Taken at 14, held as Dominion
collateral in Velorum's markets for six years; freed by Ronin-7 at the auction (Ch2). Exposes the
"Kael Vor" cover as a plant (Ch5).

CHAPTERS: Ch2–Ch16 (joins after the auction).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Early 20s (taken at 14, six years held); ~1.7 m. Slight, wiry, quick;
slightly gaunt and undernourished when first freed, fills out over time.

HEAD: Sharp, alert young face; quick darting eyes; practical short or tied-back hair. When first
freed: hollow-cheeked but bright-eyed, a collateral collar-tag still at her throat. Grey ash dusts
her hair in Ch5.

BODY: Fast, precise hands (a born systems-tech); restless, economical movement; carries old caged
wariness that eases as family reforms.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Hand-screen / data-slate, decryption rig, tools; a small holdout sidearm later.
Tech, not melee.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Practical crew tech-wear — fitted utility jumpsuit or layered vest with tool
loops and cable runs, sleeves pushed up, fingerless gloves. The cut collar-tag kept as a keepsake.

COLOR PALETTE: Teal/grey utilitarian with warm copper hair; small indicator-light accents from her rig.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: Lean, sharp tech-kid silhouette bent over a screen; the
keepsake collar-tag; the family resemblance to Kessler around the eyes.

DEMEANOR: Sharp, dry-humored (inherited from Kessler), fearless with data, the crew's hard-question
conscience; sarcastic when afraid. "I hate it, but fine." (Ch3)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Taken at 14, held six years; gaunt but sharp-eyed in the cage (Ch2).
  - Times Ronin's memory-dive in Ch3, voicing dread as sarcasm.
