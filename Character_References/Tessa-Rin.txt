TESSA RIN
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program courier — Program staff; human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: On-the-run Program logistics courier. Sells Program internals at Drovis and hands
Ronin-7 the data proving his killswitch was deliberately sabotaged, then vanishes into the crowd.
One-chapter assist, not an ally.

CHAPTERS: Ch4.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Late 20s; ~1.7 m. Lean, runner's frame; jittery, awake too long.

HEAD: Frayed, sharp face; fear laid thin under bluff; darting eyes; unkempt hair; the look of someone
who hasn't slept and knows she's hunted.

BODY: Twitchy, fast, ready to bolt; go-bag always shouldered; constantly checking exits.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A packed go-bag, a holdout sidearm, the data-chit/courier case she's selling;
a scrambler/false-ID rig.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Nondescript courier traveler's wear — hooded jacket, layered civvies for blending
into Drovis, scuffed boots; Program underkit hidden beneath, insignia removed.

COLOR PALETTE: Drab grey-green and brown; nothing memorable (deliberate camouflage).

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A hooded, twitchy courier clutching a go-bag; built to vanish.

DEMEANOR: Fast-talking, paranoid-with-reason, professional discretion; bluff over genuine fear;
gone the instant the data changes hands.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Jittery, awake too long, go-bag ready; data reveals the switch was sabotaged, then she disappears (Ch4).
