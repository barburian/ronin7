==================================================================
KESSLER
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (exempt civilian — explicitly human per project rule). Former Dominion serviceman.

ROLE & FACTION: Independent salvager; revives Ronin-7 (Ch1); owes him his life from a mercy six
years ago. Runs the Hub — his salvaged capital-class leviathan, THE CAIRN (he calls it "the rig").
Father of Iris. The crew's emotional anchor and home-base captain.

CHAPTERS: Ch1–Ch16 (recurring; home-base anchor).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Late 50s; ~1.85 m. Big, broad, worn-down working frame; heavy hands; a
salvager's durable bulk gone a little tired.

HEAD: Weathered, lined face carrying three weeks of no sleep at first meeting; grey-streaked beard
stubble; deep-set tired eyes with gallows-warmth worn into the creases. Gravelly Terran voice.

BODY: Strong but stiff; the patience of a man who waits and maintains a dead ship alone. Old work
injuries in the way he moves.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A salvager's tools — cutting torch, multi-tool, sidearm holstered but rarely
drawn; data-slate; mag-boots. Not a fighter by trade.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Practical, lived-in salvage rig — heavy canvas/leather work coat, padded vest,
tool harness, grease-stained gloves, layered thermals for the cold dark decks.

COLOR PALETTE: Oil-brown, rust, faded olive, worn leather, grey.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: Big tired bear of a man in a tool harness; fatherly weight;
reads as the freighter captain you trust.

DEMEANOR: Gravelly, wry, gallows-humored; protective (especially of Iris and Mira); loyal through
action; calm under pressure. "Go down, get who you are, come back up. That's the whole mission to me."

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Six-years-ago mercy: "You had me on my knees... and then you were just standing there." (Ch1)
  - Three weeks of no sleep visible on his face (Ch1).
