COMMANDER VALE
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion old-line commander — human per project rule).

ROLE & FACTION: Dormant Dominion authority held in cryo-stasis as the command-key for the operative
network. Woken in Ch12; believes the "defective generation" of fractured operatives can be roused
into a self-aware army under his command — the seductive "wrong answer" (command instead of liberation).

CHAPTERS: Ch12 (cryo-command vault confrontation).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Old-line commander, aged out of an earlier era and held in stasis — looks
50s–60s, preserved; ~1.78 m. Erect, commanding, strategist's frame.

HEAD: Severe, intelligent face; cryo-pallid skin with faint frost-burn at the temples; steel-grey
hair, severe cut; cold compelling eyes. Carries the certainty of someone used to being obeyed.

BODY: Stiff from stasis at first, then ramrod command posture; measured, deliberate gestures.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Command baton/sidearm; the cryo-vault throne/cradle and its command-network
interface; stasis-frost still steaming off his gear when woken.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: An older, grander Dominion command uniform — heavier ceremonial cut than Khall's,
ornate epaulettes and an antique rank-sash, half-frosted from stasis. Reads "previous generation of
the empire."

COLOR PALETTE: Frost-white and pale blue over deep imperial charcoal/oxblood; tarnished gold rank-trim.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: Frost-rimed antique officer; the seductive, dangerous prophet
of conquest; old-empire grandeur in a buried vault.

DEMEANOR: Strategic, compelling, certain command is the natural order; "You're not an edition, Cipher.
You're the original. We grew them all from you. And from one good original you can print an army that
never ends." (Ch12)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Reveals Ronin-7 is the prime Ronin template (the seventh, first-viable; 1-6 culled) — the army is
    cloned FROM him, inverting the Ch11 "you're a copy" supposition (Ch12).
  - Offers Ronin-7 an army; refuses sanctuary when he cuts the node loose (Ch12).
