SABLE   (Wraith-generation living archive)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program operative weaponized as a living-archive node — human per rule,
but heavily wired/augmented). Holds the Wraith generation's shadow-AIs.

ROLE & FACTION: An operative racked with dozens of kept Wraith-make shadow-AIs and wired into the
Concord Engine's transmission lattice as a node. Conscious through every shadow seated in her. Ally
#6; "the map" — she feels the server-veins to the other scattered archives. Wants to be a person,
not infrastructure.

CHAPTERS: Ch9–Ch16 (freed in the Tide depths, joins, becomes the living map).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Looks 30s; ~1.7 m. Operative frame, but gaunt and overburdened by the racking
hardware fused to her.

HEAD: Drawn, exhausted face; eyes that seem to hold many presences at once. DATA-LIGHT crawls under
the skin of her temples, throat and jaw — fine glowing circuit-veins that pulse with the lattice.
Hair thin or shaved at the contact points where ports seat.

BODY: Spinal and shoulder RACKING — a half-exposed lattice of conditioning hardware, cabling and
glowing data-conduits grown into her back and arms, never fully letting go. Data-light traces the
veins beneath her skin all over. Moves a little tethered, as if the racks breathe with her.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Not a fighter; the lattice itself is her "weapon"/function. Trailing conduit-tethers,
archive-glyphs that surface on her skin.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Minimal — a torn archive-shift / wrap that leaves the back-racking exposed; salvage
straps; the look of someone unplugged from a machine she was built into.

COLOR PALETTE: Ashen grey skin with cold blue-white data-light veins; black hardware; faint archive-amber glyphs.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A human host with a glowing lattice grown out of her spine and
data-light under the skin; doubled, layered voice; tragedy-made-visible.

DEMEANOR: Exhausted grief refined into purpose; reaches for personhood; voice carries a faint
doubling, "never fully one throat." "This is a voice — loud enough to reach every world at once." (Ch9)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Data-light under the skin; doubled voice; conscious through every shadow strung into her (Ch9).
