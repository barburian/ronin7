RONIN-7  /  "CIPHER"  /  "KAEL VOR" (false)  /  SOREN (birth name)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program operative — the protagonist of the human empire of killers).

ROLE & FACTION: The prime template of the Ronin program (the current/newest make:
Knight > Ninja > Wraith > Ronin) — its seventh iteration and the first viable one (Ronin-1..6
were flawed and culled); the original the Ronin editions were cloned FROM, not a downstream copy
(revealed Ch12, inverting the Ch11 supposition). Assassin built for "clean erasures"; first defector;
becomes leader of the rebellion. Codename "Cipher" (used by handler Khall and his AI Echo);
"Kael Vor" is a planted cover identity (exposed Ch5); "Soren" is his recovered birth name (Ch16).

CHAPTERS: Ch1–Ch16 (protagonist throughout).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Looks mid-30s, carries older; ~1.8 m. Lean-muscular, engineered combat
body — no bulk wasted, every line functional. Operative "manufactured" silhouette.

HEAD: Hard, still face; flat affect that thaws as the saga humanizes him. Short, practical dark
hair. Eyes wired for the optic-feed bond with Echo (a faint instrument-glint at the iris in low
light — "awake behind his eyes"). A long, surgical THROAT SCAR runs across the front of the neck
(signature). Faint killswitch scar at the base of the skull.

BODY: Augment-seams traceable under the skin at neck, spine and forearms — "expensive military
augments under cheap grafted skin," the graft a slightly different tone than his own. Economical,
lethal movement; no theatrics; goes utterly still before he acts.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A plain, wrapped KATANA — worn cord hilt, unadorned saya — that houses Echo,
the defected shadow-AI. A small steady light lives in the hilt/blade-line when Echo is present;
the blade is inert dead steel in any other hand. Later gains ability-tells from freed blade-shadows
(Overdrive/Phase-step/Unbroken/Mirror) but the sword's look stays humble.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Stripped-down operative blacks and greys — close-fit underlayer, segmented
light armor panels at chest/forearms/shins, a long worn travel coat/wrap over it. Mission-dirt
texture. Nothing ornamental; salvaged, repaired, practical.

COLOR PALETTE: Matte black, ash-grey, gunmetal; the single warm note is the katana's hilt-light
and dried-blood red accents.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: Throat scar; wrapped katana at the hip; long coat; the
operative's dead-careful stillness. Reads as a worn, haunted swordsman in dead-tech sci-fi.

DEMEANOR: Flat, measured, economical of speech and motion; conscience visibly bleeding through
the conditioning; offers his own life as the solution; the hitch-before-the-kill is his "tell."

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - "expensive military augments under cheap skin" (Ch1).
  - Throat scar referenced repeatedly (Ch1+).
  - "You built me to carry the dead." (Ch16)
