CORAL VEX   (Wraith-line operative)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program operative — Wraith make, predecessor to Ronin; human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: A freed operative of the WRAITH program (the make right before Ronin). Tore out her
own killswitch by hand and survived; hid for a lifetime behind a dead salvager's identity; keeps a
reliquary of kept operative-blades. Ally #4. Forebear/correction to Ronin, NOT a sibling — her
shadow recognizes his.

CHAPTERS: Ch7–Ch16 (joins in the reliquary, recurs as Program-knowledge resource).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Visibly older than Ronin — weathered by decades of hiding; ~1.75 m. Operative
frame gone lean and sinewy with age, still dangerous.

HEAD: Hard, weathered woman's face; grey-shot hair (cropped or tied); steel-cold eyes; faint
old augment-seams. Survival written into the lines.

BODY: Moves with the unmistakable engineered economy of an operative — recognizable gait — but
older, careful, a lifetime of caution in it. A brutal self-surgery SCAR at the base of her skull /
nape where she ripped her own killswitch out while it was live.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Her own blade housing her own defected shadow-AI (kept, not shelved — a hilt-light
of its own); a reliquary of sheathed operative-blades she guards.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Worn survivor/salvager layers over the remnants of operative underkit — long
weathered coat, wraps, patched armor scraps; the look of a killer who spent decades pretending to
be a junk-dealer.

COLOR PALETTE: Faded slate, bone, deep teal-grey; tarnished steel; cold blade-light.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: An aged operative in salvager's rags with a self-cut nape
scar and a kept blade; the elder path Ronin doesn't take.

DEMEANOR: Weary, steel-spined, reverent toward the kept shadows; authority of one who cut her own
leash. "I reached in and I tore the switch out myself, with my own hands, while it was still live."

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Older "in the way Cipher is older than his face"; moves exactly like an operative (Ch7).
