THE VELORUM BROKER   (unnamed records-fence)
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — VOLL (syndicate / black-market profiteer). Non-Dominion; same species as Kerrax,
but the lean, paranoid, low-rung variant of the crime-species rather than a fat boss.

ROLE & FACTION: Paranoid black-market records-fence on Velorum, trafficking in dead-operative
termination/disposal manifests — his vault holds Ronin-7's own file. An obstacle/objective, reached
only via Resh's back-channels; refuses to open for money. Ch2.

CHAPTERS: Ch2.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Indeterminate adult; ~1.6 m, hunched. Smaller, leaner, sickly voll — a runt of
the species worn down by fear.

HEAD: Mottled yellow-grey oily voll skin, gone sallow; a permanent flinch in the brow; small darting
close-set black-gold eyes behind smeared protective glass; thin nervous dewlaps; reedy fast voice.
Twitchy, sweating.

BODY: Hunched, narrow-for-a-voll, jittery; stubby ringed fingers tapping; never leaves his grille.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A barred, screened records-stall; a sealed vault; surveillance rig; a panic-button;
deals entirely through a grille. A bodyguard nearby.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Cheaper, grubbier syndicate finery than Kerrax — a worn ornate coat gone shabby,
a few rings, a fence's many-pocketed apron of data-chits; the look of small-time greed under siege.

COLOR PALETTE: Sallow yellow-grey oily flesh; grubby tarnished gold, smeared glass, dim stall-neon.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A hunched, flinching, sallow lesser-voll behind smeared glass
and bars; paranoid small-time fence.

DEMEANOR: Survivalist paranoia, fast-patter, trusts nothing; "You're the kind of question that gets a
man's stall burned with him inside it." (Ch2)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Paranoid shape behind smeared glass; reedy fast voice; permanent flinch; barred screened stall (Ch2).
