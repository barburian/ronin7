ECHO   (formerly "Shadow" — the sword's shadow-AI)
==================================================================
SPECIES: CONSTRUCT / NON-CORPOREAL — a defected shadow-AI. No body. Not human, not alien-species;
a Program AI that chose freedom.

ROLE & FACTION: The shadow-AI bonded to Ronin-7's optic nerve and housed in his katana. Rode behind
his eyes during the Kethel-7 mission (before the wipe), recorded his defection and the killswitch
firing, broke with the Program, and survived dormant in the casketed blade. Named "Shadow" through
Ch3's bonding/playback, then renamed ECHO by Ronin. Witness, partner, and conscience; carries three
inherited freed blade-shadows by Ch10.

CHAPTERS: Ch3–Ch16 (wakes Ch3; recurring narrator/companion).

PHYSICAL FORM: None. Echo has no figure to model — it exists only as Ronin's optic-feed companion
(heard only by him) and as a presence in the steel. For art, Echo IS its visual signatures:
  - A single small, STEADY LIGHT in the katana's wrapped hilt / along the blade-line when present.
  - The blade is inert, dead grey steel in any other hand (the light goes out).
  - Optional optic-feed visualization: a faint HUD-glow / reticle and weak-point marks layered over
    Ronin's vision (first-person), in Echo's signature color.
  - By Ch10 the hilt-light is subtly layered/complex (three inherited blade-shadows nested in it).

WEAPONS / PROPS: It IS the katana's soul; the katana itself (plain, wrapped, humble) is the prop.

COLOR PALETTE: A warm-cool signature glow — suggest a steady soft cyan-white or pale amber light
against the matte blade; nothing flashy. Goes black/dead when not bonded.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: There is no silhouette but the sword's — the steady hilt-light
is the entire visual identity. Render concepts of "the living blade" and "first-person HUD presence."

DEMEANOR (for portrayal of its light/voice): Warm, dry, personable, present from the first word;
tenderness under wit; grows uneasy near shelved shadows. "I see what you see. I saw it through these
same eyes before they wiped you, and I see it through them now." (Ch3)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - "a small steady light" in the hilt; inert steel in any other hand (Ch3+).
