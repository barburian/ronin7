==================================================================
RESH
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — WARREN-FOLK (market underclass). Non-Dominion, so alien per project rule; the
warren-folk are the smuggler/street species and Resh is their definitive figure.

ROLE & FACTION: Smuggler running an underground railroad for children out of Velorum's markets
(40+ saved over twenty years). Ally #1. Spared by Ronin at the auction; joins to strike the source,
not to repay a debt. Ch6 contact with inside-informant Morrigan.

CHAPTERS: Ch2–Ch16 (joins Ch2).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Early 40s (warren-folk); slight, lean runner's build, a touch under human
height. Quick.

HEAD: Grey-mauve warren-folk skin; large dark reflective eyes (quick, reading everything); soft
vestigial throat-frill that flushes when stressed; flat nose-slits; no hair, fine forearm down.
Worn, alert, faintly idealistic face under a fixer's surface calm.

BODY: Low-shouldered, nimble, four-fingered broad-padded hands working a hand-screen "fast and easy";
a climber's/pickpocket's economy.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Hand-screen, false manifests/forged ID rigs, smuggling tools, holdout blade; a
runner's grav-board or rope-rig for the warrens.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Scavenged market layers — patched canvas coat, oil-stained wraps, smuggler's
many-pocketed vest, hood; practical, drab, built to disappear into a crowd.

COLOR PALETTE: Grey-mauve skin; muted canvas browns, oil-grey, faded street colors.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A slight grey-mauve, big-eyed, frilled smuggler in patched
layers, hunched over a screen; the underclass survivor archetype.

DEMEANOR: Quick, clipped, economical of speech; pragmatic surface over buried idealism. "Because
you're the first crack I've seen in a wall I've spent twenty years smuggling around." (Ch2)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Quick eyes; works a hand-screen fast and easy; "I pull children out of this market. One at a
    time." (Ch2)
