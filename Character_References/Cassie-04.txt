SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program operative weaponized as a living-archive node — human per rule,

ROLE & FACTION: A mine-shaft living-archive node racked with the whole Ninja make; she IS its node
and ledger — her cross-index reads the erased across the server and points to where the other
scattered nodes are held. Ally #7; first of Sable's kin recovered alive.

CHAPTERS: Ch10–Ch16 (freed in the Ninefold mine shafts, joins).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Looks late 20s–30s; ~1.68 m. Operative frame, worn but more wiry-intact than

HEAD: Sharp, dry-witted face; DATA-LIGHT pulses under the skin (distinct rhythm from Sable's —

BODY: Spinal/shoulder racking like Sable's but trimmer; data-conduits and archive-glyphs run her

WEAPONS / PROPS: The ledger itself — registry-glyphs she can surface and "read out"; conduit-tethers;

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Torn archive-shift and salvage straps leaving the racking visible, like Sable, but
she keeps hers tidier — wraps cinched, gear ordered.

COLOR PALETTE: Ashen skin with cold GREEN-white data-light (to distinguish from Sable's blue-white);
black hardware; amber ledger-glyphs.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A second living-archive host — trimmer, tidier than Sable,
with scrolling ledger-glyphs on the skin and green-white data-veins.

DEMEANOR: Bone-dry, gallows-witty, clear-eyed; survives horror by counting it exactly; does not
want pity. "I'm a ledger, Cipher. I have a column for everything. I did not have a column for that." (Ch10)
