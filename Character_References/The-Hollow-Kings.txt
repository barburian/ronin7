==================================================================
THE HOLLOW KINGS   (agents of the Obsidian Synod)
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — OBSIDIAN SYNOD (shadow-author voidform). Non-Dominion; the crowned/masked
lieutenant-variants of the true-enemy species (Maelgorn is their apex).

ROLE & FACTION: The shadow authors who forged the orders that built the cage — including the Kethel-7
order Khall was made to carry, breaking Ronin-7 open with a sin he never committed. They operate
through proxies and forged documents; their puppet-show runs Ch1–Ch16. Revealed Ch16 as the source
of the forged order.

CHAPTERS: Ch16 (revealed; felt as the unseen "outside hand" throughout).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Eldritch, ageless; tall regal voidforms, ~2.2 m; a council of several near-peers.

HEAD: Eyeless obsidian-black heads behind / fused into ornate HOLLOW CROWNS or masks — emptier than
Maelgorn, literally hollow where a core should be (you can see through the crown's center into void).
Cold WHITE-BLUE fault-light seams (lower rank than Maelgorn's furnace-orange).

BODY: Tall, smooth, polished black voidforms with too-many-jointed limbs and too-smooth motion;
shadow-smoke edges; a hollow cavity at the chest/head where light shows through.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Forged-order artifacts, seals and writs; proxy-strings; thrones of office. They act
by decree and forgery, not by hand.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Fused regalia of office — fractured obsidian mantles and the signature hollow
crowns/masks; each King slightly distinct in crown-motif and degree of hollowness.

COLOR PALETTE: Obsidian black, void-purple undertone, cold white-blue fault-seams; hollow voids
showing through.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: Tall eyeless obsidian figures crowned with hollow crowns you
can see through; cold white-blue seams; a council of forgers.

DEMEANOR: Cold, bureaucratic-eternal authorship of suffering; menace by absence and decree.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Forged the Kethel-7 order to break Ronin-7 and aim him at the Concord Engine (Bible §6, Ch16).
