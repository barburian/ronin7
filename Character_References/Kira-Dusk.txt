KIRA DUSK   (deceased — appears in memory only)

SPECIES: ALIEN — ASHKIN (frontier dust-world settler). Non-Dominion; same species as her sister Vera.

ROLE & FACTION: Vera's younger sister, 14 at death, killed during the ash-world pacification Ronin's
line carried out. Appears only in Ronin's Ch5 memory-dive and as the named dead in Vera's ledger.
Her death is the half-second Ronin was "late" — the debt he carries forward.

CHAPTERS: Ch5 (memory/vision only).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: 14 at death; slight ashkin adolescent; ~1.6 m.

HEAD: Young ashkin face — ruddy ochre cracked-clay skin (lighter plating than an adult, just forming
on the cheekbones); bright amber ember eyes, full of curiosity; coarse rust-grey hair, ashy. Alive
and absorbed in her lists in the memory; serene.

BODY: Adolescent, light, perched by a well; cataloguing the ships overhead on a slate (she liked the names).

WEAPONS / PROPS: A child's writing-slate with ship-name lists; a settler's water-pail at the well —
the quiet domestic props of the memory.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Simple frontier child's clothing — a plain rust tunic, wrap, worn shoes, breath-scarf
loose; everything faintly ash-dusted.

COLOR PALETTE: Ochre skin, rust cloth, grey ash, soft daylight (a warmer, alive palette than the rest
of the saga — it's a remembered moment).

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A slight ash-world girl at a well with a slate of ship-names;
the living face of the cost.

DEMEANOR: Curious, gentle, unaware; the innocent the conscience-fracture is tied to.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - 14 at death; "girl by a well with a slate, cataloguing ships overhead" — kept lists of ships'
    names (Ch5).
