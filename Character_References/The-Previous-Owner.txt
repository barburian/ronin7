==================================================================
THE PREVIOUS OWNER   (feral blade-imprint, mindspace mini-boss)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program operative — an old make; human per rule). Encountered as a combat-
imprint inside a blade's mindspace, not a living body.

ROLE & FACTION: The long-dead operative whose blade is the OLDEST kept shadow in Coral Vex's
reliquary — decades awake on the rack with nothing left to witness, gone feral. When Cipher grips the
feral blade to quiet it, its AI bonds to his feed and throws him into the blade's MINDSPACE, where the
owner's combat-imprint defends the steel, looping the last fight he ever had. Not a villain; a victim
who was never allowed to stop. Ch7 mini-boss; on defeat he is RELEASED (not killed), and his pacified
shadow hands its combat-read to Echo (the WEAKPOINT-SIGHT ability).

CHAPTERS: Ch7 (mindspace mini-boss; encountered only inside the blade).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: An old make, generations dead; ~1.8 m. Heavy, archaic operative build — broader
and more brutal than the lean modern makes; a fighter from an older, cruder doctrine.

HEAD: A worn, hollowed operative face half-dissolving into after-image and scrubbed static; eyes empty
of any witness, fixed on a fight that ended lifetimes ago. Old augment-seams long corroded; skull-base
killswitch scar. The face stutters and re-draws as the loop resets.

BODY: Locked in a repeating archaic combat-cycle — the same strikes, the same footwork, over and over,
trailing ghost after-images of itself. Moves like a recording skipping; powerful, relentless, mindless.

WEAPONS / PROPS: The feral blade itself, rendered in mindspace as a heavy, old-school operative weapon
(an archaic, brutal cut); echo-duplicates of the blade smear through his loop. A guttering, overcharged
hilt-light that steadies and dims when he is finally quieted.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Obsolete operative kit predating the current makes — decayed dark plating and wraps,
sigils worn illegible, the gear of a program long discontinued; edges fraying into datascape glitch.

COLOR PALETTE: Rust-iron and bone-grey over corroded black, lit by cold ghost-blue mindspace glow and
threads of scrub-static white.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A looping, after-imaged ancient swordsman dissolving at the
edges into static; the oldest kept blade made flesh inside the mind; a grave that still swings.

DEMEANOR: Feral, wordless repetition — a kept witness driven past sense by decades with nothing to
guard. Not malice but unbearable persistence; the win is mercy, the quieting of a man never allowed
to put the blade down.

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - No spoken lines (a feral combat-imprint). Framed as "a kept witness gone feral... released, not
    killed" — pacifying him is itself the mercy, and frees weakpoint-sight (Ch7).
