ALDRIC  /  KNIGHT-1   (the eldest leashed keeper)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program operative — the very first Knight make; human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: The first operative of the first program — "Knight-One," the prototype every later
make was cut from. Leashed keeper of the broken bone-canyon node (Knight-generation shadows),
half-lost to the node's broadcast; cannot be freed, only put down. Ch11 boss (fought through a
narcosis dreamscape); his death frees the UNBROKEN ability (fear-ward + survive one lethal blow).

CHAPTERS: Ch11 (bone-canyon dreamscape boss).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: The oldest thing the Program ever leashed — ancient; ~1.9 m. The prototype
operative frame: the heaviest, broadest, most archaic of all the makes.

HEAD: An ancient, weathered, half-vacant face, eroded by centuries of keeping an overloaded archive;
deep-set hollow eyes with faint broadcast-static crawling across them; long grey-white hair/beard,
matted. Existing between person and shadow — features sometimes flicker/de-resolve.

BODY: Heavy, monumental, slow-immense; the oldest and most archaic combat school in the game.
Old, deep scarring; armor fused into him by ages of wear.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A huge, ancient great-blade (the heaviest, most archaic weapon the player faces);
a node-overloaded blade-shadow that frees at death. The bone-canyon and the dream interrupt the duel.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: The very first operative armor — heavy, knightly, antique plating gone to ruin;
fused, broadcast-corroded, draped in canyon dust and dream-static. Reads "ancestor of all the others."

COLOR PALETTE: Bone-white, rust, ash, dead grey; cold static-flicker; dim hilt-light.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A towering, ancient armored knight half-dissolving into
static; the distant ancestor of every operative; ruin made to stand.

DEMEANOR: Ancient certainty, the weight of being first; half-broadcast, dream-haunted. "I was the
first. Knight-One... I am the oldest thing they ever leashed, and I have kept this a long time." (Ch11)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - "Every make they ran after me, they cut from me." (Ch11)
