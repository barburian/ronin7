MASTER KAELEN   (Iron Dojo — the Vesting / Graduation Tower boss; "the architect")
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program master — human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: Architect-master of the Vesting (the final tower): shadow-AI bonding, killswitch
implantation, and the final hollowing before deployment. Calm, clinical, curious about his own work
like a researcher. Delivers the chapter's conscience reveal — the Program engineers emotionlessness
on purpose, and mercy is the seam that leaks through. Ch6 final boss; dies giving the confession.

CHAPTERS: Ch6.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Middle to older; ~1.8 m. Refined, spare, upright; a scholar's frame, not a brute's.

HEAD: Calm, intelligent, ascetic face; clean-lined; thin grey hair or shaved; precise, curious,
detached eyes. Reads as the cold mind behind the machine.

BODY: Measured, clinical, unhurried even in defeat; surgeon-precise gestures; almost relieved as he
confesses while dying.

WEAPONS / PROPS: His knowledge is the weapon — the Vesting apparatus: bonding cradles, killswitch
implanters, hollowing rigs; a master's blade he wields with clinical precision.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: A master-architect's vestment — austere high-collared dark robe/coat with fine
Program sigils, surgical underlayer, thin instruments; elegant, restrained, ceremonial.

COLOR PALETTE: Deep charcoal and sterile white; thin silver sigils; cold instrument-blue.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: An ascetic, robed master with surgeon's hands; precision and
cold interest; the intellectual horror of the trio.

DEMEANOR: Calm, curious, intellectually honest about his own evil; "We built you hollow on purpose...
What leaks through is mercy." (Ch6)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Measured, calm, clinical; architect of leashes and conditioning; relief in the deathbed reveal (Ch6).
