MORRIGAN
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program engineer — human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: Rogue Program engineer embedded for nine years inside the Iron Dojo training
citadel, building the leashes (killswitches, shadow-AI bonding, conditioning hardware) while secretly
smuggling one child out per season via Resh. The crew's inside informant; Ally #3. Knows empathy is
the designed flaw and wants proof it can win. Runs the Cairn's analysis bench after joining.

CHAPTERS: Ch6–Ch16 (walks out of the Iron Dojo with the crew, joins).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: 30s into 40s; ~1.72 m. Lean, tired-in-the-bone; engineer's stillness over
contained fury.

HEAD: Sharp, severe, fatigued face; dark hair, practical and unfussed; flat, blunt eyes that miss
nothing. No softness in the expression; precision over warmth.

BODY: Spare, controlled movement; the stillness of someone who works delicate hardware all day;
quiet anger held under it.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Engineer's diagnostic kit, firmware/analysis tools, the Program hardware she knows
intimately; an analysis bench. A concealed sidearm.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Program technician's coveralls / field-engineer rig — utility jumpsuit, tool
harness, magnification visor pushed up on the forehead, ID stripped after defection.

COLOR PALETTE: Slate, oil-grey, muted teal; tool-metal; small diagnostic indicator-lights.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A lean, severe field-engineer with a brow visor and tool
harness; controlled fury under technical calm.

DEMEANOR: Blunt, technical, dry; no illusions about her complicity; judges plainly. "Whoever cut your
leash knew the firmware better than the people who shipped it... That's an insider." (Ch6)
