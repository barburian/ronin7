==================================================================
MERA VOSS
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program staff — ex-Dominion tracker; human per project rule).

ROLE & FACTION: Ex-Dominion tracker/hunter (Ally #2). Sent to confirm Ronin-7's death (Ch4);
defects after witnessing the Kerrax mercy — proof an operative can choose. Becomes the crew's
recon/tactical expert.

CHAPTERS: Ch4–Ch16 (hunts him Ch4, joins, stays aboard).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: 30s; ~1.75 m. Controlled, economical, trained frame — athletic, not bulky.

HEAD: Hard, composed face with a hunter's flat attention; reads trails and kill-boxes the way she
reads people. Practical short or tightly bound hair; cool, assessing eyes. Minimal expression
early, cracking open as conviction shifts.

BODY: Disciplined, still, arms often folded; every gesture spare. Moves like the operatives she
used to hunt — the same trained economy, slightly less surgical.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Tracker's kit — long-range scoped rifle/marksman weapon, sidearm, optics/scanner,
trail-reading sensor gear, restraint gear from her hunter days.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Tactical Dominion-derived tracker gear stripped of insignia — fitted armored
bodysuit, segmented plates, utility harness, light cloak/scarf for cover; functional, muted.

COLOR PALETTE: Slate grey, desaturated green, black; gunmetal optics.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: Lean tactical hunter with a long weapon and folded-arms
stillness; an ex-Dominion soldier who scrubbed her own insignia.

DEMEANOR: Cold, professional, observant; distrustful then quietly converted; "I'd rather be on the
side that hesitates." (Ch4)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - "I've put down four of your kind... That isn't a fault in the metal. That's a man." (Ch5)
