==================================================================
MATRON HESPA   (Iron Dojo — the Cradle / Intake Tower boss)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program master — human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: Warden of the Cradle (the Intake Tower of the Iron Dojo citadel), where the youngest
intake are stripped of their names and given numbers. Certain she is being kind — "the kindest thing
that will ever happen to them." Ch6 boss (the central horror with a grandmother's face); killed, no
spare condition. Does not fight or flee.

CHAPTERS: Ch6.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Older — grandmotherly; ~1.6 m. Soft, round, plump frame; gentle, unhurried.

HEAD: Soft, round, kindly face; warm crinkled eyes; neat grey hair in a bun; a perpetual gentle
smile. The horror is that it all looks genuinely loving.

BODY: Slow, motherly, unbothered even in combat; sits calmly with a child on her knee. Soft hands.

WEAPONS / PROPS: None overt — the intake machinery, name-stripping consoles, numbering tools of the
Cradle are her "weapons"; a child often beside her.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: A warden-matron's soft warm dress / robe — nursery-gentle, layered, clean; an
apron or shawl; the costume of a kindly caretaker, with subtle Program insignia woven in.

COLOR PALETTE: Warm cream, dove-grey, soft rose; gentle nursery tones that curdle on second look.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A plump, smiling grandmother in soft robes amid child-erasure
machinery; gentle horror.

DEMEANOR: Falsely, fully kind; serene; "I take away the things that hurt them... I am the kindest
thing that will ever happen to them." (Ch6)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Soft-voiced, round-faced, motherly, unbothered in combat (Ch6).
