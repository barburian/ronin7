MAELGORN   (the Obsidian Synod — the true enemy)
==================================================================
SPECIES: ALIEN — OBSIDIAN SYNOD (shadow-author voidform). Non-Dominion; the apex form of the
true-enemy species, revealed Ch16.

ROLE & FACTION: The intelligence behind the ten syndicates and the war-machine — the architect of
the cage, which feeds on perpetual war. Forged the Kethel-7 order to break Ronin-7 and aim him, as a
manufactured rebel, at the Dominion's Concord Engine (which threatened the Synod's war by imposing
total control). The "outside hand" Morrigan sensed; unmasked and confronted in Ch16.

CHAPTERS: Ch6 (sensed), Ch12 (guessed at), Ch16 (revealed, final confrontation).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Eldritch, ageless; the largest, deepest-black Synod form; ~2.4 m, regal.

HEAD: A featureless or near-featureless obsidian-black head that drinks light, eyeless — instead
hairline FAULT-LIGHT seams the face in dull FURNACE-ORANGE (his rank-mark; others run cold white-blue).
Edges fracture faintly into shadow-smoke.

BODY: Tall, regal, polished obsidian-black voidform; elongated, too many joints, movement unnervingly
too-smooth; the brightest fault-light of any Synod form, banked like coals.

WEAPONS / PROPS: A throne / war-loom of the cage; the forged-order artifacts; he rarely needs a
weapon — he moves the galaxy by proxy. Shadow-smoke and fault-light as presence.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Not clothed so much as crowned — a sovereign's fused obsidian regalia, a heavy
fractured crown/mantle of black glass, the apex of the Synod silhouette.

COLOR PALETTE: Obsidian black with void-purple undertone; furnace-orange fault-seams; no skin tone.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A towering, crowned, eyeless obsidian sovereign veined with
banked furnace-orange light, edges bleeding shadow-smoke; cold absolute menace.

DEMEANOR: Cold sovereignty, the long view, feeds on war; "I needed a blade I didn't have to hold. So
I broke one open and let it believe the breaking was its own." (Ch16)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - The intelligence behind the ten syndicates; forged the order to aim Ronin at the Engine (Bible §6, Ch16).
