DR. HERIS   (incl. the Ch8 "faceless woman" vision form)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program architect-engineer — human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: The engineer who built Ronin-7 AND his killswitch, then was conscripted to scale her
suppression science into the Concord Engine. Secretly planted the saving flaw that fractured his
conscience and made the switch fail. Ally #9; defects to destroy the Engine she was forced to build.
The unnamed faceless woman in the Ch8 vision (gloved hands seating a child's killswitch, whispering
"I hope you will save us") IS Heris — face never shown there by design; resolved in Ch13.

CHAPTERS: Ch8 (faceless vision, hands only), Ch13–Ch16 (revealed, joins).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: 50s–60s; ~1.7 m. Slight, upright, worn by guilt but precise; surgeon's stillness.

HEAD: Lined, intelligent, austere face; grey hair pinned back severely; sharp tired eyes carrying
unspoken guilt. Reads as a brilliant, cold, conscience-burdened scientist.

BODY: Careful, exact, economical hands (the same gloved hands from the Ch8 vision); clinical posture.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Surgical/engineering tools; killswitch and Engine schematics; the sabotage-key keyed
to the seam she hid in the Engine. In the Ch8 vision: only her gloved forearms and hands, working
with terrible care over a child's sealed skull.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Program medical-engineer's attire — high-collared sterile lab coat / surgical
smock over a dark under-suit, gloves, fine instruments at the belt. Post-defection: the same, worn,
insignia stripped.

COLOR PALETTE: Surgical white and pale grey over charcoal; sterile blue light; clinical steel.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A severe, slight, lab-coated architect with gloved precise
hands; in vision form, only those hands resolve out of darkness.

DEMEANOR: Cold precision over deep moral weight; doesn't ask mercy, asks to "finish it." Whispered:
"I hope you will save us." (Ch8)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Built the killswitch and the flaw on purpose; conscripted Engine architect (Bible §4 #9, Ch13).
