==================================================================
DRILLMASTER CARADOC   (Iron Dojo — the Proving / Trial Tower boss)
==================================================================
SPECIES: Human (Dominion/Program master — human per rule).

ROLE & FACTION: Brutal master of the Proving (the Trial Tower), where older children are beaten into
shape and failures discarded. Believes pain is the only honest teacher. The one master who wants the
fight — a hands-on boss duel. Ch6 boss; killed (demands the killing blow), no spare condition.

CHAPTERS: Ch6.

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Middle years, carved by decades of training; ~1.95 m. Big, slab-muscled,
built for violence.

HEAD: Hard, brutal, contemptuous face heavily SCARRED from decades of breaking people; close-shorn
or bald; a parade-ground voice "built to carry across a yard"; cold proud eyes.

BODY: Powerful, scarred all over (every scar "a lesson"); moves with brutal, drilled efficiency;
default sneer of contempt.

WEAPONS / PROPS: Hand-to-hand and a heavy training weapon (staff/baton/blunt blade); the Proving's
trial-yard apparatus, whipping posts, breaking-frames.

CLOTHING / ARMOR: Drillmaster's harness — stripped to scarred torso or a heavy sleeveless training
gambeson, bracers, a sergeant's half-plate; functional, austere, Program-marked.

COLOR PALETTE: Iron-grey, blood-rust, sweat-dark leather; bare scarred skin.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A huge, bald, scar-covered drill-sergeant; loud, proud,
brutal; the opposite face of Hespa's quiet horror.

DEMEANOR: Loud, certain, unrepentant; cruelty as virtue; "There's the ones that break and the ones
that get broke, and somebody has to do the breaking..." (Ch6)

CANON SOURCE QUOTES:
  - Big, scarred, built for violence; voice built to carry across a yard (Ch6).
