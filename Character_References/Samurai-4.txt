ROLE & FACTION: The Program's finest hunter-operative, sent to stop Soren at the threshold of

CHAPTERS: Ch16 (hunts him down the Iron Sepulcher, then joins for the finale).

AGE / BUILD / HEIGHT: Late 20s–30s; ~1.75 m. Elite operative frame — lethal precision engineered

BODY: Flawless economy of motion; conditioned stillness that develops the same "hitch before the
kill" as Ronin once she begins to break.

mission-worn (she's still issued, not yet a fugitive).

COLOR PALETTE: Black and cold steel-blue, sharp white edge-lines, faint blade hilt-light.

SILHOUETTE / DISTINGUISHING FEATURES: A pristine dark-armored mirror of Ronin with a half-mask and
twin blade; the "what he was" made flesh, then unmasked into an ally.

DEMEANOR: Robotic certainty cracking into mercy; "Stand down or I complete the order." > then the
hitch. Welcomed plainly aboard as new, strange, family.
